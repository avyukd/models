## 5. The Setup

### What This Business Is

Aduro is a pre-commercial technology company attempting to commercialize Hydrochemolytic Technology (HCT) for chemical recycling of plastics. The technology uses water and inexpensive catalysts to break down mixed, contaminated plastics at lower temperatures/pressures than pyrolysis, producing liquid hydrocarbons suitable for steam crackers.

**Claimed Advantages:**
- No molecular hydrogen required (major cost/infrastructure savings)
- Handles contaminated feedstock (cheaper inputs)
- Output works in crackers "as is" without hydrotreatment (huge if true)
- 90% liquid yields vs. 78% for pyrolysis

**Unproven Claims:**
- All of the above remain unverified at commercial scale
- Pilot results historically fail to replicate at scale in this industry

### Industry Context

The advanced plastics recycling industry is in a shakeout phase:

| Company | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Brightmark | Bankrupt subsidiaries; 5% capacity utilization |
| BlueCycle | Bankruptcy Q1 2025 |
| PureCycle | Struggled to 67% uptime; $224M loss first 9 months 2024 |
| Ioniqa | Shut down 2024 |
| New Hope Energy | Shut down 2024 |

The fundamental problem: virgin plastic remains cheaper than recycled. Chemical recycling requires ~$60/bbl oil minimum to compete. Current petrochemical oversupply makes virgin cheap.

### Why Now?

The market has repriced ADUR +300% from 52-week lows on Shell GameChanger graduation and clean $20M capital raise. The question is whether this represents appropriate de-risking or speculative enthusiasm ahead of commercial proof.

## 11. Final Assessment

**This is a venture bet, not an investment.**

Aduro has interesting technology claims, aligned management (~40% insider ownership), and clean recent financing. The Shell GameChanger graduation provides meaningful third-party validation of the chemistry. The licensing model is capital-efficient if it works.

However:

- Zero commercial revenue and zero binding agreements
- Industry in brutal shakeout with high failure rates
- Burn rate exceeds guidance; runway shorter than marketed
- Technology unproven at commercial scale
- 12-18 months until critical demo plant data

The risk-reward at $300M EV does not compensate for the uncertainty. The bull case requires technology execution that has failed repeatedly across the industry. The downside is severe (80-100% loss).

**Rational capital allocation says: wait.**

If the demo plant works and commercial traction emerges, the stock may be 2-3x higher, but the investment will have dramatically lower risk. Paying for de-risked information is almost always better than paying for uncertainty.

If the demo plant fails or is delayed, the stock trades to $2-4, and the decision to wait was correct.

**There is no urgency to own this name.** The catalyst calendar is known. The milestones are 12+ months away. Capital has better risk-adjusted uses.
