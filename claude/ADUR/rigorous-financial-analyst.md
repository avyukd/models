## Executive Summary

Aduro Clean Technologies is a pre-revenue, development-stage clean technology company with proprietary Hydrochemolytic Technology (HCT) for chemical recycling of plastics. The company has no meaningful commercial revenue, substantial operating losses (~$12M in FY25, accelerating to ~$6.3M quarterly in Q1 FY26), and is entirely dependent on external financing. The recent $20M raise at $11.50/share with half-warrants at $16 strike provides approximately 24 months of runway at current burn rates, though burn is increasing. With ~40% insider ownership and relatively clean financing terms, the capital structure is more aligned than typical pre-revenue clean-tech, but this remains a binary technology bet with no near-term path to sustainable revenue.

**Financial Assessment:** Speculative pre-revenue company. No normalized earnings possible. Analysis focuses on cash runway, dilution trajectory, and capital needs to reach potential revenue inflection.

### Runway Calculation

**Conservative Case (Current Burn):**
- Estimated cash: ~$27M
- Quarterly burn: $6.3M
- Runway: ~4.3 quarters = **~13 months** (through early 2027)

**Base Case (Burn stabilizes at $5M/quarter):**
- Estimated cash: ~$27M
- Quarterly burn: $5.0M
- Runway: ~5.4 quarters = **~16 months**

**Management-Implied Runway:**
- Management and supplemental context suggest ~24 months runway
- This implies assumed burn of ~$3.4M/quarter, which seems optimistic given Q1 FY26 actuals

**Key Concern:** The "24-month runway" guidance appears to assume burn rates well below recent actuals. If Q1 FY26's $6.3M quarterly burn persists or accelerates (which is likely given demo plant construction), actual runway may be 12-16 months without additional financing.

## 12. What Consensus May Be Missing

### Non-Consensus Observation: Burn Rate Divergence from Guidance

The "24-month runway" narrative appears inconsistent with recent financial data. Q1 FY26 showed $6.3M quarterly operating losses - implying ~$25M annual burn. With ~$27M cash post-December raise, actual runway may be closer to 12-16 months.

**Why This Matters:**
- Another financing could be required by late 2026
- If stock is lower at that point, dilution could be severe
- The 24-month guidance appears to assume either: (a) significant cost reduction, (b) some non-dilutive funding, or (c) optimistic burn assumptions
- Investors assuming 24-month runway may be surprised by earlier capital needs

### Secondary Observation: SBC Magnitude

Stock-based compensation appears to be running at $15-20M+ annualized based on the gap between operating loss and adjusted EBITDA. For a pre-revenue company, this level of non-cash dilution is aggressive. The 743,500 option grant in July 2025 alone could represent $5-10M in fair value depending on assumptions.

## 13. Conclusion

### Financial Assessment

Aduro Clean Technologies is a speculative, pre-revenue technology bet with:

**Strengths:**
- Clean balance sheet (minimal debt)
- High insider ownership (~40%) providing alignment
- Recent $20M raise with relatively clean terms
- Shell GameChanger graduation provides some technology validation
- Licensing model, if successful, would be capital-efficient

**Weaknesses:**
- Zero commercial revenue and no binding licensing agreements
- Accelerating cash burn ($6.3M/quarter in Q1 FY26)
- Significant ongoing dilution (44% share increase in 18 months)
- Aggressive SBC for pre-revenue company
- 2+ years minimum to potential commercial revenue
- Runway may be shorter than marketed (~13-16 months vs "24 months")

### Investment Conclusion

**Pre-revenue binary technology bet.** The stock is pricing significant probability of commercial success. Financial analysis supports neither a "buy" nor "sell" conclusion - this is a technology and business execution call, not a financial one.

From a pure numbers perspective: the company burns $6M+/quarter, has zero commercial customers, and trades at $300M+ EV for unproven technology. This is consistent with venture-stage pricing, not traditional equity valuation.

For investors who believe in the technology and licensing model, the insider alignment and clean financing terms are relative positives. For skeptics, the burn trajectory and lack of commercial agreements warrant caution.

**The market is paying ~$300M for an option on technology that has yet to operate continuously at scale or secure a single binding commercial agreement.**
