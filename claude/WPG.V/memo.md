## The Setup

West Point Gold (formerly Gold79 Mines) is a junior gold explorer focused on the Gold Chain project in Arizona's Oatman District, which historically produced ~2Moz Au. The company has systematically consolidated the most prospective ground in the district, including patented claims that provide private-land permitting advantages.

The structural backdrop is as favorable as it gets for a junior gold explorer:
- Gold at US$2,900/oz, supported by central bank buying (1,000+ tonnes/year) and fiscal/geopolitical drivers
- Major gold discoveries collapsing (<10 significant discoveries per decade since 2010)
- Reserve replacement ratio below 1x for majors -- M&A is the primary growth strategy
- Junior mining cycle in acceleration phase -- institutional generalist money has not yet arrived
- A genuine 1.5-2.5Moz discovery in a US jurisdiction is worth more today than at any point in 15 years

The company also holds Baxter Spring (Nevada, acquired Oct 2025, 128 historical drill holes) and Jefferson Canyon (Kinross JV, Nevada). The Kinross partnership validates geological credibility -- a major does not JV with a promoter.
