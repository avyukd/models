## The Decision

Today's drill results — particularly the 13.48 g/t Au over 21.34m bonanza intercept — materially de-risk the maiden resource estimate expected later in 2026. We take the bet because the geology is exceptional and the cycle is supportive.

**Tactical (1-5 days): BUY at or near C$1.60.** These are among the best drill results from any junior explorer in 2025-2026. Unlike rock-chip results (see GPAC.V), these are diamond drill intercepts with real geological significance — the move has staying power.

## Key Drivers (What Actually Moves This Stock)

1. **Maiden resource estimate (H2 2026)** — This is the binary event. >1.5Moz at >2 g/t Au = BUY confirmed, stock re-rates to C$250-350M. <1Moz or grade drops below 1.5 g/t = stock re-rates to C$60-80M. Everything else is noise relative to this.

2. **Results from 30 pending holes** — Arriving over the next 3-6 months. Each batch of assays is a catalyst. Step-out targets (Sheep Trail, Black Dyke, Frisco Graben) could transform the story from single-zone to district-scale. This is where the surprise upside lives.

3. **Gold price** — At $2,900/oz, the cycle is supportive. A move to $3,000-3,500 would expand multiples for the entire sector and pull WPG higher. A correction to $2,200-2,400 would crush junior valuations regardless of drill results. WPG's grade provides some insulation (economic even at $1,500/oz), but the stock price would still suffer.
