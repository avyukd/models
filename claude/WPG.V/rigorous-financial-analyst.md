# WPG.V — Rigorous Financial Analysis

**Price:** C$1.60 | **Market Cap:** C$173M | **Date:** 2026-02-25

---

## Key Finding

WPG is well-funded for its current 15,000m drill program with C$7.4M cash (Dec 31, 2025) but will likely need another raise within 6-9 months to fund step-out drilling and Baxter Spring programs. At C$173M market cap for a pre-resource explorer, the stock prices in a meaningful maiden resource (~1.5-2.5Moz) at a reasonable EV/oz — not cheap, but not egregious in a $2,900+ gold environment.

## Exploration Target (Not a Resource)

The company disclosed an exploration target (September 2025) at Tyro Main Zone of **19.5-31.2M tonnes @ 2.0-3.0 g/t Au**. This is conceptual — NI 43-101 explicitly states it is uncertain if further exploration will delineate this as a mineral resource.

**Implied contained gold (conceptual):** 1.25-3.0Moz Au (midpoint ~2.1Moz)

**Metallurgy:** Preliminary bottle-roll tests show 86% gold recovery — reasonable for this deposit type.

3. Gold remains above US$2,500/oz

## Gold Price Sensitivity

At current ~US$2,900/oz gold, the exploration target implies ~US$6-9B in-ground value (undiscounted). Even at a typical 1-3% in-ground valuation for exploration-stage assets, this supports C$60-270M market cap. WPG's current C$173M sits in the middle of this range — **priced for a successful maiden resource but not for perfection.**

**Key risk:** A gold correction to US$2,000/oz would compress junior valuations by 40-60% regardless of drill results.

1. **Funded for 2-3 quarters** but a raise is coming — watch for dilutive financing

4. **The stock prices in success** at Tyro Main Zone — upside requires either better-than-expected resource or new discoveries at step-out targets
