# IWG plc (IWG.L) Financial Analysis

**Price**: 241.20 GBp (as provided)
**Market Cap**: ~GBP 2.4B (~$3.0B)
**Enterprise Value**: ~$8.5B (including ~$6.2B lease liabilities under IFRS 16)

---

## Key Finding: The "EBITDA" You See Is Not Real Earnings

**The critical insight for IWG is that IFRS 16 accounting creates a massive gap between reported EBITDA and actual cash earnings.** Management reports pre-IFRS 16 EBITDA of $557m (FY24), but the company generated only ~$20m of GAAP net income and minimal true free cash flow after lease obligations. The stock trades on optical EBITDA growth, but the underlying economic reality is far more constrained.

---

## 1. Business Financial Profile

**Business Model**: IWG operates flexible workspace under brands including Regus and Spaces. The company is transitioning from an owned/leased model (capital-intensive, lease liability exposure) to a franchised/managed model (capital-light, fee income).

| Characteristic | Assessment |
|----------------|------------|
| Capital Intensity | HIGH - $6.2B lease liabilities on balance sheet |
| Cost Structure | HIGH fixed costs (long-term leases) vs variable revenue |
| Operating Leverage | Significant - small revenue changes drive large profit swings |
| Cyclicality | HIGH - correlated with office demand/employment cycles |
| Revenue Quality | Mixed - recurring office subscriptions but high churn potential |

The company operates each leased property through an SPV structure, which provides some liability isolation but does not eliminate the fundamental lease obligation.

---

## 2. Income Statement Analysis

### Revenue Trend (FY21-FY24)

| Year | Revenue ($m) | Growth |
|------|--------------|--------|
| 2021 | 3,006 | Recovery |
| 2022 | 3,329 | +11% |
| 2023 | 3,689 | +11% |
| 2024 | 3,690 | Flat |

**System-wide revenue** (including franchisee revenue) was $4.2B in 2024, but **consolidated revenue** was essentially flat at $3.69B. This distinction matters: IWG reports headline figures on system-wide basis, but only consolidated revenue flows through the P&L.

### Profitability (FY24 vs FY23)

| Metric | FY24 | FY23 |
|--------|------|------|
| Pre-IFRS 16 EBITDA | $557m | $503m |
| IFRS 16 EBITDA | ~$1,830m* | ~$1,750m* |
| Operating Profit (IFRS) | $510m | $179m |
| Net Income | ~$20m | $(270)m loss |
| EPS | 2.0c | (26.7)c |

*IFRS 16 EBITDA is much higher because lease expense moves below the line to depreciation/interest.

**Critical Observation**: The $557m "EBITDA" that management highlights excludes ~$1.3B of annual lease-related charges. Under IFRS 16, these show up as depreciation and interest expense rather than rent expense, but they represent real cash obligations.

### Gap Between GAAP and Economic Earnings

The reconciliation from IFRS 16 EBITDA (~$1,830m) to pre-IFRS 16 EBITDA ($557m) requires adding back:
- Rental income from subleases (recognized as lease receivables under IFRS 16)
- Deducting rental costs on the lease portfolio (~$1.3B annual cash rent)
- Center closure costs reflected as impairments

This ~$1.3B gap represents **real cash rent obligations** that are cosmetically removed from EBITDA under IFRS 16 accounting.

## 3. Cash Flow and Normalized Earnings

### Cash Flow Reality

| Metric | FY24 | FY23 |
|--------|------|------|
| Pre-IFRS 16 EBITDA | $557m | $503m |
| Less: Maintenance Capex | $(93)m | $(113)m |
| Less: Cash Interest (~est) | $(55)m | $(60)m |
| Less: Cash Taxes (~est) | $(30)m | $(25)m |
| **Pre-IFRS 16 "Owner Earnings"** | **~$380m** | **~$305m** |

However, this pre-IFRS 16 framework still requires significant nuance:

**Lease Liability Reduction**: Under IFRS 16, the "principal" portion of lease payments is not in EBITDA but is a real cash outflow that reduces the liability. IWG must service ~$1B+ annually in total lease payments, only partially offset by sublease income.

**Reported Free Cash Flow**: The company targets FCF of "at least $140m" for FY25, which represents actual cash available after all obligations. This is the true measure of cash generation.

### Normalized Earnings Reconciliation

Starting from GAAP Net Income:

| Item | Amount ($m) |
|------|-------------|
| GAAP Net Income (FY24) | ~$20m |
| Add: Impairment charges (non-cash) | +$93m |
| Less: Impairment reversals (non-cash) | $(24)m |
| Add: Other one-time items | +$17m |
| **Adjusted Net Income** | **~$106m** |

**Normalized EPS**: ~10.6 cents on ~1B shares outstanding

At 241.20 GBp (~305 cents), the stock trades at ~29x normalized earnings. This is expensive for a business with:
- Flat consolidated revenue growth
- Massive off-balance sheet style lease obligations
- Cyclical exposure to office demand

## 4. Balance Sheet and Financial Risk

### Capitalization Structure (Dec 2024)

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Total Assets | $9.6B |
| Total Liabilities | $9.5B |
| **Shareholders' Equity** | **$93m** |
| Lease Liabilities (IFRS 16) | $6.2B |
| Net Financial Debt | $712m |
| **Total Adjusted Debt** | **~$6.9B** |

**The balance sheet is highly leveraged.** While management focuses on "net financial debt" of $712m, this excludes $6.2B of lease liabilities. Total equity is only $93m against $9.6B of assets.

### Debt Structure and Maturities

| Instrument | Amount | Maturity |
|------------|--------|----------|
| Euro Bond (BBB rated) | EUR 625m (~$680m) | June 2030 |
| Revolving Credit Facility | $720m (undrawn capacity) | June 2029 |
| Convertible Bonds | GBP 158m (~$200m) | Dec 2027 (put Dec 2025) |

**Positive**: No refinancing required until 2029 (excluding the convertible put option in Dec 2025).

**Leverage Ratios**:
- Net Debt / Pre-IFRS 16 EBITDA: 1.3x (management target: 1.0x)
- Including Lease Liabilities: ~12x Pre-IFRS 16 EBITDA

### Net Current Liability Position

The company reports a net current liability position of $2.2B. A significant portion relates to short-term lease liabilities matched against ROU assets. While structured through SPVs, this indicates ongoing liquidity requirements.

## 5. Capital Allocation Assessment

### Capex Discipline Improving

| Metric | FY24 | FY23 |
|--------|------|------|
| Total Net Investment | $186m | $221m |
| Maintenance Capex | $93m | $113m |
| Growth Capex | $88m | $95m |
| Landlord Contributions | $56m | $57m |

**Net growth capex fell to $51m** as 95% of new deals are now capital-light (franchise/managed model).

### Capital Light Transition

The strategic pivot to franchising is the bull case for IWG:
- Fee income grew 30% to $79m in FY24
- 899 new center signings in 2024, mostly capital-light
- Target: $1B pre-IFRS 16 EBITDA at scale

If successful, IWG transforms from a capital-intensive lease arbitrage business to a capital-light franchise royalty stream. The question is whether $79m of fee income can scale to become material against $3.7B of consolidated revenue.

### Shareholder Returns

- Dividend reinstated in 2024
- $130m buyback target for FY25
- $59m returned in H1 2025

This is aggressive given the balance sheet leverage and modest FCF generation (~$140m target for FY25).

## 6. Red Flags Checklist

| Risk Factor | Assessment |
|-------------|------------|
| Persistent negative FCF | MIXED - FCF positive but thin (~$140m target on $3.7B revenue = 3.8% margin) |
| Rising SBC as % of revenue | LOW CONCERN - Not a material factor for IWG |
| Margin expansion from accounting | RED FLAG - IFRS 16 inflates EBITDA by ~$1.3B annually |
| Weak cash conversion | RED FLAG - $557m "EBITDA" generates only ~$140m FCF |
| Balance sheet fragility | RED FLAG - $93m equity, $6.9B total debt + lease obligations |
| Aggressive revenue recognition | LOW CONCERN - Straightforward office rental model |
| Related party transactions | NOT IDENTIFIED |

### Additional Concerns

1. **Lease Liability Concentration**: $6.2B of lease obligations with weighted average life of ~5 years means ~$1.2B+ annual cash commitments.

2. **Cyclical Vulnerability**: Revenue flatlined in FY24 despite economic recovery. A recession could drive negative operating leverage given the fixed cost base.

3. **Franchise Transition Risk**: The capital-light model depends on landlords/franchisees bearing capex and lease risk. In a downturn, partner defaults could impair fee income.

4. **H1 2025 Guidance Miss**: Shares fell 17% in August 2025 when management guided to the low end of $525-565m EBITDA range, citing accelerated franchise investment.

## Key Financial Drivers

**The 1-3 metrics that actually matter for IWG:**

1. **Fee Income Growth**: The transition to capital-light franchising is the entire bull case. $79m in FY24 must grow substantially to change the earnings quality of the business. Watch this metric quarterly.

2. **Pre-IFRS 16 EBITDA Margin**: Currently 15%, targeting expansion toward $1B EBITDA on ~$5B system revenue. Margin compression would signal pricing pressure or cost inflation.

3. **True Free Cash Flow**: The ~$140m FCF target for FY25 is the real measure of cash generation. If this doesn't grow despite "record EBITDA," the accounting is flattering reality.

---

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**Non-consensus insight**: The market appears to value IWG on pre-IFRS 16 EBITDA multiples (~8x forward), which implies the business is reasonably priced. However, **true FCF yield is only ~4-5%** ($140-160m FCF on $3B market cap), which is thin compensation for the balance sheet risk.

The $6.2B lease liability is not "off balance sheet" under IFRS 16, but investors may still be mentally separating it from "real debt." In a stress scenario, lease obligations are senior to equity and cannot be easily restructured without landlord consent (or SPV defaults that damage the franchise).

**The franchise transition is real but early**: Fee income of $79m represents only 2% of consolidated revenue. Management's $1B EBITDA target requires substantial execution over 3-5 years. The current valuation may be pricing in success that is far from assured.

---

## Conclusion

IWG is a complex accounting story where headline metrics (record EBITDA, record revenue) obscure a more challenging reality:

- **Flat consolidated revenue growth** despite post-pandemic tailwinds
- **Minimal equity cushion** ($93m) against massive lease obligations ($6.2B)
- **Thin true FCF** (~$140m) relative to market cap (~$3B)
- **Aggressive shareholder returns** ($130m buyback) on a leveraged balance sheet

The capital-light franchise pivot is strategically sound but early stage. At 241 GBp, the stock prices in significant execution success. A more cautious investor would wait for proof that fee income can scale before paying 29x normalized earnings for a cyclical, leveraged office rental business.

**Assessment**: Fully priced on optimistic assumptions. The risk/reward favors patience.
