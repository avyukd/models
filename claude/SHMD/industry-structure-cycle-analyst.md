# PCB & Semiconductor Capital Equipment Industry Analysis

## The One Industry Factor That Matters

**AI datacenter capex is the singular driver.** The hyperscalers (Google, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft) nearly doubled quarterly capex from $30B to $55B between mid-2023 and Q3 2024. This flows directly to high-layer-count PCBs, IC substrates, and advanced packaging equipment. Everything else (smartphones, PCs, traditional servers) remains sluggish.

For a PCB equipment supplier like SCHMID, the question is not "is there growth?" but "can they capture the AI-driven high-end segment?" Wet processing for mSAP/SAP (sub-20um line/space) is the technology gatekeep. Companies without this capability are locked out of the fastest-growing segments.

---

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**Consensus view:** PCB equipment is a derivative play on semiconductor cycles; mid-cycle with steady growth ahead.

**Non-consensus view:** The PCB equipment market is bifurcating far more aggressively than investors appreciate.

1. **The commodity PCB equipment market is structurally declining.** China now produces 76% of global PCB output by volume (up from 53% in 2020). Domestic Chinese equipment suppliers are capturing share rapidly. Naura revenue +30% H1 2025, AMEC +44%. The new 50% domestic equipment mandate accelerates this.

2. **The high-end segment (mSAP, IC substrates, advanced packaging) is structurally undersupplied.** ABF substrates have a 20% supply-demand gap through 2026 per Ajinomoto. Lead times stretched to 35+ weeks with 25% spot premiums.

3. **This is not a unified cycle.** Memory, smartphones, PCs remain weak. AI/HPC is overheating. Playing "the cycle" will get you killed if you're exposed to the wrong segment.

**Implication:** A German specialty equipment supplier with mSAP/SAP capability should trade at a structural premium, not cycle-average multiples. BUT: they must prove they're capturing AI server board orders, not legacy commodity work. The October 2025 AI server PCB orders announcement suggests they're positioned correctly.

## Industry Structure Analysis

### Competitive Dynamics

PCB wet processing equipment is **fragmented at the commodity level, consolidated at the high-end:**

- **Commodity wet processing:** Intense price competition, Chinese domestic players gaining share (NAURA, AMEC ecosystem suppliers)
- **High-end (mSAP/SAP for HDI, IC substrates):** Concentrated among specialists: SCHMID, Tokyo Electron, SCREEN, Lam Research (via Semsysco), ACM Research
- **Structural barrier:** mSAP/SAP requires sub-2um copper uniformity, 35um L/S capability minimum, Class 1000 cleanroom integration. Not all wet processing equipment can do this.

### Supply Elasticity

**Low elasticity in high-end, moderate in commodity.**

- High-end equipment requires 18-24 month lead times for capacity expansion
- Technology development cycles are 3-5 years for new generation platforms
- Commodity equipment faces Chinese domestic capacity overhang

### Pricing Dynamics

- **High-end:** Price discipline maintained; technology-driven specifications allow differentiation
- **Commodity:** Price erosion from Chinese competition; 3-5% annual ASP declines typical

## Structural Trends That Matter

### 1. SAP/mSAP Technology Transition (Bullish for SCHMID)

The shift from subtractive etching to semi-additive processes is the defining technology trend:
- Subtractive processes hit limits at 30um line/space
- mSAP extends to 20um, SAP to sub-10um
- Required for: smartphones (SLP), AI servers, advanced HDI, IC substrates
- **SCHMID's InfinityLine H+/V+ explicitly targets this segment**

Market sizing: Ultra-HDI PCB market projected $1.68B (2025) to $3.07B (2030), 10.2% CAGR.

### 2. AI Datacenter Demand (Bullish)

- Hyperscaler capex: $100B+ annually, ~doubled from 2023
- AI server PCBs: 20+ layer count, 6+ HDI orders, high-speed CCL
- IC substrate demand: ABF 20% undersupplied through 2026
- Advanced packaging equipment: +48% growth in test equipment (2025), +20% A&P equipment

**Direct driver for SCHMID:** October 2025 AI server PCB orders announcement signals they're winning in this segment.

### 3. Reshoring / China+1 (Mixed)

- CHIPS Act: $70B+ for US domestic; $400B in private investment announced
- European Chips Act: 43B in investment through 2030
- PCB reshoring: $4.6B invested in Southeast Asia (73% Thailand, 17% Vietnam)
- **Net impact for SCHMID:** Positive. Greenfield capacity requires new equipment. Western suppliers preferred for non-China projects.

### 4. China Domestic Substitution (Bearish Risk)

- 50% domestic equipment mandate now in effect for new capacity
- Chinese equipment revenue growing 30-44% annually
- Risk: SCHMID has China exposure; could face share loss in commodity segments
- Mitigation: High-end mSAP/SAP not yet replicated domestically

## Historical Cycle Reference Points

### Semiconductor Equipment Cycles
- Average downturn: -21% (equipment) vs -8% (semiconductors)
- Cycle length: ~3.7 years average since 2000
- 2018-2019: -26% SOX peak-to-trough (6 quarters)
- 2022-2023: -45% SOX peak-to-trough; memory CapEx -50% (SK Hynix)

### PCB Industry Cycles
- 2023: Global PCB output -15.6% (severe inventory correction)
- 2024: +5.8% recovery (inventory replenishment, early AI demand)
- 2025E: +6.1% (AI driving high-end; consumer sluggish)

### Mid-Cycle Definition for PCB Equipment

**Mid-cycle conditions:**
- PCB industry capacity utilization: 65-75%
- ABF substrate utilization: 70-80%
- Equipment book-to-bill: 1.0-1.1x
- Lead times: 12-16 weeks (standard), 20-24 weeks (high-end)

**Current conditions:**
- ABF utilization: 60-70% (H2 2024), rising toward 80%+ in high-end
- ABF lead times: 35+ weeks (above mid-cycle)
- High-end constrained; commodity at/below mid-cycle

## Industry-Level Risks

### Primary Risk: AI CapEx Pullback

If hyperscaler spending moderates (regulatory, ROI concerns, recession), the entire high-end PCB equipment thesis collapses. This is the single largest risk.

### Secondary Risks

1. **China retaliation/export controls:** Could disrupt supply chains; force technology decoupling
2. **ABF capacity overshoot:** If new capacity comes online faster than demand (2027+ risk)
3. **Technology disruption:** Glass-core substrates could displace ABF in late 2020s
4. **Commodity margin compression:** Chinese domestic substitution continues
