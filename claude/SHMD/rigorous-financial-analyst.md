# SCHMID Group N.V. (NASDAQ: SHMD) - Financial Analysis

**Date:** February 2026
**Stock Price:** ~$9.20 | **Market Cap:** ~$440M | **Shares Outstanding:** ~55.6M (post-XJ dilution)

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: SPECULATIVE TURNAROUND WITH SEVERE FINANCIAL DISTRESS

SHMD is a German capital equipment maker for PCB/substrate manufacturing trading at a ~$440M market cap despite:
- **Deeply distressed financials**: H1 2025 revenue of EUR 16.9M (-43% YoY), negative gross profit (-EUR 1.7M), adjusted EBITDA of -EUR 5.3M
- **Balance sheet crisis**: Net debt of ~$64M, going concern issues, Nasdaq delisting threat for late 20-F filing
- **Massive dilution cycle**: 55.6M shares outstanding after XJ Harbour debt-equity swap (was 43M); $30M convertible notes just issued (Jan 2026) with warrants; EUR 10M insider loan at 15% interest with conversion rights at $2.15
- **Implied 2026 valuation**: At EUR 100M+ revenue guidance and 12%+ EBITDA margin = ~EUR 12-15M EBITDA; current EV/EBITDA implies 25-30x on optimistic 2026 numbers

**Variant Perception**: The market is pricing this as an AI/advanced packaging play with order momentum. The financial reality is a distressed, loss-making, heavily-diluted turnaround with execution and financing risk.

### 1. Order-to-Revenue Conversion (THE critical variable)
- Backlog of EUR 53M+ in machinery orders as of Nov 2025
- H1 2025 revenue was only EUR 16.9M despite orders - conversion timing is everything
- Delayed advance payments pushed revenue to 2026 - can they actually ship and collect?
- **What to watch**: Quarterly revenue vs backlog progression; payment/collection patterns

- Gross margin went NEGATIVE in H1 2025 (-10% implied) vs 24% historical
- ~800 employees, fixed manufacturing footprint in Germany (high cost)
- Breakeven likely requires EUR 70-80M+ revenue annually
- **What to watch**: Gross margin trajectory as revenue scales back toward EUR 70-77M

### 3. Dilution and Capital Structure
- Already 12.5M new shares to XJ Harbour at $2.15 (eliminating $27M debt)
- $30M convertible notes (Jan 2026) with conversion mechanics + warrants
- EUR 10M insider loan at 15% interest, convertible at $2.15
- Fully diluted share count could exceed 70-80M shares depending on conversions
- **What to watch**: Conversion triggers, warrant exercise, any additional financing

## WHAT CONSENSUS MAY BE MISSING

### The Non-Consensus Insight: Recurring IFRS 2 Accounting Distortion Masks Ongoing Losses

Most coverage focuses on the EUR 70.6M IFRS 2 charge from de-SPAC as "one-time." However, the underlying business excluding this charge was still deeply unprofitable:

**H1 2024 Reconciliation:**
- Reported Operating Loss: ~EUR 70M
- Less IFRS 2 (de-SPAC): EUR 70.6M
- Implied "Adjusted" Operating Income: ~EUR 0.6M
- On EUR 29.7M revenue = ~2% operating margin at best

**H1 2025 Reality:**
- No IFRS 2 distortion
- Operating Loss: EUR -7.8M
- On EUR 16.9M revenue = -46% operating margin
- Adjusted EBITDA: EUR -5.3M

**The miss**: Even "normalized" H1 2024 showed minimal profitability. The EUR 70.6M charge masked that the business was barely breaking even at EUR 60M annualized revenue. Now at EUR 34M annualized run-rate (H1 2025 x2), the operating leverage works violently in reverse.

### Second Non-Consensus Point: Customer Advance Payment Risk

The company explicitly states revenue delays were due to "delays in receiving contractual advance payments." In capital equipment, advance payments are often 20-40% of order value. If customers are delaying advances, this signals:
1. Customer financial stress or changing priorities
2. Potential order cancellation risk
3. Working capital strain on SCHMID

The EUR 53M backlog may not convert at historical rates.

## CONCLUSION

**Financial Assessment**: Distressed turnaround with severe balance sheet fragility

**Bull Case**: AI/advanced packaging tailwinds drive EUR 100M+ revenue in 2026, operating leverage kicks in, 15% EBITDA margins achieved = EUR 15M EBITDA, stock re-rates to 20x EBITDA = EUR 300M EV = ~$6/share (BELOW current price)

**Bear Case**: Order conversion disappoints, additional dilution at $2-3 prices, Nasdaq delisting, 70-80M fully diluted shares, normalized earnings of EUR 3-5M = $0.04-0.06 EPS, stock trades at 20x = $0.80-1.20

**Base Case**: Company survives, achieves EUR 80-90M revenue by 2026, 12% EBITDA margins, EUR 10-12M EBITDA, 65M fully diluted shares, fair value $4-6/share = 30-50% downside from current $9.20

**Verdict**: Current valuation requires flawless execution on an aggressive turnaround with no margin for error. The stock has run from $1.86 (Jan 2025 low) to $9.20 on order momentum. The financial reality is:
- Negative gross profit in H1 2025
- $64M net debt requiring rescue financing
- Nasdaq delisting risk
- Massive dilution pipeline

This is a speculative bet on AI/PCB capex recovery, not a fundamentally sound investment at current prices.
