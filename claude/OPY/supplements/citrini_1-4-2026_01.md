# Long Oppenheimer — Investment Summary (OPY US)

## Company Overview
- **Oppenheimer Holdings (OPY US)** is a middle-market investment bank and wealth management firm.
- Core strengths:
  - **Investment Banking** (notably biotech, technology, municipal finance)
  - **Wealth Management** (~$55B AUM)
  - **Retail Brokerage** (~$88B client equity)
- A rare small institution combining meaningful IB, WM scale, and brokerage footprint.

---

## Recent Earnings Volatility & Inflection
- Capital Markets division historically volatile:
  - **2021 (SPAC boom):** $30–$90M quarterly profits
  - **2024:** $10–$20M quarterly losses
- **Turning point in 2025:**
  - Q3 Capital Markets income: **$12M**
  - First profitable quarter since **Q3 2022**
  - Benefiting from:
    - Rising sales & trading volumes
    - Elevated small-cap equity issuance
    - Resurgent biotech sector

---

## Valuation
- Trades at **deep discount**:
  - **9.1× trailing earnings**
  - **~8× 2026 earnings**
  - **$2.50 discount to TBV** (~$70.48)
- Valuation multiple more typical of:
  - Credit-risk heavy banks
  - Catastrophe-exposed insurers  
- **Mismatch:** OPY is fee-based, debt-free, minimal credit risk.

---

## Balance Sheet & Risk Profile
- **Debt-free**
- Minimal credit exposure:
  - Primarily margin loans to high-net-worth clients
  - Loans collateralized by highly appreciated equities
- No meaningful systemic credit risk.

---

## Compensation Distortion (OARs)
- Employee comp heavily tied to stock performance via **Oppenheimer Appreciation Rights (OARs)**:
  - Cash-settled
  - 5-year maturity
- **2.6M OARs outstanding**
- 2024–2025 earnings distorted by stock rally:
  - 70% stock price increase materially inflated compensation expense
  - Q3: 16% rally ($65 → $73) caused **$13.5M (~$0.95/share after tax)** earnings hit
- Implication:
  - Underlying operating earnings are **stronger than reported**
  - Recent pullback ($73 → $68) is a **tailwind for Q4 earnings**

---

## Capital Allocation & Returns
- History of:
  - Aggressive **share repurchases below book value**
  - Long-term **outperformance vs peers**
- Insider ownership: **~32%** (float constrained)

---

## Relative Performance (2016–2025 Total Return Index)
- OPY outperformed:
  - Raymond James (RJF)
  - Stifel (SF)

  - S&P 500 (SPY)

---

## Liquidity & Coverage Constraints
- Average daily volume: **~$2.5M**
- Limited sell-side research coverage
- Small float + high insider ownership
- Too illiquid for most institutional investors → valuation inefficiency.

---

## Strategic Optionality
- Attractive acquisition target:
  - Particularly for **foreign institutions** seeking a turnkey U.S. platform
- Even absent M&A:
  - Compelling way to gain exposure to:
    - Capital markets recovery
    - Wealth management fee growth
  - At **rock-bottom valuation**
- Management has **~$250M of personal capital at risk** (strong alignment).
