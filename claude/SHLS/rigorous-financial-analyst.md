# Shoals Technologies (SHLS) -- Financial Analysis at $10.00/share (~$1.66B market cap)

**Date: 2026-02-24 | Basis: Q4 2025 earnings release, FY2024 10-K, quarterly filings**

## Business Financial Profile

Shoals is an EBOS (electrical balance of systems) provider for utility-scale solar, expanding into BESS and data centers. Capital-light manufacturing model with moderate fixed costs. Revenue is project-based and lumpy -- not recurring. High operating leverage: margins swing significantly with volume. Cyclicality is meaningful, tied to solar project permitting, interest rates, and policy.

## Income Statement: Revenue Recovery, Margin Deterioration

| Period | Revenue | Gross Margin | Adj. EBITDA |
|--------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| FY2023 | $489M | ~34.4% | $99.1M |
| FY2024 | $399M | ~35.6% | $99.1M |
| FY2025 | $475M | ~35.0% est. | $99.5M |
| FY2026 guide | $560-600M | -- | $110-130M |

Revenue recovered in FY2025 (+19% YoY) after an 18% decline in FY2024, driven by solar project delays and competitive dynamics. The headline growth masks a troubling pattern: adjusted EBITDA was essentially flat for three consecutive years ($99.1M, $99.1M, $99.5M) despite significant revenue volatility. This means Shoals generated the same absolute EBITDA dollars on $489M revenue as it did on $399M and $475M -- the margin structure is deteriorating.

Q4 2025 gross margin compressed to 31.6% from 37.6% prior year. Management attributes $3.3M to tariffs/duties and $1.5M to new facility costs. But the math does not fully explain it: Q4 revenue was $148.3M at 31.6% gross margin = ~$46.9M gross profit. At prior year's 37.6%, that would be $55.8M -- an $8.9M gap. The identified items ($4.8M) explain only about half. The remainder suggests structural cost pressures from material inflation and/or competitive pricing.

## Normalized Earnings Reconciliation

Working from reported FY2025 figures (per Q4 2025 earnings release):

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| GAAP Net Income (FY2025) | $33.6M |
| Add: Amortization of intangibles | ~$8M est. |
| Add: Legal/professional fees (litigation-related, abnormal) | ~$15M |
| Less: Tax effect of above (~25%) | (~$6M) |
| Adjusted Net Income (company-reported) | $60.6M |
| Less: Stock-based compensation (est. ~$25-30M) | ($27M) est. |
| Add: Tax shield on SBC (~25%) | $7M |
| **Normalized after-tax earnings (SBC-adjusted)** | **~$41M** |

Key note: The company's reported adjusted net income of $60.6M excludes SBC entirely, which flatters the picture. SBC was likely $25-30M in FY2025 based on ~3M RSU/PSU grants annually and prior year disclosures showing a $5.9M decrease in SBC. I estimate ~$27M for FY2025 (~5.7% of revenue). This is not trivial.

At ~$41M normalized earnings on 167M diluted shares = **~$0.25/share normalized EPS.** At $10.00/share, the stock trades at **~40x normalized earnings on current results.**

## Cash Flow: The Red Flag

FY2025 CFO guidance was $15-25M with capex of $30-40M. This means **negative free cash flow** in FY2025 despite $475M in revenue and $33.6M GAAP net income. The new facility buildout drove elevated capex ($30-40M vs. historical ~$8-10M). FY2026 guided CFO of $65-85M with capex of $20-30M implies $35-55M FCF -- an improvement but still modest relative to market cap (2-3% FCF yield).

Cash conversion has been poor: $33.6M GAAP net income vs. $15-25M CFO signals working capital absorption or other non-cash items beyond D&A not converting to cash.

## Balance Sheet & Financial Risk

As of Q3 2025: $8.6M cash, $126.8M revolver drawn on a $200M facility. Net debt ~$118M, or ~1.2x adj. EBITDA. Term loan was prepaid in full ($43.8M). Total assets $795M, total liabilities $81M (per search results -- this may exclude the revolver draw; need to verify). The balance sheet is not distressed but not clean either. With negative FCF in FY2025, the revolver balance likely increased into year-end.

## Litigation Overhang

Legal/professional fees increased $15.7M YoY in FY2025 across three fronts: (1) patent infringement suits against Voltage/Hikam at the ITC and federal courts, (2) wire insulation shrinkback remediation (a product quality issue from prior years), and (3) securities litigation. The patent case is strategically important -- Shoals' BLA product is its key differentiator and the ITC initially ruled in its favor before reversing, now on appeal. The $15.7M annual legal spend is ~3.3% of revenue and is unlikely to normalize to zero; ongoing IP enforcement will remain costly. I would normalize out roughly half (~$8M) as truly non-recurring.

## Red Flags Checklist

- [X] **Persistent negative free cash flow** -- FY2025 FCF negative; FY2026 guided to only $35-55M
- [ ] Rising SBC as % of revenue -- appears stable at ~5-6%, but need more data
- [X] **Margin expansion driven by accounting choices** -- Adjusted EBITDA excludes SBC and litigation costs; real margins are materially lower
- [X] **Weak cash conversion** -- GAAP earnings of $33.6M vs. CFO of $15-25M
- [ ] Balance sheet fragility -- manageable at 1.2x but bears watching
- [ ] Aggressive revenue recognition -- no evidence
- [ ] Related party transactions -- none flagged

## FY2026 Guidance Credibility

Revenue guidance of $560-600M (+18-26% YoY) is supported by $748M backlog. But EBITDA guidance of $110-130M implies 19-22% EBITDA margins, versus ~21% in FY2025. The midpoint ($120M) was below Street estimates of $134M -- this is why the stock dropped 10.8% on earnings. Q1 2026 guidance of $125-135M revenue with $16-21M EBITDA (12-16% margin) signals continued margin pressure in the near term.

## Valuation Framework

| Metric | FY2025 Actual | FY2026E (midpoint) |
|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| Revenue | $475M | $580M |
| Adj. EBITDA | $99.5M | $120M |
| Normalized earnings (SBC-adj.) | ~$41M | ~$55M est. |
| EV/EBITDA | ~18x | ~15x |
| P/Normalized E | ~40x | ~30x |

EV = $1.66B market cap + $118M net debt = ~$1.78B. At $10/share, the market is pricing in meaningful growth and margin recovery. For the valuation to work, you need FY2026 EBITDA at the high end ($130M+), continued 20%+ revenue growth beyond 2026, and gross margins stabilizing above 33%.

## Key Financial Drivers

1. **Gross margin trajectory** -- This is the single most important variable. The difference between 32% and 37% gross margin on $580M revenue is $29M of gross profit, which flows almost entirely to EBITDA. Tariff exposure and competitive pricing are the swing factors.
2. **Revenue growth sustainability** -- Backlog supports near-term growth, but solar project activity is policy-sensitive (IRA durability, tariff regime, interest rates).
3. **FCF conversion** -- Until capex normalizes post-facility buildout, the company is not generating meaningful free cash flow relative to its valuation.

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**The three-year EBITDA stagnation is the real story.** FY2023: $99.1M. FY2024: $99.1M. FY2025: $99.5M. Revenue went from $489M to $399M to $475M over this period, and EBITDA did not budge. This suggests the business has a higher cost floor than the Street models, likely from rising legal expenses (~$15M+ annually), facility costs, and competitive pricing pressure. If FY2026 EBITDA comes in at $120M on $580M revenue (20.7% margin), it would represent the fourth consecutive year of sub-$130M EBITDA -- a far cry from the $135M+ the Street was expecting. The market is pricing a growth and margin inflection that has not materialized in three years of data.
