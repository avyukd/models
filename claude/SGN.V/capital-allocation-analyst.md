# SGN.V — Capital Allocation & Management Analysis
**Date:** Feb 19, 2026

## Verdict: Typical Junior Explorer Capital Allocation — Dilution Is the Business Model

---

## Key Findings

**Share Structure:**
- Market cap ~$98M CAD at $0.36/share = ~272M shares outstanding
- Fully diluted share count likely significantly higher (warrants, options typical for TSX-V explorers)
- Junior explorers fund via equity — dilution is structural, not a red flag per se, but the rate matters

**Cash Position (Q4 2025):**
- Total assets: ~$28.4M (up 7% q/q)
- Total liabilities: ~$3.2M (down 85% q/q — likely debt repayment or reclassification)
- Cash/short-term assets: ~$11.8M vs short-term liabilities of ~$1.7M
- Debt-to-equity: 2.8% — very conservative
- **Cash runway: >1 year based on current burn rate**

**Revenue:**
- TTM ~$839K from residual heap leach recoveries at Mineral Ridge (suspended mining since Nov 2017)
- This is de minimis — the company is functionally a pure explorer

**Funding History:**
- As a TSX-V explorer, funded through equity raises, flow-through shares, and warrant exercises
- The 2025 gold bull market likely made recent raises less dilutive (higher share price = fewer shares issued per dollar raised)

## Capital Allocation Grade: C+ (Neutral for a Junior)

**Positives:**
- Clean balance sheet — minimal debt
- Adequate cash runway for current drill program
- Aggressive drilling pace (46 holes, 15,024m) suggests capital being deployed into the ground, not overhead

**Concerns:**
- $98M market cap on $839K revenue = shareholders paying for exploration optionality
- No visibility into insider ownership levels from available data
- Future dilution is inevitable — the question is at what price and terms
- Compensation details not readily available for assessment

## Non-Consensus Insight
The fact that they have >1 year cash runway with an active 46-hole drill program suggests recent financing was well-timed (likely raised into the gold rally at favorable terms). The risk is the NEXT raise — if gold corrects, dilution terms worsen dramatically.

## Key Risk
If gold pulls back 20-30%, the next equity raise happens at much lower prices, significantly diluting existing shareholders. Junior explorers have zero pricing power on their own equity issuance — they're gold price takers on both their asset AND their stock.
