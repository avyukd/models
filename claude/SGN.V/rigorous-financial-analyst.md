## Non-Consensus Insight
The $98M valuation already embeds expectations of resource growth. If the remaining 11 pending holes deliver results similar to 25MN-044 (3.14 g/t over 49.62m), the resource could meaningfully expand and the valuation would look cheap in hindsight. But if results are more like 25MN-045 (0.65 g/t), the current price is full.

## Key Risk
At C$132/oz on purely inferred ounces with no feasibility study, there's significant downside if: (1) resource update disappoints on grade, (2) gold corrects, or (3) next raise is dilutive. There's no earnings floor — this is pure optionality pricing.
