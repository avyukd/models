# OMER (Omeros Corporation) -- Financial Analysis

**Date:** 2026-02-26 | **Price:** ~$11.69 (as of 2/15/26) | **Market Cap:** ~$829M | **Shares Out:** 70.9M

## Business Financial Profile

Pre-commercial/early-commercial biotech transitioning from cash-burn mode. Two value drivers: (1) Yartemlea (narsoplimab), FDA-approved Dec 2025 for TA-TMA, U.S. launch Jan 2026; (2) $240M upfront cash from Novo Nordisk for zaltenibart (MASP-3 inhibitor), deal closed Q4 2025. Legacy OMIDRIA royalty stream declining. High fixed-cost structure, zero operating leverage currently, binary risk profile.

## Normalized Earnings -- Not Applicable

This company has no normalized earnings power to assess. It has never been profitable. The relevant framework is cash runway and probability-weighted revenue from Yartemlea.

**Cash burn rate (annualized, pre-launch):** ~$100-120M based on recent trends (reduced from ~$149M in FY2024 due to OpEx cuts).

**Runway estimate:** At ~$150-170M cash and ~$100-120M annual burn (now increasing with Yartemlea launch costs), the company has approximately 12-18 months of runway without additional financing, assuming no meaningful Yartemlea revenue.

## Yartemlea Revenue -- The Key Unknown

- **Pricing:** $36,000 per single-dose vial; total treatment cost estimated at $400K-$500K per patient
- **Target population:** TA-TMA in HSCT patients (small orphan indication)
- **Peak sales estimates:** Range wildly from "mid-to-high single-digit millions" to ~$300M depending on analyst
- **NTAP (New Technology Add-on Payment):** Expected October 2026; critical for hospital reimbursement
- **EMA decision:** Mid-2026

Without NTAP, hospital adoption will be slow because hospitals absorb the cost under DRG bundled payments. This is the single most important near-term catalyst.

## Valuation Framework

At ~$829M market cap with no earnings, traditional multiples are meaningless. The stock trades on:

**What the market is implying at $11.69:**
- $829M market cap minus ~$80M estimated net cash = ~$749M enterprise value
- This implies the market assigns ~$749M+ to Yartemlea's NPV plus pipeline optionality
- For $749M EV to be justified at a 10% discount rate, Yartemlea needs to generate ~$150-200M in peak sales with high margins (reasonable for orphan drug if adoption materializes)

**What must be true:** Yartemlea must achieve meaningful commercial uptake, which requires NTAP approval (Oct 2026) and successful launch execution. At $400-500K per treatment in a small population, even 500-750 patients annually would generate $200-375M revenue.

## Key Financial Drivers

1. **Yartemlea commercial uptake** -- Everything else is noise. NTAP decision in Oct 2026 is the gating factor for hospital economics. Pre-NTAP revenue will be minimal.
2. **Cash runway** -- With ~$150-170M post-Novo and ~$100-120M annual burn (likely increasing with launch costs), dilution or additional financing is probable within 12-18 months unless Yartemlea revenue ramps quickly.
3. **Convertible note dilution** -- $70.5M in 2029 convertibles at 9.50% represent future dilution risk on a $829M market cap.

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**The NTAP timing gap is underappreciated.** Yartemlea launched in January 2026, but NTAP reimbursement is not expected until October 2026. For 9 months, hospitals treating TA-TMA patients with Yartemlea absorb the ~$400-500K cost within existing DRG payments -- a massive economic disincentive. Analyst models projecting meaningful 2026 revenue may be front-loading adoption that won't materialize until NTAP kicks in. The real revenue story is H2 2026 at earliest, more likely 2027.

Additionally, the Novo Nordisk deal is widely celebrated but the $240M upfront is already spent (debt repayment + ongoing burn). The remaining $1.86B in milestones is contingent on Novo's development of zaltenibart -- Omeros has no control over this. The market may be assigning option value to milestones that are years away and probability-weighted far below face value.

**Bottom line:** This is a binary bet on Yartemlea commercial execution in a small orphan market, with a ticking clock on cash runway. The stock is not analyzable on traditional financial metrics. At $829M market cap, the market is pricing in meaningful Yartemlea success, but the NTAP gap and cash burn math suggest the risk-reward is tighter than consensus implies.
