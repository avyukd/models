# Parke Bancorp (PKBK) - Rigorous Financial Analysis

**Analysis Date:** February 2, 2026
**Company:** Parke Bancorp, Inc. (NASDAQ: PKBK)
**Headquarters:** Washington Township, New Jersey
**Asset Size:** $2.25 billion (as of December 31, 2025)
**Market Cap:** ~$266 million
**Current Stock Price:** $22.42

---

## Executive Summary

Parke Bancorp is a small community bank operating in southern New Jersey and the Philadelphia metropolitan area with a distinct niche: heavy CRE concentration combined with meaningful cannabis banking operations. The bank has demonstrated exceptional NIM recovery in 2025 (from 3.00% to 3.64%), producing ROA of 1.77% and ROTE of ~12%, metrics that significantly outperform the community bank peer group.

**The Variant View:** Consensus likely underestimates PKBK's earnings power normalization. The 2025 NIM surge to 4.09% in Q4 is not sustainable, but the bank's underlying earning power has been masked by (1) a one-time $9.5M theft loss in Q3 2023, (2) NIM compression bottoming in 2024, and (3) elevated cannabis deposit volatility. Normalized mid-cycle earnings power is approximately $2.50-$2.80 per share, placing the stock at ~8-9x normalized earnings. With tangible book value likely around $27-28 per share at year-end 2025, the stock trades at ~0.80-0.85x TBV, a discount that is hard to justify given the bank's credit quality, profitability metrics, and capital strength.

### Key Business Characteristics

1. **CRE-Focused Lender:** Loan portfolio heavily weighted toward commercial real estate (owner-occupied and non-owner occupied), construction, and residential investment properties.

2. **Cannabis Banking Niche:** One of the few banks actively serving cannabis industry clients. Cannabis-related deposits peaked at $177.3M in 2022, fell to $96.7M in late 2023, rebounded to $151.9M in Q4 2024, and declined to $61.9M by year-end 2025 (per Q4 2025 earnings release).

3. **Small Branch Footprint:** 7 branch offices; primarily relationship-driven commercial lending model.

4. **High Uninsured Deposits:** Approximately 50% of deposits are uninsured (driven by cannabis and municipal clients), creating potential liquidity risk during stress periods.

## What Consensus May Be Missing

### The Non-Obvious Insight

**Consensus View:** PKBK is a small, illiquid community bank with elevated CRE risk that will see earnings normalize lower as NIM compresses.

**What Consensus May Be Missing:**

1. **The 2023 Earnings Were Distorted, Not 2025**

   The $9.5M theft loss in Q3 2023 (an armored car robbery) wiped out that quarter's earnings. Adjusting for this one-time event, FY 2023 normalized earnings were ~$38M, or ~$3.20/share -- very close to FY 2025's $3.16. The FY 2024 trough ($2.27) was the anomaly driven by NIM compression at the cycle bottom. The bank is returning to its true earnings power, not experiencing an unsustainable spike.

2. **Cannabis Deposit Outflows Are a Feature, Not a Bug**

   The $90M cannabis deposit decline in 2025 reduced PKBK's highest-cost and most volatile funding source. The bank replaced this with more stable money market and checking deposits. The deposit mix is actually improving in quality even as cannabis headcount declines. This reduces funding cost volatility going forward.

3. **CRE Concentration is Mostly Non-Office**

   The "CRE is toxic" narrative primarily applies to office CRE. PKBK's portfolio is weighted toward residential construction, owner-occupied commercial (small business), and multi-family in the undersupplied NJ housing market. The bank has minimal office exposure. Painting all CRE-concentrated banks with the same brush misses the composition differences.

4. **Tier 1 Capital at 21% Provides Massive Buffer**

   Most community banks operate at 10-12% Tier 1. PKBK's 21% ratio means the bank could absorb 9% losses (as % of risk-weighted assets) before falling to "well-capitalized" minimums. At 0.53% NPLs and immaterial charge-offs, the market is pricing in credit stress that hasn't materialized.

5. **Valuation Discount Despite Superior Returns**

   PKBK produces 1.77% ROA vs. peer medians of 0.9-1.1%. It produces 12% ROTE vs. peer medians of 9-11%. Yet it trades at 0.82x TBV while peers trade at 0.95-1.2x. The discount is explained by illiquidity and stigma, not fundamentals. If PKBK simply traded at 1.0x TBV with its current returns, the stock would be $27.

## Investment Conclusion

### Summary Assessment

Parke Bancorp is a high-quality community bank franchise trading at a meaningful discount to intrinsic value. The bank's CRE concentration and cannabis banking niche create headline risk, but the underlying financial performance is excellent:

- **Profitability:** ROA of 1.77% and ROTE of 12%+ significantly exceed peers
- **Credit Quality:** NPL ratio of 0.53% with 321% coverage is pristine
- **Capital:** Tier 1 of 21% provides exceptional buffer
- **Valuation:** 0.82x TBV and 7x normalized P/E is undemanding

### Risks

1. CRE credit losses if NJ/Philly commercial real estate deteriorates
2. Deposit flight from uninsured accounts in a banking stress event
3. NIM compression if Fed cuts aggressively and curve flattens
4. Cannabis regulatory changes (both positive and negative scenarios)
5. Illiquidity and small float (~$266M market cap)

### Normalized Earnings Power

| Scenario | Normalized EPS | P/E at $22.42 |
|----------|----------------|---------------|
| Conservative | $2.50 | 9.0x |
| Base | $2.75 | 8.2x |
| Optimistic | $2.90 | 7.7x |

### Valuation Opinion

**PKBK is attractively valued on normalized earnings.** At the current price of $22.42:
- Downside appears limited to ~$20 (0.75x TBV) in a stress scenario
- Fair value range of $25-30 based on 1.0x TBV or 10x normalized earnings
- Potential M&A premium of $33-38 at 1.2-1.4x TBV

The market is pricing in credit stress that has not materialized and ignoring the bank's superior profitability metrics. For investors with tolerance for illiquidity and CRE cycle risk, PKBK offers an attractive risk/reward at current levels.
