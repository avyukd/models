**Analysis Date:** February 3, 2026

---

## Executive Summary

Red Violet is a high-quality, capital-light data analytics business with exceptional unit economics (84% adjusted gross margins), zero debt, and accelerating cash flow generation. The stock trades at 64x forward GAAP earnings and 38x trailing FCF - a premium valuation that requires continued 20%+ growth execution.

**Bottom Line:** The business fundamentals are strong and improving, but the current price already reflects significant margin expansion and growth. At $39.26, the stock offers limited margin of safety unless the company can sustain 20%+ revenue growth while expanding EBITDA margins toward their 40% target.

---

## Key Financial Drivers (What Actually Matters)

**1. Revenue Growth Sustainability (Most Critical)**
- FY2024: 25% growth to $75.2M
- Q1-Q3 2025: 21-26% YoY growth
- Consensus expects deceleration to ~14% in FY2026 ($100.5M)
- If growth slows to 15%, the premium multiple compresses significantly

**2. Margin Expansion Path to 40% EBITDA**
- FY2024 adjusted EBITDA margin: 31.4%
- Q3 2025 margin: 39% (near target)
- Every 1 point of margin expansion adds ~$0.06 to EPS at current revenue base
- Operating leverage is real: fixed costs spreading over growing revenue base

**3. Cash Conversion Quality**
- FY2024 FCF: $14.4M (143% YoY growth)
- FCF conversion on adjusted net income: ~125%
- This is the most attractive aspect of the financial profile

## What Consensus May Be Missing

### The Non-Obvious Insight: Operating Leverage Is Underappreciated

**Most analysts focus on revenue growth, but the real story is margin leverage.**

The math:
- FY2024 adjusted EBITDA margin: 31.4%
- Q3 2025 adjusted EBITDA margin: 39.0%
- Management target: 40%+

If revenue grows 15% but EBITDA margin expands 5 points:
- Revenue: +15% ($75M to $86M = +$11M)
- EBITDA at old margin (31%): $26.7M
- EBITDA at new margin (36%): $31.0M
- **EBITDA grows 31%, not 15%**

The company is at an inflection point where incremental revenue drops to the bottom line at very high rates. Every dollar of new revenue generates ~$0.50+ of incremental EBITDA given the fixed cost base and 84% gross margins.

**Street models likely assume margin expansion, but may underestimate the magnitude of earnings acceleration as the company approaches 40% EBITDA.**

### Additional Non-Consensus Observations

1. **Balance sheet optionality is mispriced.** $45M cash + FCF generation + buyback authorization = ability to be opportunistic on M&A or aggressive on buybacks without dilution. At $39, buybacks would be accretive to normalized earnings power.

2. **Customer count growth is accelerating, not decelerating.** Added 304 IDI customers in Q3 2025 vs. 183 in Q4 2024. Higher-tier customers ($100K+ revenue) grew from 72 to 96 in FY2024. This suggests pricing power and market share gains.

3. **FOREWARN real estate exposure is a hidden risk.** Over 590 REALTOR associations contracted. Real estate transaction volumes are cyclical. A housing downturn would pressure this revenue stream, though IDI diversification mitigates this.

## Investment Conclusion

**At $39.26, RDVT is a high-quality business trading at a fair-to-full valuation.**

**What must be true for this price to make sense:**
1. Revenue growth sustains at 15%+ for 3+ years
2. EBITDA margins reach and hold 40%
3. Competitive position remains defensible against larger players
4. No material regulatory disruption to data access

**What breaks the thesis:**
1. Revenue growth decelerates to <10% (valuation compresses rapidly)
2. Pricing pressure from competitors erodes gross margins
3. Data privacy regulation restricts product functionality

**Recommendation Framework:**

| Price | Assessment |
|-------|------------|
| <$30 | Attractive - margin of safety on normalized earnings |
| $30-40 | Fair value - priced for execution |
| $40-50 | Full value - requires bull case outcomes |
| >$50 | Expensive - growth expectations stretched |

**Current price of $39.26 sits in the "fair value" zone.** This is a hold for existing shareholders and a watch-list candidate for new investors seeking a pullback entry.
