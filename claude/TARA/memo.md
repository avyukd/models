## 2. Key Drivers (The 2 Things That Matter)

**First: 12-month durability in BCG-Unresponsive.** The 6-month CR of 68.2% is competitive. The 12-month CR decaying to 33% is not. This is the load-bearing variable for the nearer-term commercial indication. If the registrational cohort confirms this decay, TARA-002 is a fourth-or-fifth-best option in a market where cretostimogene (24-month CR 41.8%, BLA filing 2026) will likely be approved before TARA-002 even completes Phase 3 enrollment.

**Second: BCG-Naive as the real option.** The 57.9% 12-month CR with 100% maintenance in BCG-Naive is the genuinely differentiated data point. FDA has aligned on a registrational path here. No approved therapy targets this population specifically. But this is also the indication that is furthest from revenue -- Phase 3 has not started, and commercial launch would be 2029-2030 at earliest.

The market watches headline CR rates. What it should watch is the durability decay curve and cretostimogene's regulatory timeline.

---

## 3. Thesis Summary

- TARA-002 has a legitimately differentiated profile on safety (zero Grade 3+ AEs) and administration (instills like BCG, no cold chain, no co-administration), which matters in community urology where 68% of NMIBC patients are treated.
- The BCG-Unresponsive 12-month durability problem (68% to 33% decay) is a material competitive weakness that the headline numbers obscure. Cretostimogene's 24-month CR of 41.8% is meaningfully better at the timepoint that matters.
- BCG-Naive is the cleaner opportunity but is years from commercialization, and the BCG shortage tailwind may dissipate if Merck's manufacturing expansion comes online in late 2026.
- CG Oncology is filing its BLA in 2026 with Breakthrough Therapy Designation. TARA-002 is starting Phase 3 in 2H 2026. The competitive timing mismatch is severe -- cretostimogene will have 3-4 years of commercial head start.
- At ~$322M market cap with ~$125-133M cash, the market assigns ~$190-200M in pipeline value, implying a moderate probability of success. This is not obviously cheap or expensive for a Phase 2 asset with mixed durability data.

## 5. The Setup

Protara is a single-asset clinical-stage biotech. TARA-002 is an intravesical immunotherapy for high-risk NMIBC administered via the same instillation procedure urologists already use for BCG. The drug has a genuinely clean safety profile and avoids the logistical friction of gene therapy (Adstiladrin) or BCG co-administration (Anktiva).

The structural problem is timing. The NMIBC market for BCG-unresponsive patients now has four approved therapies plus cretostimogene filing for approval. By the time TARA-002 could reach market (2029-2030 at earliest), it would be the sixth or seventh option in a ~$2.4B market where early movers have built KOL relationships, payer contracts, and physician habits.

Today's 19% selloff despite positive data reflects the market digesting this reality: the data is good but not great, the competition is ahead, and the Dec 2025 offering diluted the share base by ~39%.

## 8. Risks and Kill Switches

**Thesis invalidators:**
- Phase 3 12-month CR in BCG-Unresponsive confirms 33% durability rate (drug is uncompetitive)
- Cretostimogene approval with strong label and rapid uptake (market compressed)
- Additional dilutive raise at depressed prices (>20% dilution)
- Phase 3 enrollment delays pushing timelines past cash runway

**Permanent capital loss scenarios:**
- Phase 3 failure (stock goes to cash value, ~$2.30-2.50/share)
- BCG shortage resolution eliminates urgency for alternatives in Naive population

## 9. Positioning and Portfolio Logic

**Decision: No position.** This is a pass, not a conviction call in either direction.

The bull case requires believing that safety/convenience differentiation overcomes a durability deficit in a market where competitors have multi-year head starts and the addressable population may shrink. The bear case requires shorting a $322M biotech with $130M+ in cash and a legitimately differentiated safety profile in a real unmet medical need -- the downside to cash value is only ~60%, and a positive Phase 3 readout could send the stock to $15-20+.

The risk/reward is symmetric and assumption-heavy. I cannot size this responsibly. The 12-month durability decay is a real concern that the market is only now beginning to price, but the BCG-Naive opportunity provides a floor of optionality that prevents a clean short thesis.

**What moves this out of the Too Hard pile:**
- Phase 3 enrollment data confirming adequate patient recruitment pace
- Updated 12-month durability data from the full registrational BCG-Unresponsive cohort
- Clarity on cretostimogene's FDA review timeline post-BLA completion
- Stock trading at or below cash value (creates asymmetric long entry)

**If forced to act:** I would set a limit buy at $3.50-4.00 (near cash value) where the risk/reward shifts meaningfully in favor of a long position with the BCG-Naive option essentially free. At $6.00, you are paying ~$200M for an unproven pipeline option with mixed data and severe competitive timing disadvantage. That is not a price where I want to deploy capital.
