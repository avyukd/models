# Gold Industry Cycle Position — TWR.V Context

## Where We Are in the Gold Cycle

**Late-stage bull market, but with structural support.**

- Gold at ~$2,900+ USD (Feb 2026), near all-time highs
- GDXJ (junior gold miners ETF) up **167-178% in 2025** -- one of the best years ever for junior miners
- Central banks bought 863t in 2025, expected ~755t in 2026 -- still elevated vs pre-2022
- Gold mine production plateauing at ~3,672t (2025 record), limited new supply coming online

## Junior Explorer Capital Environment

**Best in a decade.** Flow-through financing in Canada is available and priced favorably. Investors are actively funding exploration plays. The GDXJ 52-week range of $47-$152 shows the magnitude of the re-rating.

## Open-Pit Economics at Current Gold Prices

| Gold Price (USD) | Typical Open-Pit Cutoff Grade |
|---|---|
| $1,750 | 0.65 g/t |
| $2,250 | 0.50 g/t |
| $2,900+ (current) | ~0.35-0.40 g/t |

**At current gold prices, TWR.V's 0.65 g/t bulk grade is solidly above cutoff.** Even a pullback to $2,000 keeps the project marginal. The high-grade Thunder North zones (2+ g/t) would be economic at almost any gold price.

## M&A Cycle

Majors and mid-tiers are actively acquiring. Gold producers delivered strong 2025 results and M&A activity is accelerating as companies seek growth to replace depleting reserves. This is favorable for juniors with scale potential.

## What This Means for TWR.V

- **Tailwind**: Gold price makes previously marginal grades economic. Financing environment is favorable for the inevitable next raise.
- **Risk**: If gold corrects 20-30%, the junior space gets crushed first and hardest. TWR.V with $766K cash would struggle to raise at favorable terms in a bear market.
- **Net**: The macro is as good as it gets for a company like TWR.V. But "as good as it gets" also means the cycle could turn.
