# Tower Resources Ltd. (TSXV: TWR) -- Financial Survivability & Dilution Analysis

**Date**: February 19, 2026
**Market Cap**: ~C$33.8M | **Share Price**: C$0.195 | **Shares Outstanding**: ~173.3M
**Stage**: Pre-revenue gold exploration | **Key Asset**: Rabbit North, British Columbia

---

## Bottom Line Up Front

Tower Resources is a pre-revenue exploration-stage company with C$766K cash on hand against a ~C$34M market cap. The company has survived for years through serial flow-through private placements, issuing equity roughly annually. With drilling resuming in March 2026 and only ~2-4 months of G&A runway remaining (before any drill spend), **a capital raise is near-certain within the next 3-6 months**. The question is not *if* but *at what price and how dilutive*. Recent drill results at Blue Sky and Thunder North provide the narrative for the next raise, but the financial reality is that this company is structurally dependent on continuous equity issuance.

## 4. Financing Risk: The Next Raise

- **Probability**: >95% within 6 months
- **Size**: C$1.5-3.0M
- **Structure**: Flow-through PP (tax benefit allows near-market pricing)
- **ITM warrant exercises** (8.2M @ C$0.18, expiring mid-2026) could provide ~C$1.5M bridge

---

## 5. What Consensus May Be Missing

1. **ITM warrants are doing real work** -- C$1.5M potential cash doubles current balance, may defer dilutive placement 6-12 months.
2. **Sandstorm 3% NSR** is a permanent tax on economics -- meaningful drag on project NPV at modest 0.5-0.7 g/t grades.
3. **Flow-through mechanics mask true cost of capital** -- tax benefit means dilution less punitive per dollar raised than it appears.
