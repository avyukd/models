# CHER.V (Cheelcare Inc.) - Management & Capital Allocation Analysis

## Key Finding

**Founder-controlled micro-cap with structural alignment but unproven capital allocation discipline.** Co-founders Eugene Cherny (CEO) and Dima Paltsev (CFO) collectively control ~35% of shares outstanding. Combined insider ownership exceeds 45%. This is meaningful alignment for a pre-profit company. However, the company just completed its RTO in July 2025, leaving no track record of public-market capital allocation decisions. The critical question is whether founders will prioritize reaching profitability or pursue aggressive growth funded by serial dilution.

## Incentive Structure & Alignment

High founder ownership (35% combined) creates structural alignment. When CEOs own 18% of a company, they feel stock price declines personally.

**However:** The company is cash-flow negative, losing $547K in Q2 FY26 alone against $849K cash on hand. This creates pressure for future capital raises. Will founders accept slower growth to preserve ownership, or dilute aggressively?

**Incentive Alignment: Mixed** (Strong ownership, untested under capital pressure)

---

## Stock-Based Compensation Discipline

- Total options to insiders: ~591,000 (~3% potential dilution)
- Warrants outstanding from RTO financing: ~2.3M at $1.50 strike
- No buyback program (nor would one make sense at this stage)

The RTO structure diluted original Cheelcare shareholders from 100% to ~54%. Necessary financing, but sets precedent that equity is the funding mechanism.

**Dilution Risk: Moderate-to-High**

---

## Capital Allocation Track Record

**No track record exists.** Cheelcare listed in July 2025. No history of acquisitions, buybacks, or dividends.

**Pre-RTO decisions:**
- Convertible debentures ($1.1M) converted at 25% discount--standard terms
- Private placement ($3.5M at $0.75 with half-warrants)--standard TSX-V RTO structure
- New 16,000 sq ft facility lease (2025)--aggressive commitment on ~$1.6M revenue base

**Capital Position Today:**
- Cash: $849K
- Quarterly burn: ~$480K
- **Runway: ~2 quarters**

**Capital Allocation Discipline: Unproven**

---

## The One Thing That Determines Value Creation

**Whether founders can reach operating profitability before exhausting their ownership stake through serial dilution.**

Quarterly burn of $480K on $849K cash means the dilution clock is ticking. If the next 2-3 financings occur at progressively higher prices with insider participation, founders are acting like owners. If financings occur at declining prices with founders not participating, alignment degrades.

---

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**The insider ownership is real but overstated as protection against dilution.**

45% insider ownership looks like strong alignment. But much of this is escrowed/restricted, and founders cannot prevent dilution--only share in the pain. Founder ownership percentage matters less than founder *behavior* in the next financing. Watch whether insiders participate.
