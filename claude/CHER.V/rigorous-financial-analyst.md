# Cheelcare Inc. (TSXV: CHER) - Financial Analysis

**Date:** February 1, 2026
**Stock Price:** C$0.98
**Market Cap:** ~C$19M (disputed - some sources show C$6.7M)
**Fiscal Year End:** April 30

---

## Key Finding: Cash Runway Is The Binding Constraint

This is a micro-cap, pre-profitability Canadian medical device company burning cash at an accelerating rate while revenue declines year-over-year. The stock trades on regulatory catalysts (Medicare reimbursement, FDA 510(k)) rather than fundamentals. At current burn rate, the company has approximately 2-3 quarters of cash runway before requiring additional financing.

## The Three Things That Matter

**1. Cash Burn vs. Cash Position**
- Cash at Oct 31, 2025: **$849K**
- Working capital: **$1.03M**
- Q2 cash burn (implied): ~$500K+/quarter
- **Runway: ~2 quarters at current burn**

This is the load-bearing variable. The company raised C$3.5M in late 2024/early 2025. That cash is largely consumed. Dilution is coming.

**2. Revenue Direction Is Wrong**
Revenue declined 9-10% YoY in Q2 despite expanded dealer network (500+ locations), new manufacturing facility, and Medicare reimbursement in progress. Management calls this "foundational" but declining revenue during scaling is unusual.

**3. Operating Loss Explosion**
Net loss expanded from ($34K) to ($547K) - a 16x increase. Operating expenses run at ~$775K/quarter (~$3.1M annualized) against TTM revenue of ~$1.6M. Spending 2x revenue on opex while revenue contracts.

## Normalized Earnings Analysis

**No path to normalized earnings without significant revenue growth.**

At current gross margins (58%), breakeven requires ~$5.3M in annual revenue (3.4x current run-rate), assuming no OpEx growth.

---

## Key Financial Drivers

1. **Cash runway** - Next capital raise timing and terms determine equity value
2. **Revenue inflection** - Medicare approval (Jan 2026) should accelerate U.S. sales. Q3/Q4 FY26 results critical
3. **Curio commercialization** - The robotic wheelchair is the growth story but contributes no meaningful revenue yet

---

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**Medicare approval is necessary but may not be sufficient.**

- If dealer network expanded from ~300 to 500+ locations, why is revenue flat-to-down?
- 2,000 cumulative Companion units over company history implies ASP ~$700-800/unit and limited penetration
- Medicare primarily benefits the Companion (add-on device), not the higher-value Curio

The bear case: demand constraints are structural (small TAM, entrenched competition from Invacare/Permobil/Sunrise Medical), and cash burns before reaching escape velocity.

## Conclusion

Speculative micro-cap bet on regulatory catalysts. January 2026 Medicare approval is genuine, but financials show declining revenue, 16x YoY loss expansion, ~2 quarters cash runway, and negative book equity. Position sizing should reflect binary outcome and high probability of near-term dilution.
