# CHER.V (Cheelcare Inc.) - Investment Memo

**Decision: NEUTRAL (Too Hard / Too Hairy)**

**Conviction:** N/A
**Time Horizon:** N/A
**Classification:** Too Hard

---

## Why This Is A Pass

This is a sub-scale, pre-profitability micro-cap with ~2 quarters of cash runway, declining revenue, no moat, and a thesis that depends entirely on regulatory catalysts (FDA 510(k), HCPCS codes for Medicare/Medicaid). The variant perception required to own this stock is that Medicare reimbursement unlocks meaningful revenue acceleration before the company runs out of cash. I cannot underwrite that with confidence.

**The math does not work:**
- Cash: $849K
- Quarterly burn: ~$480-500K
- Runway: ~2 quarters
- Revenue trajectory: Declining (-10% YoY in Q2 despite expanded dealer network)
- Path to breakeven: Requires 3.4x current revenue with no OpEx growth

Dilution is coming regardless of catalyst outcomes. The question is only at what price. Without a variant view on *when* and *how much* revenue inflects, this is a lottery ticket, not an investment.

---

## Key Drivers (The 1-3 Things That Actually Matter)

1. **Cash runway vs. revenue inflection timing** - Can revenue accelerate before the next dilutive financing? Current trajectory says no.

2. **Medicare/HCPCS reimbursement** - This is the only catalyst that could change the unit economics. January 2026 Medicare approval for Companion is real but insufficient--HCPCS codes for the higher-value products are still pending.

3. **Dealer network effectiveness** - 500+ dealer locations, 20+ VA centers, yet revenue is *declining*. This is the most concerning data point. If demand existed, we should see it.

---

## Why I Cannot Form A Variant View

| Market Believes | What I Would Need To Believe | Gap |
|-----------------|------------------------------|-----|
| Regulatory approval will unlock demand | Same, but need evidence | Revenue declined with expanded distribution--why? |
| Technology is differentiated | True, but insufficient | Patents do not create switching costs in wheelchairs |
| Founder alignment protects downside | 45% ownership is real | But founders cannot prevent dilution, only share the pain |

**The honest answer:** I do not have differentiated information on whether Medicare reimbursement changes dealer economics enough to reverse the revenue decline. Without that, I am guessing.

---

## What Would Move This Out Of The Too Hard Pile

1. **Q3 or Q4 FY26 revenue inflection** - If Medicare approval in January 2026 translates to visible revenue acceleration in the next 1-2 quarters, the thesis becomes underwriteable.

2. **Financing at a premium** - If the company raises at $1.50+ with insider participation, signals founder confidence in near-term inflection.

3. **HCPCS code approval for Curio/Xceed** - The high-value products (~$20K ASP) are not yet reimburseable. This is the real unlock, not Companion.

---

## The Bear Case Is Default

- $849K cash, $480K/quarter burn = financing by mid-2026
- Revenue *declined* 10% YoY despite 67% increase in dealer locations
- Operating losses exploded 16x YoY
- Negative book equity ($3.16M deficit)
- No moat--patented technology in a reimbursement-driven market with entrenched competitors

The burden of proof is on the bull case. I have not seen evidence that demand exists at scale.

---

## Red Flags That Prevent Conviction

1. **Dealer expansion without revenue growth** - This is not a "building foundations" story. If dealers could sell the product, they would.

2. **Operating loss explosion** - From ($34K) to ($547K) in one year while revenue declined. Spending profile is disconnected from demand.

3. **Pre-profitability with no runway** - This is not a company with 2-3 years to figure it out. Clock is ticking.

---

## If Forced To Take A Position

**I would not.** But if required:

- Maximum 25bps position (lottery ticket sizing)
- Entry only after Q3 FY26 results show revenue inflection
- Hard stop if financing occurs below $0.75 (RTO price)
- Sell into any strength on regulatory announcements without revenue follow-through

---

## Conclusion

CHER.V is a pre-profit micro-cap trading on hope that regulatory catalysts will unlock demand that is not currently visible in the financials. The technology is real but not a moat. Insider alignment is real but does not prevent dilution. Cash runway is ~2 quarters.

**The right answer is to watch, not to own.**

If revenue inflects in Q3/Q4 FY26, revisit. Until then, this belongs in the too hard pile.
