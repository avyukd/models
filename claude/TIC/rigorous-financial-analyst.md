# TIC Solutions (NYSE: TIC) - Financial Analysis

## Key Financial Drivers

**1. Leverage and Deleveraging Trajectory** - With $1.6B term loan debt post-NV5 merger (4.1x net leverage), the stock trades on ability to deleverage to target 3.0x through EBITDA growth and FCF generation. Every 0.5x turn of deleverage unlocks ~$125M equity value at current multiples.

**2. Margin Normalization Post-Integration** - Current 16.3% Adjusted EBITDA margin vs. 15.5-16.5% 2026 guidance. Higher-margin NV5 segments (51% GM in consulting, 48% in geospatial vs. 28.5% legacy) create path to margin expansion, but integration execution risk is high.

**3. Organic Revenue Growth Sustainability** - 4-7% organic growth masks complexity; company exited lower-margin relationships. True underlying demand in TICC services (compliance-mandated, recurring) vs. macro/oil & gas cyclicality determines multiple.

---

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**The goodwill and intangible asset intensity is extreme and understated in typical coverage.** The July 2024 ASP Acuren acquisition ($1.88B) recognized ~$866M goodwill and ~$775M identifiable intangibles per SEC filings. The August 2025 NV5 acquisition ($1.7B) adds another layer. Combined, TIC's balance sheet is dominated by acquisition-related intangibles with $26M+ quarterly amortization drag that hits GAAP earnings but is added back to Adjusted EBITDA.

This creates a structural gap: Adjusted EBITDA of $240-250M guidance vs. likely GAAP net loss. The company is "profitable" only on a non-GAAP basis. True economic earnings require subtracting: (a) interest expense (~$100M+ annually at SOFR+275 on $1.6B), (b) maintenance capex, (c) ongoing integration/transformation costs, and (d) any normalized SBC. The FCF yield looks attractive at 6%+, but this assumes no acquisition spending and minimal SBC - both assumptions warrant scrutiny.

### Debt Details
- Rate: SOFR + 275 bps (repriced Jan 2025, down 75 bps)
- Maturity: 2031
- Covenant-lite structure typical for sponsor-backed deals

### Deleveraging Math
At $245M Adj. EBITDA and 4.1x leverage, to reach 3.0x requires:
- Option A: ~$270M EBITDA (10% growth) holding debt constant
- Option B: ~$150M debt paydown from FCF (2+ years at current generation)

Both paths are plausible but require execution. Any margin miss or growth slowdown extends the timeline materially.

### Goodwill/Intangible Concentration
The July 2024 Acuren acquisition alone recognized:
- Goodwill: ~$866M
- Identifiable Intangibles: ~$775M
- Total Assets: ~$2.2B

This means ~75% of the balance sheet is acquisition-related intangibles. Impairment risk exists if TICC market multiples compress or integration fails.

## Summary Assessment

**TIC is a highly levered roll-up trading at a premium multiple to TIC industry peers, with GAAP losses masked by non-GAAP presentation.**

The investment case requires belief in:
1. NV5 integration success and synergy realization
2. Organic growth sustaining 4-5% in cyclically sensitive end markets
3. Margin expansion from segment mix shift
4. Successful deleveraging without additional equity dilution

Normalized mid-cycle earnings power suggests fair value of $11-14/share assuming flawless execution. Current price (~$10) offers modest upside but limited margin of safety given leverage and integration risk.

**Earnings quality: Low (GAAP losses, heavy adjusted metrics reliance)**
**Cash flow quality: Medium (FCF positive but heavily burdened by interest)**
**Balance sheet: Stretched (4.1x leverage, intangible-heavy)**
