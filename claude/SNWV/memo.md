## The Setup

SANUWAVE makes UltraMIST, a low-frequency ultrasound wound debridement device, and sells disposable applicators as consumables. 98% of revenue is UltraMIST; dermaPACE (shockwave for diabetic foot ulcers) has minimal traction.

The business was nearly dead in 2023-2024 due to a horrific capital structure: convertible notes and warrants created $40M+ of derivative liabilities and forced constant equity issuance. The October 2024 restructuring (1:375 reverse split + $43M debt-to-equity conversion + $10.3M PIPE) cleaned this up. The September 2025 J.P. Morgan refinancing ($24M term loan at SOFR +350) normalized the debt structure.

What matters now: the wound care market is experiencing a regulatory-driven dislocation. CMS skin substitute spending exploded from $256M (2019) to $10B+ (2024). CMS is crushing it back with 90% reimbursement cuts. Organogenesis, MiMedx, and 200+ placental/amniotic product companies are scrambling. UltraMIST is billed under a procedural code that is unaffected.
