# SANUWAVE Health (SNWV) - Financial Analysis

**Note:** Ticker SNWV is SANUWAVE Health, Inc., a medical device company focused on wound care (not "SandWave AI").

---

## Key Finding: Operationally Profitable, But GAAP Earnings Distorted by Non-Cash Derivative Swings

The core business is now operationally profitable at ~$1.5-2M operating income per quarter. However, GAAP net income is meaningless due to massive non-cash derivative liability mark-to-market swings (+/- $6-20M per quarter). The October 2024 recapitalization (1:375 reverse split + $43M debt-to-equity conversion) cleaned up a disastrously dilutive capital structure. The question now: can the company sustain 30-40% growth and translate operating income into actual free cash flow?

### Debt Structure Evolution
- Pre-Oct 2024: $43.2M convertible notes + warrants (1.7B pre-split shares of dilution)
- Oct 2024: Debt-to-equity exchange + 1:375 reverse split + $10.3M PIPE @ $8.25
- Sept 2025: Refinanced to J.P. Morgan $23M term loan (4-year) + $5M revolver (SOFR +350 bps, no prepayment penalty)

**Critical observation:** The derivative liabilities causing GAAP earnings chaos were tied to the old convertible notes/warrants. Post-restructuring, these should largely disappear, making future GAAP earnings more interpretable.

## Key Financial Drivers

**1. Revenue growth rate (most important)**
- Currently 22-36% YoY; deceleration from 60%
- Installed base of 1,000+ systems; growth requires new account acquisition
- Healogics GPO addition and large account pipeline are the swing factors
- If growth decelerates to <20%, multiple compression is severe

**2. SBC trajectory**
- Post-restructuring, SBC restarted from zero
- Q3 2025 run-rate: ~$5.6M annualized (~12% of FY25 revenue)
- This directly offsets operating leverage; watch whether it grows faster than revenue

**3. Interest expense post-refinancing**
- Old debt: ~$7M+ annual interest
- New J.P. Morgan facility: ~$3M annual interest at current rates
- $4M+ annual savings flows directly to bottom line

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**The derivative liability noise is ending.** Most financial databases show massive GAAP losses (-$31M in FY24) that are disconnected from operating reality. The October 2024 restructuring eliminated the convertible notes and warrants that caused the derivative mark-to-market swings. Future quarters should show clean GAAP earnings that actually reflect operating performance.

**Implication:** As GAAP earnings become interpretable, screens and quantitative models that filtered out SNWV for "massive losses" may start including it. The company could report positive GAAP EPS in FY2026 if derivative liabilities stabilize.

**Risk to this view:** If management issues new equity-linked instruments (convertibles, warrants), derivative accounting chaos returns.

## Summary Assessment

SANUWAVE is an operationally improving business (75-78% gross margins, $5-7M operating income run-rate) obscured by a historically disastrous capital structure. The October 2024 restructuring and September 2025 refinancing have largely fixed the balance sheet. The question is whether 25-35% growth can continue and whether SBC discipline will hold.

**Valuation:** At ~6x revenue and 20x+ Adj EBITDA, the stock is priced for execution. Not cheap, but not absurd for a 30%+ grower with 78% gross margins in a market with regulatory tailwinds against competitors.

**Key risk:** Growth deceleration + SBC dilution could make current valuation look expensive quickly.
