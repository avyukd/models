# TSMC (TSM) - Rigorous Financial Analysis

## Key Finding Upfront

TSMC is a rare case of a company with genuine pricing power, fortress balance sheet, minimal dilution, and exceptional cash conversion - yet trades at a meaningful premium to historical averages. The investment question is binary: Are you paying up for the AI super-cycle sustaining 25-30% revenue growth, or buying into a cyclical peak that reverts to mid-teens growth?

---

## Business Financial Profile

| Attribute | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| Capital Intensity | Very high - $30-56B annual capex |
| Fixed vs Variable Cost | Overwhelmingly fixed (depreciation-heavy) |
| Operating Leverage | Extreme - small utilization changes swing margins 500+ bps |
| Cyclicality | Moderate (historically); AI demand currently smoothing cycles |
| Revenue Quality | High - sticky customer relationships, long design cycles |

| Period | Revenue (NT$B) | YoY Growth | Gross Margin | Operating Margin |
|--------|----------------|------------|--------------|------------------|
| FY2023 | 2,162 | -4.5% | 54.4% | 42.6% |

**Key Observations:**
- FY2023 was a trough year with margin compression from N3 ramp and lower utilization
- FY2024-2025 recovery was sharper than historical patterns due to AI accelerator demand
- Q4 2024 margins (59% gross, 49% operating) are near all-time highs

### SBC Analysis (Minimal)

- RSA issuance: 4.2M shares in 2024 (vs. 26B shares outstanding = 0.016%)
- Offset by: 3.25M share buyback for cancellation
- Employee profit sharing: NT$70B paid in **cash**, not equity

**Conclusion:** SBC is immaterial. No adjustment needed.

## Key Financial Drivers

1. **HPC/AI Revenue Growth Rate** - THE variable. HPC grew 58% in 2024.
2. **Gross Margin Sustainability** - Every 100bps = ~$900M operating income.
3. **CapEx Intensity** - $52-56B in 2026 constrains near-term FCF.

---

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**Non-consensus insight: The overseas fab margin dilution may be overstated.**

Street models assume 2-3% gross margin dilution from Arizona/Japan fabs. However:
- Arizona Fab 1 achieved yields comparable to Taiwan in Q4 2024
- Customers willing to pay premiums for US-produced chips
- CHIPS Act subsidies offset higher labor costs

If overseas fabs achieve 90%+ of Taiwan margins (vs. consensus 80-85%), geographic diversification becomes accretive.

---

## Bottom Line

TSMC has exceptional financial quality. The question is purely valuation. At 20x forward earnings, you're paying for sustained AI-driven growth at 25%+ CAGR and margins holding near record highs. Mid-cycle normalized earnings of $10-11/share at historical 22x = $220-242 fair value. Current price is reasonable but not cheap.
