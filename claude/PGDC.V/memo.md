## 2. Key Drivers (The 1-3 Things That Actually Matter)

**1. Verified Economics Do Not Exist.** The company is building a mine without a feasibility study. There is no independently verified AISC, no reserve estimate, no NPV/IRR assessment. All cost claims ($1,300-1,500/oz AISC) originate from promotional materials. The NI 43-101 technical report is expected Q2 2026 - AFTER first pour. This is not normal.

**2. The Profit-Split Structure Impairs Common Shareholder Returns.** The Black River Mine preferred structure (80/20 then 60/40) means common shareholders bear 100% operating risk while receiving 20% of near-term cash flows. At reasonable production estimates, common shareholders' 10-year NPV is ~$107M vs. $275M market cap - paying 2.6x NPV for full execution success.

**3. Gold Is At Late-Cycle/Peak Prices.** Gold at ~$2,700/oz is 50-90% above mid-cycle ($1,400-1,800). Current margins are unsustainably high. A return to mid-cycle would compress margins by 50-70%. Without verified AISC, we cannot assess whether Calcatreu survives mean reversion.

## 3. Thesis Summary

- **Unverified economics + late-cycle prices + profit-split structure = unfavorable risk/reward**
- Pan American Silver, a sophisticated $5B producer, divested Calcatreu for $15M in 2018 as "non-core" - a negative signal bulls dismiss
- At $275M market cap, priced for flawless execution at peak gold prices

- Cannot responsibly size a position without verified cost data

## 5. The Setup

**Business Context:**
Patagonia Gold is a junior gold/silver miner transitioning from development to first production at Calcatreu in Rio Negro, Argentina. First pour expected Q1 2026. The company holds a 900,000+ hectare land package across three provinces, though only Rio Negro is actively developable (Chubut has mining restrictions; Santa Cruz assets are minimal).

**How We Got Here:**
- Hunt Mining and Patagonia merged 2019-2020
- Insiders funded ~$300M over 30 years without dilution
- Stock ran from $0.04 to $0.80 (2000%+) in 2025 as construction progressed
- Current market cap: $275M CAD

**Structural Forces:**
- Gold at late-cycle highs creating favorable first-production timing
- Argentina under Milei experiencing rare pro-business window
- No external equity raised; 75% insider ownership
- $40M preferred financing creates profit-split drag

## 9. Positioning & Portfolio Logic

### Why This Does NOT Deserve Capital Now

1. **No verifiable edge:** We cannot underwrite AISC, production, or mine life with any confidence
2. **Adverse asymmetry:** Paying for bull case success; bear case implies 85% downside
3. **Opportunity cost:** Capital can be deployed in situations with verified economics
4. **Timing:** Wait for data - NI 43-101 and first production quarters will provide actual cost data

### What Would Move This Out of the Too Hard Pile

1. **Verified AISC below $1,400/oz** from actual production or NI 43-101
2. **Stock price decline to $100-120M market cap** creating margin of safety
3. **Gold price decline** to mid-cycle ($1,500-1,700) proving project viability under stress
4. **RIGI registration** providing Argentina stability protection

### If Forced to Trade

If required to have a view: Slight short bias given late-cycle gold exposure, unverified economics, and full-success pricing. However, shorting a 2000% runner with 75% insider ownership entering first production is high risk. Better to pass entirely.

## 10. Decision Rationale

**Why Neutral (Too Hard)?**

This is not a "good company at a fair price that I should own." This is a pre-production junior gold miner with:

1. **No feasibility study** - We cannot verify any cost or production claim
2. **Late-cycle commodity exposure** - Entering production at peak prices with no visibility into cost structure to assess mean reversion survival
3. **Punitive profit-split structure** - Common shareholders receive 20% of near-term cash flows
4. **Full-success pricing** - $275M market cap requires bull case execution
5. **Negative strategic signal** - Pan American Silver, a sophisticated Argentina operator, sold this asset as "non-core" for $15M
6. **Argentina concentration without RIGI** - 100% exposure to jurisdiction with serial default history, no stability protection

**The core problem:** We do not have enough information to form a variant view. The bulls assume promotional AISC figures are accurate. The bears assume PAAS saw something concerning. We cannot arbitrate this without actual production data.

**When data is this ambiguous and valuation is this full, the correct decision is to pass.**
