## Gold Price Cycle Position: Late-Stage Bull, But Structurally Underpinned

Gold trading at ~**$4,990/oz** as of mid-February 2026, having breached $5,000 intra-month. ~100%+ gain from ~$1,800-2,000 range in 2022-2023.

- Current bull began late 2022. At ~735+ days, mid-duration relative to 2001-2011 cycle (3,650 days).
- 16-year macro cycle framework places us ~10 years into historically 11-12 year upleg. **Late-cycle on macro framework.**
- Structural drivers (central bank buying 863 tonnes in 2025, de-dollarization, fiscal deficits, geopolitical fragmentation) are qualitatively different from prior cycles.

**Industry AISC:** ~$1,605/oz in Q3 2025 (up 9% y/y). At $5,000 gold, industry earning **$3,400/oz margins** -- 210% above AISC. 97% of primary gold production profitable.

## Junior Explorer Financing: Cycle Is Hot

- YTD funds raised by junior/intermediate miners: **US$12.8B** through Oct 2025, exceeding full-year 2024 total of US$10.3B
- Gold financings rebounded **136% y/y** to US$6.7B

**Hot financing window.** Capital availability is not a constraint -- quality projects are.

## M&A Cycle: Majors Running Out of Easy Targets

**The single most important dynamic for micro-cap explorer valuations.**

- Mining M&A: **$139B across 91 deals**, **77%+ focused on gold/silver**
- Gold-specific M&A: $31B across 84 transactions, deal volume up 42%
- Acquisition premiums: **35-45%** range
- **Zero major gold discoveries (2+ Moz) in both 2023 and 2024** -- first time in recorded history
- Average discovery size fallen from 7.7 Moz to 4.4 Moz

After exhausting de-risked targets in 2024-2025, majors are forced to look at earlier-stage exploration assets and less conventional jurisdictions. **2026 is likely the year they start hunting district-scale plays and juniors.**

- Dual gold-copper projects disproportionately sought after

## Papua New Guinea Jurisdiction

**Fraser Institute: 57th of 82** jurisdictions (below-median).

### Mining Bill 2025: Material Regulatory Risk

- **State equity: up to 30%** in any mining project (deferred payment from future earnings)
- **Royalties: 5% with state equity, 10% without** -- effectively forcing state equity
- Grandfathering for existing mining lease holders only
- **New projects and exploration licenses fall under new regime**

For an explorer without a mining lease, any future development subject to new bill if passed. 30% dilution of project economics + higher royalty burden.

---

## What Consensus May Be Missing

The M&A cascade into the exploration tier is about to accelerate. Majors have largely exhausted de-risked acquisition targets. The combination of (a) zero major discoveries for two years, (b) record gold prices, and (c) all-time-high producer FCF creates conditions where **exploration-stage acquisitions at premiums well above historical norms become rational**. A major with $50B market cap buying a $50M explorer at 100% premium is a rounding error.

**Counterweight:** PNG's Mining Bill 2025 could dampen M&A interest from risk-averse majors, though those with existing PNG operations (Newmont via Lihir) would be natural acquirers at a jurisdictional discount.
