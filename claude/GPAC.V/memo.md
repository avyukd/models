## The Decision

**Fundamental: NEUTRAL.** Genuine geology in a terrible jurisdiction. The Wild Dog corridor has real district-scale potential, but PNG's Mining Bill 2025 structurally caps upside (30% state free-carry + 5% royalty in success case), the same deposit defeated prior operators, and $1.8M cash means dilutive financing is imminent. This is not a position to own -- it is optionality to rent.

**Tactical (24-48h): BUY at open if within 5% of C$0.45.** The Magiabe West Vein press release (123-137 g/t Au rock-chips) will drive a 15-25% retail-driven pop. Sell into any 20%+ move. Pass entirely if it gaps >15% at open.
