## The Setup

OXBC is a $1.1B asset community bank in Oakland County, Michigan with a hybrid model: traditional relationship banking plus a national asset-based lending subsidiary (OCF).

**What makes this interesting:**

The bank is operating at exceptional margins (5.23% NIM vs. 3.5% peers) while trading at a discount to book (0.89x TBV). This disconnect exists because:

1. It is an illiquid OTC stock (~$89M market cap)
2. NPAs rose 58% YoY, spooking credit-focused investors
3. OCF introduces complexity and risk that pure community bank investors dislike
4. The $170M temporary deposit surge in Q4 2025 creates balance sheet noise

**Structural forces:**

- Michigan community banking is consolidating. 181 deals in 2025, regulatory tailwinds, aging owner demographics.
- NIM troughs may be behind us as deposit betas prove asymmetric downward.
- Michigan's CRE exposure is less risky than national averages (underweight office, overweight industrial).

## Risks & Kill Switches

### What Invalidates the Thesis

1. **Credit blowup beyond the single relationship.** If NPAs continue rising across multiple borrowers, the thesis breaks. This would indicate systemic underwriting problems, not an isolated workout.

2. **Deposit franchise erosion.** Cost of funds rising above 2% would indicate the franchise advantage is fading. Monitor quarterly.

3. **OCF catastrophic loss.** A major fraud or single-name blowup in the national ABL book would consume earnings and distract management.

### What Causes Permanent Loss

- Material fraud (low probability given 140-year history and insider ownership)
- Michigan recession + auto industry collapse simultaneously triggering CRE losses
- Management making value-destructive acquisition instead of being acquired

### What Forces Re-evaluation

- NIM compression below 4.5% for two consecutive quarters
- NPAs rising above 3% of assets
- Thompson/Clemente selling stake
- Regulatory environment reversal (new restrictive merger policies)
