# 2CRSi SA (EPA: AL2SI) - Financial Analysis

## Key Finding: Working Capital-Driven Cash Trap With Margin Aspirations Far From Proven

**Core thesis:** 2CRSi's recent turnaround narrative (profitability, AI tailwinds, $610M contract) obscures a structurally challenged business with razor-thin margins, severe working capital volatility, and unproven execution on margin expansion targets. The stock trades on forward aspirations (12% EBITDA margin at EUR300M revenue) that have no historical precedent in this company's operations.

---

## Income Statement: Thin Margins, Volatile History

| Metric | FY 2022/23 | FY 2023/24 (12mo pro forma) | FY 2024/25 |
|--------|-----------|---------------------------|-----------|
| Revenue | ~EUR184M (incl. Boston) | EUR167.6M | EUR220.7M |
| EBITDA | Negative | EUR6.3M (3.8%) | EUR5.6M (2.5%) |
| Net Income | Losses | EUR2.4M (1.4%) | EUR2.1M (0.95%) |

**Critical observation:** EBITDA margin *declined* from 3.8% to 2.5% year-over-year despite 32% revenue growth. This is the opposite of operating leverage. Management attributed H2 margin compression to "an exceptional discount to one of its main customers in the US to compensate for a delivery delay" - a one-time item, but delivery execution risk is now a known issue.

**Cost structure (H1 2024/25):**
- Material costs: 59% of revenue (down from 80% in H1 2023, but still hardware-heavy)
- Personnel: 18.2% of revenue (down from 44% - significant rationalization)
- Operating leverage exists but is being offset by customer concessions/pricing pressure

**SBC:** Unable to locate specific stock-based compensation figures in public disclosures. This is a disclosure gap. French small-caps often have minimal SBC, but must verify in annual report footnotes.

## Cash Flow: The Real Story

**Negative free cash flow generation despite reported profitability.**

| Period | Operating Cash Flow | Cash Position (period end) |
|--------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| H1 2023/24 | -EUR5.3M | n/a |
| H1 2024/25 | +EUR3.0M | EUR1.2M |
| FY 2024/25 end (Jun 2025) | -EUR0.2M | ~EUR0M |
| Oct 2025 | n/a | EUR8.2M |

**Key insight:** FY 2024/25 ended with essentially zero cash (-EUR0.2M) due to "trade receivables still pending collection at the fiscal year-end." This normalized by October 2025 (EUR8.2M cash), but this reveals:

1. **Massive receivables volatility** - EUR42.7M in receivables vs. EUR220M revenue suggests DSO of ~70 days, which is manageable but lumpy collection patterns create liquidity risk
2. **Working capital is the swing factor** - Cash generation is entirely dependent on timing of customer payments
3. **No structural free cash flow buffer** exists at current margin levels

**Reconciliation (approximate FY 2024/25):**
- Net income: EUR2.1M
- Add: D&A (estimated EUR2-3M based on EBITDA to net income bridge)
- Less: Working capital build (large negative in H2)
- **Result: ~EUR0 operating cash flow for full year**

## Balance Sheet: Improved but Fragile

**As of H1 2024/25 (Dec 2024):**
- Cash: EUR1.2M
- Total debt: EUR11.1M (up from EUR9.9M YoY)
- Net debt: ~EUR10M
- Shareholders' equity: EUR27.4M
- Debt/Equity: 40-45% (manageable)
- Net debt/EBITDA: ~1.0x (conservative)

**Post-Boston context:** The Boston Limited sale (June 2023) was used to:
1. Repay EUR9M acquisition loan
2. Reduce consolidated debt from EUR54.6M to <EUR17M
3. Pay EUR0.20/share exceptional dividend

**Liquidity:** EUR8.2M cash + EUR4.8M credit lines = EUR13M available (as of Oct 2025). Adequate for current operations but no margin of safety for contract execution issues or working capital swings.

**State-guaranteed loans:** EUR4.7M of EUR5.3M bank borrowings are PGE (French state-guaranteed COVID loans) - favorable terms but eventual refinancing exposure.

## Normalized Earnings: What Can This Business Actually Earn?

**Management's FY 2025/26 targets:**
- Revenue: EUR300M+
- EBITDA margin: 12%+
- Implied EBITDA: EUR36M+

**Historical reality check:**
- Best achieved EBITDA margin (recent): 3.8% (FY23/24 proforma)
- Most recent full year: 2.5% (FY24/25)
- H1 2024/25 alone: 10% (but tiny revenue base of EUR20.9M)

**My normalized earnings estimate:**

## Red Flags Checklist

- [x] **Weak cash conversion** - Reported profits not consistently converting to cash; FY24/25 ended with zero cash despite EUR2.1M net income
- [x] **Margin targets with no precedent** - 12% EBITDA target would be 3x the best achieved margin in recent years
- [x] **Customer concentration risk** - Large US contract (disclosed $290M order) creates lumpy revenue and execution risk; discount already granted for delivery delay
- [x] **Working capital trap** - EUR42.7M receivables on EUR220M revenue; collection timing determines cash position
- [ ] Rising SBC - Unverified; disclosure appears limited
- [ ] Balance sheet fragility - Improved post-Boston sale; manageable but no cushion
- [x] **Aggressive forward guidance** - Revenue forecast of 7x H1 growth for H1 2025/26 is exceptional if achieved

## The $610M Contract: Real but Front-Loaded

**Structure:**
- $610M master contract with US data center operator (Jan 2024)
- First order: $290M for California AI Factory (Sep 2025)
- Delivery: Spring 2026
- Involves Nvidia hardware, WEKA storage

**What this means financially:**
- This is hardware pass-through revenue with likely single-digit gross margins on the equipment
- The "Cloud Solutions" subsidiary aims to capture recurring services revenue at higher margins
- But services revenue recognition will lag hardware delivery by 12-18+ months
- Current financials show **no evidence of meaningful services margin uplift yet**

## Key Financial Drivers (What Actually Matters)

### 1. Gross Margin on Major Contracts
The stock trades entirely on the assumption that 2CRSi can achieve 10%+ EBITDA margins. With material costs at 59-80% of revenue historically, gross margin expansion is the load-bearing variable. Every 100bps of gross margin improvement at EUR300M revenue = EUR3M EBITDA.

### 2. Working Capital Management / DSO
Cash generation is hostage to receivables collection. A bad quarter-end on collections can turn a profitable year into a liquidity event. The June 2025 year-end (zero cash) vs. October 2025 (EUR8.2M) illustrates this volatility.

### 3. Execution on US Contract
The $290M California order is ~130% of FY24/25 revenue. Delivery delays already caused one customer discount. Another execution issue could impair both margins and the customer relationship.

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**The non-obvious insight:** The FY24/25 EBITDA margin *declined* despite 32% revenue growth. This is hidden in the 16-month vs. 12-month comparison noise. On a clean 12-month basis:

- FY23/24: EUR6.3M EBITDA on EUR167.6M = 3.8%
- FY24/25: EUR5.6M EBITDA on EUR220.7M = 2.5%

**Why this matters:** 2CRSi gave away margin to grow revenue. The "exceptional discount" for delivery delay is symptomatic of a company stretching to win/retain business. If this pattern continues, the 12% margin target becomes mathematically implausible.

**The Street (3 analysts, all Buy, avg target EUR17.83) appears to be extrapolating:**
1. Revenue growth continues (reasonable given backlog)
2. Margins expand toward targets (no evidence yet)
3. Cash conversion improves (unproven)

The base case appears fully priced. The bear case is not in the price.

## Valuation Framework

**Current market cap:** ~EUR265M (at EUR12/share, 22.3M shares)

**What the market implies:**
- At EUR265M market cap and FY24/25 net income of EUR2.1M, implied P/E = 126x trailing
- For the stock to be "fair" at 20x normalized earnings, need EUR13M+ net income
- That requires ~EUR25M+ EBITDA, or 8%+ margins on EUR300M revenue

**What must be true for current price:**
1. Revenue reaches EUR300M+ (backlog supports this)
2. EBITDA margin expands from 2.5% to 8%+ (no evidence of this trajectory)
3. Working capital normalizes and cash conversion improves (contrary to recent trend)

**Risk/reward:**
- Upside case (EUR22 target): 12% margins, 15x forward EBITDA = EUR540M EV
- Downside case (EUR5-6): Margins stay at 3-4%, cash burn continues, multiple compresses to 10x EBITDA

The asymmetry favors skepticism until margin improvement is demonstrated in actual results.
