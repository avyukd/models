# 2CRSi SA (EPA: AL2SI) - Investment Memo

## Recommendation

- **Decision:** Neutral (Too Hard)
- **Conviction:** High (in passing)
- **Classification:** Too Hard - asymmetric downside, unverifiable execution risk, no variant edge

---

## Key Drivers (What Actually Moves This Stock)

1. **$610M contract execution** - The California AI factory order is ~130% of FY24/25 revenue. Delivery in Spring 2026. If it works, bull case. If it doesn't, repeat of Blade 2021.

2. **EBITDA margin expansion from 2.5% to 12%** - Management's target has zero historical precedent. EBITDA margin *declined* from 3.8% to 2.5% despite 32% revenue growth in FY24/25. The stock only works at 12% margins.

3. **GPU allocation** - 80% of revenue is NVIDIA-dependent. A small European OEM has no structural claim to supply priority. When supply normalizes (2026-2027), even this advantage disappears.

---

## Why This Is Too Hard

### The Math Problem

| Scenario | Revenue | EBITDA Margin | EBITDA | EV/EBITDA | Implied Price |
|----------|---------|---------------|--------|-----------|---------------|
| Bear (current run-rate) | EUR250M | 3% | EUR7.5M | 15x | ~EUR5 |
| Base (modest improvement) | EUR300M | 5% | EUR15M | 15x | ~EUR10 |
| Bull (mgmt target) | EUR300M | 12% | EUR36M | 15x | ~EUR24 |

Current price: EUR12. Trading at 126x trailing earnings.

**The stock requires the bull case to justify current price.** The bull case requires:
- 3-4x margin expansion from current levels
- Flawless execution on a contract larger than annual revenue
- No repeat of Blade (2021) or Boston (2019-2023) patterns

### What I Cannot Verify

1. **Customer creditworthiness** - The $610M contract customer is undisclosed. Cannot assess counterparty risk. Blade went bankrupt and nearly killed the company.

2. **Margin path** - No evidence of operating leverage. FY24/25 showed *negative* operating leverage (margins down despite revenue up). The "exceptional discount" for delivery delays suggests no pricing power.

3. **GPU allocation durability** - When NVIDIA supply normalizes in 2026-2027, what is 2CRSi's competitive position? No moat analysis identified structural barriers.

4. **Management learning curve** - Same CEO who bought Boston (value-destroyed), invested in Blade (wrote off), and diluted shareholders 65% since IPO is running this "turnaround."

## What Would Move This Out of Too Hard

1. **2+ quarters of demonstrated margin expansion** - Need to see 5%+ EBITDA margin sustained, not just guided.

2. **Customer disclosure or credit enhancement** - Who is the $610M customer? Letter of credit, prepayment, or identity disclosure would reduce counterparty risk.

3. **Successful Spring 2026 delivery** - Post-delivery, the execution risk is behind and services revenue begins.

4. **GPU supply normalization without margin collapse** - Need to see 2CRSi compete on something other than allocation access.

## Risk/Reward Summary

- **Upside:** 2x if bull case plays out (EUR24)
- **Downside:** 50-60% if bear case (EUR5-6)
- **Probability-weighted:** Unclear. Cannot handicap execution risk on an undisclosed customer with a CEO who has a pattern of overreach.

**The asymmetry is unfavorable.** Downside is concrete (margins stay at 3%, multiple compresses). Upside requires multiple unverifiable assumptions to compound correctly.

## Decision Rationale

This is not a "I don't understand it" pass. I understand the business. The problem is:

1. **No variant perception** - I cannot identify what the market is specifically wrong about. The bull case is consensus (contract execution + margin expansion). The bear case is historical pattern recognition. Neither gives me edge.

2. **Unverifiable key variable** - Customer identity and creditworthiness is the load-bearing assumption. Cannot underwrite.

3. **Management track record** - Two capital allocation failures (Blade, Boston) under current CEO with no governance check. Turnaround is 1 year old after 5 years of losses.

4. **Late cycle industry** - Even if company executes, the industry structure is unfavorable for small OEMs and showing late-cycle signs.

**Passing is the right decision.** The stock could double or halve from here, but I have no edge in predicting which.

---

## If Forced to Have a View

If I had to bet, I would lean **short** for the following reasons:

- EBITDA margin declined despite revenue growth (hidden in reporting)
- Management pattern suggests another concentration blow-up is base case, not bear case
- 126x trailing with zero cash conversion is not pricing in any execution risk
- Industry late cycle with structural margin pressure

But I would not short because:
- Retail/momentum flows in small-cap AI names can persist
- Contract announcements provide positive catalysts through Spring 2026
- 51% insider ownership limits float

**Net:** Neutral (Too Hard). Watch from sidelines. Re-evaluate post-Spring 2026 delivery.
