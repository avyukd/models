# AppLovin Corporation (APP) -- Financial Analysis

**Date**: February 8, 2026
**Stock Price**: ~$406 (as of Feb 7, 2026)
**Market Cap**: ~$138B
**Enterprise Value**: ~$140B (market cap + ~$3.5B debt - ~$1.7B cash)
**52-Week Range**: $200.50 - $745.61
**Shares Outstanding (diluted)**: ~341M

---

## Key Thesis Up Front

AppLovin is now a pure-play AI-driven advertising platform (post-divestiture of Apps business in mid-2025) generating extraordinary margins (80%+ adjusted EBITDA) on a ~$5.5B revenue base growing 40-70%+ YoY. The stock has pulled back ~45% from its December 2025 highs (~$734) to ~$406. The central question is: **At ~30x forward P/E on consensus 2026 EPS of ~$14.75, does this price adequately compensate for execution risk in TAM expansion (e-commerce), regulatory overhang (SEC investigation, short-seller reports), and the sustainability of 80%+ margins?**

### Revenue Quality Assessment

- **Post-divestiture, AppLovin is 100% advertising revenue.** The Apps business (~$1.5B in FY2024 revenue) was sold to Tripledot Studios for ~$400M cash + ~20% equity stake (total deal ~$800M) -- closed June 30, 2025.
- **Advertising revenue is performance-based**, meaning advertisers pay based on measurable outcomes (installs, ROAS). This is higher-quality than impression-based revenue because it directly ties to advertiser ROI. However, it is inherently cyclical -- ad budgets are among the first costs cut in downturns.
- **Revenue concentration risk**: AppLovin's core TAM is mobile gaming advertising. The e-commerce expansion (Axon Ads Manager, self-serve launching mid-2026) is the key growth narrative but is unproven at scale.
- **Continuing operations annualized run rate**: ~$5.5-5.7B based on Q2+Q3 2025, with Q4 guided to $1.57-1.60B. Consensus FY2025E revenue is ~$5.57B.
- **FY2026 consensus revenue**: ~$7.9B (per analyst estimates), implying ~42% YoY growth from continuing operations.

**Judgment**: Revenue quality is high within its niche (performance advertising), but the single-segment concentration and dependence on the mobile gaming ecosystem create fragility. The e-commerce TAM expansion is the consensus bull case, but it has zero revenue proof points yet.

The adjusted EBITDA margin of 82% is genuinely extraordinary, but it overstates economic earnings by ~$130M/quarter in SBC alone. GAAP net margins of ~60% from continuing operations are the more relevant measure of economic profitability. These are still world-class margins.

**Source**: Earnings press releases (Q4 2024, Q1+Q3 2025), MacroTrends (TTM FCF).

### Cash Conversion Assessment

- **CapEx is negligible.** AppLovin's software-based model requires almost no physical capital investment. Purchases of property and equipment are in the single-digit millions per quarter. This is a genuine asset-light business.
- **FCF conversion from GAAP net income is excellent.** TTM FCF of ~$3.36B vs. estimated TTM GAAP net income of ~$2.7B implies >100% cash conversion. The excess is primarily SBC (a non-cash charge added back to operating cash flow).
- **Working capital impact appears benign**, consistent with a platform business that collects from advertisers before paying publishers.

**Judgment**: Cash generation is the strongest element of the bull case. FCF margins of 60%+ on a post-divestiture, pure advertising business are among the highest in all of technology. The near-zero capex intensity means nearly all operating cash flow converts to free cash flow.

### Balance Sheet Assessment

- **Leverage is manageable.** Net debt / TTM EBITDA of ~0.4x is conservative for a business generating $3B+ in annual FCF. The absolute debt load of $3.5B is easily serviceable.
- **No near-term maturity wall.** Earliest maturity is 2029, giving ample runway.
- **Annual interest cost**: ~$190M (estimated: weighted average coupon ~5.4% on $3.55B). This is well-covered by ~$4.4B TTM EBITDA.
- **The revolving facility provides $1B of additional liquidity.**
- **Debt/equity ratio of 2.4x looks high** but is misleading given the low equity base (partly due to aggressive buybacks reducing equity). The proper leverage metric for a cash-generating software business is net debt/EBITDA, which is very low.

**Judgment**: Balance sheet is a non-issue. This company can easily service its debt and has significant financial flexibility.

### Net Dilution from SBC

- SBC creates shares; buybacks retire them. With ~$520M annualized SBC vs. buybacks running at $2B+/year, net share count is declining. This is a genuine positive -- the company is more than offsetting dilution.
- However, this analysis depends on the company continuing to allocate >$2B/year to buybacks at current elevated prices.

**Judgment**: Capital allocation has been shareholder-friendly. The key concern is whether buying back shares at 30x+ forward earnings is optimal vs. holding cash for M&A or organic reinvestment into e-commerce expansion.

## 6. Red Flags Checklist

- [ ] **Persistent negative free cash flow** -- NO. FCF is massively positive and growing.
- [x] **Rising SBC as % of revenue** -- MIXED. Declining on a full-year basis, but the October 2025 performance RSU grant ($410M fair value) signals potential future SBC inflation. Monitor closely.
- [ ] **Margin expansion driven by accounting choices** -- NO. Margins are genuinely expanding due to operating leverage in the advertising platform and divestiture of the lower-margin Apps business.
- [ ] **Weak cash conversion** -- NO. FCF > GAAP net income consistently.
- [ ] **Balance sheet fragility** -- NO. Manageable leverage, no near-term maturities.
- [x] **Aggressive revenue recognition** -- ELEVATED CONCERN. The Muddy Waters report (March 2025) alleged AppLovin systematically violates platform terms of service by extracting proprietary user IDs from Meta, Snap, TikTok, Reddit, and Google to construct unauthorized user profiles ("PIGs"). The SEC has opened an investigation into data-collection practices. If these allegations have merit, the revenue derived from this data advantage could be at risk. **This is the single largest tail risk to the business.**
- [ ] **Related party transactions** -- No material concerns identified.

### What the Current Price Implies

At $406/share and ~$138B market cap, the market is pricing in:
- **On 2026 consensus ($14.75 EPS)**: ~28x forward P/E. For a 40%+ revenue growth company with 80%+ margins, this is not obviously expensive if growth sustains.
- **On normalized mid-cycle ($11.54)**: ~35x P/E. This requires the business to maintain 80% EBITDA margins and grow to $7B+ in revenue -- both of which are plausible but not guaranteed.

### "What Must Be True" Analysis

For $406/share to be fairly valued at a 25x normalized P/E (generous for a high-growth, high-margin software business):
- Required normalized EPS: $406 / 25 = **$16.24**
- At 80% EBITDA margin, 7.5% SBC, 18% tax rate, this requires **~$10B in revenue**
- That implies ~80% revenue growth from FY2025E ($5.5B) over 2-3 years
- This is achievable only if the e-commerce expansion succeeds materially

For a more conservative 20x normalized P/E (appropriate if growth decelerates to 15-20%):
- Required normalized EPS: $406 / 20 = **$20.30**
- This requires ~$12-13B in revenue at current margins -- essentially tripling from FY2025E
- This is the bear case: the stock is expensive unless growth sustains above 30%+ for years

## 9. Key Financial Drivers

**The 1-3 metrics that actually drive this stock:**

1. **Advertising revenue growth rate** -- This is the single most important variable. The stock re-rates violently on revenue beats/misses because the market is pricing in sustained 30-50% growth. Any deceleration below 25% would likely compress the multiple from 28x to 20x forward, representing ~30% downside. Any acceleration above 50% (e-commerce working) could re-rate the stock back toward $600+.

2. **Adjusted EBITDA margin sustainability** -- The 80%+ margin is what makes the growth story investable. If margins compress to 70% (due to e-commerce mix, competitive pressure, or regulatory costs), normalized EPS drops ~25-30%. The market is pricing in margin stability or expansion.

3. **Regulatory/legal resolution** -- The SEC investigation and serial short-seller reports create a binary risk. If AppLovin's data practices are found to violate platform ToS and must change, the AXON engine's effectiveness could be impaired. This would hit both revenue growth and margins simultaneously.

## 10. What Consensus May Be Missing

**The non-obvious insight: The October 2025 performance RSU grant reveals management's compensation incentive structure at an inflection point.**

In October 2025, AppLovin granted 0.9M performance-based RSUs worth $410.5M at grant date (per Q3 2025 10-Q). This is a single grant worth nearly as much as the company's entire annual SBC run rate. The performance conditions were not disclosed in the search results but are likely tied to aggressive revenue or stock price targets.

**Why this matters**:
- If these vest, SBC will spike meaningfully above the "declining SBC as % of revenue" narrative that bulls cite
- The $410M grant at ~$456/share (implied grant price) signals management was highly confident in near-term performance -- but the stock is now trading below that price
- More importantly, this kind of large lumpy performance grant creates incentives for management to do whatever it takes to hit targets over the vesting window, which can include pulling revenue forward, pursuing aggressive accounting, or making dilutive acquisitions
- Analysts projecting declining SBC/revenue ratios likely have not sized this grant correctly

**Second observation**: The Apps business was sold for ~$800M total ($400M cash + equity) despite generating ~$1.5B in annual revenue. That is ~0.5x revenue for a gaming business -- suggesting the Apps portfolio had very low profitability or was deteriorating faster than headline revenue suggested. The "clean up the story" narrative is valid, but the fire-sale valuation deserves more scrutiny as a signal about the quality of AppLovin's legacy operations.

## Summary Assessment

**Financially strong, but fully priced for an optimistic scenario.**

- The advertising business is genuinely exceptional: 80%+ EBITDA margins, 60%+ FCF margins, near-zero capex, and rapid growth
- Balance sheet is clean and leverage is conservative
- Cash generation is among the best in technology
- At ~$406 (~28x 2026E EPS), the stock is not absurdly expensive for a 40%+ grower, but it requires sustained execution on e-commerce TAM expansion and resolution of regulatory/legal overhang
- The key risk is not financial -- it is that the AXON engine's effectiveness depends on data practices that are under active SEC scrutiny. If those practices must change, the entire margin and growth profile is at risk
- At current prices (down 45% from peak), the stock offers better risk/reward than at $700+, but a margin of safety only exists if you believe e-commerce advertising will work and the SEC investigation resolves benignly
