# Vodafone Group Plc (VOD) - Rigorous Financial Analysis

**Date:** January 2026
**Focus:** LEAP Options — Precision Required on Upside/Downside Cases

---

## Executive Summary

Vodafone is a structurally challenged European telecom undergoing significant portfolio rationalization. FY25 delivered Adjusted EBITDAaL of €11.0B and Adjusted FCF of €2.5B on a post-divestiture basis. The company has:

- Sold Italy (€7.9B) and Spain (€4.1B)
- Merged UK operations with Three (~51% Vodafone ownership)
- Cut dividend 50% to €0.045/share
- Reduced net debt from €33B to ~€22B
- Executed €2B+ in share buybacks (ongoing €2B more authorized)

**The investment question:** Is the current ~£23.5B market cap (GBP 0.98-1.00/share) pricing in terminal decline, or is stabilization underpriced?

---

## 1. Earnings Quality Analysis

### IFRS 16 Distortions

Vodafone's reported financials are heavily influenced by IFRS 16 lease accounting:

| Metric | FY25 | Notes |
|--------|------|-------|
| Lease liabilities on balance sheet | ~€15B | Towers, sites, property |
| Lease payments per year | €3.3B | Shows up in FCF, not EBITDA |
| Interest on leases | €451M | Included in finance costs |

**Key distortion:** Adjusted EBITDA of €11.0B includes ~€3.3B of lease expenses that are classified as financing activities under IFRS 16. True "cash EBITDA" after lease payments is closer to €7.7-8.0B.

### Spectrum Amortization

- Spectrum licenses amortized over 15-25 years
- FY25 spectrum purchases: €421M (down from €454M)
- Spectrum is a real cash cost that must be maintained for competitive positioning
- Treating it as "non-recurring" would overstate normalized earnings

### Operating Profit vs. Cash Reality

| Line Item | FY25 (€M) |
|-----------|-----------|
| Adjusted EBITDAaL | 11,000 |
| Less: Lease payments | (3,288) |
| Less: Capex | (6,862) |
| Less: Spectrum | (421) |
| **Operating Cash Generation** | **~429** |

This explains why "Free Cash Flow" of €1.85B requires additional adjustments and why the ~€11B EBITDA translates to only ~€2.5B Adjusted FCF.

## 3. Balance Sheet Analysis

### Net Debt Position

| Metric | FY25 | Post-Italy Sale |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| Gross debt | ~€45B | ~€38B |
| Cash and equivalents | ~€10B | ~€8B |
| **Net debt** | **€33B** | **~€22B** |
| Net debt/Adjusted EBITDAaL | 2.7x | ~2.0x |

The Italy sale proceeds (€7.9B) reduced leverage significantly. Target leverage range is 2.25-2.75x.

### Pension Liabilities

- UK defined benefit pension scheme is material
- Net pension deficit: ~€2-3B depending on discount rate assumptions
- Not a crisis but adds to leverage picture

### Hidden Assets

1. **65% stake in Vodacom (Africa):** Market cap ~€18B, Vodafone's stake worth ~€12B. Vodacom is acquiring more Safaricom (Kenya) - deal in progress.

2. **50% stake in VodafoneZiggo (Netherlands):** Estimated value €2B+. Liberty Global in discussions to acquire (ongoing since 2025).

3. **~10% residual stake in Vantage Towers:** Worth ~€1.3B at recent sale prices.

**Sum of parts analysis:**

| Asset | Value (€B) |
|-------|------------|
| Vodacom stake (65%) | ~12.0 |
| VodafoneZiggo (50%) | ~2.0 |
| Vantage Towers residual | ~1.3 |
| VodafoneThree UK (implied) | ~8-10 |
| Germany operations | ~10-12 |
| Other (Turkey, Other Europe) | ~3-5 |
| **Gross asset value** | **~36-42** |
| Less: Net debt | (~22) |

Current market cap is ~€23-24B (GBP ~£19-20B at current FX). The market is pricing close to the low end of sum-of-parts, implying skepticism about Germany and UK merger execution.

### What's Priced In?

At 4.2x EV/EBITDA, the market is pricing Vodafone as:

- Continued Germany weakness
- Value trap with history of disappointment

For context:

## 6. Key Drivers and Non-Consensus Insights

### The 3 Things That Actually Matter

1. **Germany trajectory:** 33% of revenue. FY25 was -5% service revenue. Q1 FY26 showed improvement to -3.2%. If this inflects positive in 2026, it validates the turnaround.

2. **UK merger synergies:** GBP 700M/year target by Year 5. The market will focus on early integration signals and whether management raises or lowers expectations.

3. **Capital return execution:** €4B buyback program at current depressed prices is materially accretive. If shares are retired at €1.00-1.20 and fair value is higher, this is genuine value creation.

### What Consensus Gets Wrong

**The market prices Germany as terminal decline.** But:
- MDU TV loss was a one-time regulatory shock, now absorbed
- 1&1 roaming agreement provides incremental revenue
- New management and cost cuts are taking effect
- Stabilization (not growth) would rerate the stock

**The market underestimates buyback power.** At current prices:
- €2B buyback = ~8% of market cap retired
- If intrinsic value is 50% higher, each buyback is highly accretive
- This is mechanical value creation if executed at current levels

4. VodafoneZiggo or Vodacom stake monetized at premium

## 8. Non-Consensus View Summary

**The market is pricing Vodafone for continued failure. The variant perception is:**

The combination of:
- A cleaned-up balance sheet (2.0x leverage)
- Portfolio simplification (exited value traps)
- Buybacks at depressed prices (€4B at ~4x EBITDA)
- A consolidated UK position (scale finally achieved)

...creates a setup where base case stabilization delivers meaningful returns, and upside scenarios are underpriced.

**The counterargument (which has merit):**
- Vodafone has destroyed value for 20 years
- Management has negligible skin in the game
- Germany is structurally disadvantaged vs. Deutsche Telekom
- "This time is different" rarely is

**For LEAP positioning:** The asymmetry favors long exposure with defined risk. The stock is pricing in significant probability of continued failure. If failure doesn't materialize, the rerating is significant. If it does, the downside is ~25-35%, not terminal.
