## 2. Key Drivers (The 1-3 Things That Matter)

**1. Noida Execution (THE swing factor):** CHF 750M committed (~15% of market cap) to a greenfield airport in India. Already delayed. No bilateral investment treaty protection. Management has quietly walked back EBITDA expectations from "CHF 10M contribution" to "neutral in year one." This single bet will determine whether international expansion was value-creating or empire-building. The market has not sized this risk correctly.

**2. Zurich Capacity Ceiling:** The core asset is a cash machine (55% EBITDA margin, 2051 concession). But Zurich has the strictest night curfew among European hubs (23:30-06:00) and German airspace restrictions further constrain operations. Volume growth is capped by politics, not demand. The market prices continued growth; the physical capacity ceiling is closer than consensus assumes.

**3. International Segment Opacity:** CHF 1B+ deployed into EM concessions with zero disclosure of segment-level ROIC. Chile already impaired (CHF 7.8M). IFRIC 12 accounting masks true operational performance. Investors cannot verify whether this capital is earning its cost of capital. This is not a data gap - it is a governance gap.

---

## 3. Thesis Summary

- **High-quality core asset at full valuation:** Zurich Airport is a monopoly infrastructure asset with a 55% EBITDA margin, 1.6x leverage, and a 2051 concession. Normalized earnings power of CHF 330M. FCF will normalize higher once Noida construction ends. None of this is mispriced at 12x EV/EBITDA and 24x P/E.

- **Noida is a binary bet the market has not correctly sized:** CHF 750M into a greenfield Indian airport without BIT protection. Delays have occurred. Management expectations have deteriorated. If Noida fails to earn its cost of capital, it represents 2.3x annual normalized FCF that could have been returned to shareholders.

- **International segment returns are unknowable:** The company does not disclose segment EBITDA or ROIC. The Iquique impairment demonstrates execution risk is real. Management continues acquiring assets (Natal, 2024) while existing investments have not proven out. This pattern is concerning.

- **Capacity constraints cut both ways:** The structural capacity ceiling at Zurich (strictest curfews in Europe) limits upside optionality. Meanwhile, Schiphol's forced shrinkage is a modest tailwind for European hub competition but does not directly flow to Zurich given its own constraints.

- **The Circle real estate is genuine but not enough:** CHF 197M in stable rental income at 90%+ occupancy provides counter-cyclical ballast. This is underappreciated. But it is not sufficient to justify a position when the core question (international returns) is unanswerable.

## 5. The Setup

**What You Are Buying:**
- A monopoly on Switzerland's primary international airport through 2051
- A 55% EBITDA margin infrastructure asset with 1.6x leverage
- CHF 197M of stable real estate income from The Circle development
- A 2.2% dividend yield with room to grow as Noida capex normalizes
- A concentrated bet on India delivering acceptable returns

**Structural Forces:**
- European airport capacity is structurally constrained (Schiphol forced shrinkage is the leading indicator)
- SWISS/Lufthansa dependency creates mutual lock-in (stable but limits pricing power)
- Canton of Zurich's 33% statutory holding provides implicit government backing but reduces market discipline
- International expansion has consumed CHF 1B+ with no verifiable returns

**Why This Is Too Hard:**
1. Cannot verify international segment returns - management does not disclose
2. Cannot size Noida risk - greenfield execution with INR/CHF currency exposure and no BIT protection
3. Cannot determine if international capital would be better returned to shareholders
4. Valuation (12x EV/EBITDA) leaves no margin of safety for execution risk

## 9. Positioning & Portfolio Logic

**Why No Position:**

This is a high-quality business at a fair price with a concentrated, unknowable risk embedded in it. The quality is priced. The risk is not sized.

The variant perception opportunity (market misprices India vs Brazil risk, market underappreciates capacity ceiling) does not translate into an actionable trade because:
1. The valuation provides no margin of safety for being wrong
2. The information necessary to underwrite Noida (segment ROIC, construction updates, traffic projections) is not available
3. The asymmetry is unfavorable - upside requires Noida success + no further curfew restrictions + continued margin stability; downside just requires any one of those to disappoint

**What Would Change This:**

A 20-30% lower entry point would compensate for the unknowable international risk. At CHF 180-200, you are paying 9-10x EV/EBITDA for the Zurich core and getting international optionality for free. That is interesting. At CHF 256, you are paying full freight for both.

Alternatively, clear evidence that Noida is working (opening on time, traffic ramp exceeding projections, management providing segment ROIC disclosure) would de-risk the thesis and make the current valuation justifiable.

---

## 10. Final Assessment

**This Is Too Hard Because:**

1. **Cannot verify the key variable:** International segment ROIC is not disclosed. After CHF 1B+ invested, shareholders cannot determine whether this capital is earning its cost.

2. **The bet that matters is binary and unknowable:** Noida is 70% of international capex. It will either prove international expansion was smart or prove it was a mistake. We cannot handicap this from available information.

3. **No margin of safety:** At 12x EV/EBITDA, the stock prices in continued operational excellence. Any disappointment on Noida, curfew restrictions, or Lufthansa strategy creates downside without corresponding upside for positive surprises.

4. **Management behavior is concerning:** Acquiring new assets (Natal, 2024) while existing investments have not proven out. Walking back Noida expectations. Never disclosing segment returns. This pattern suggests empire-building may be prioritized over returns.

**The Right Trade Is No Trade:**

Watch Noida. Wait for a better entry point or better information. Passing is the correct decision when the thesis requires assumptions you cannot verify.
