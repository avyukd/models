# MDRX (Veradigm) — Rigorous Financial Analysis

**Stock Price:** ~$4 (OTC, delisted from NASDAQ Feb 2024)
**Market Cap:** ~$620M (est. ~155M shares outstanding)

---

## Key Driver: The Restatement Overhang Is the Entire Story

Everything about MDRX's financial picture flows from the accounting restatement. Revenue was overstated by ~$40M across FY2020-2021. The company has been unable to file timely financials since late 2023, leading to NASDAQ delisting in Feb 2024. Shares now trade OTC.

---

## Revenue Trajectory (Declining)

| Metric | FY2022 (Restated) | FY2023 (Est.) | FY2024 (Est.) | FY2025 (Est.) |
|--------|-------------------|---------------|---------------|---------------|
| GAAP Revenue | ~$588M | $620-625M* | $583-588M | ~Flat to 2024 |
| Adj. EBITDA | N/A | $139-144M | $85-90M | TBD |
| GAAP Net Income | Negative (restated) | $62-65M | ($46)-($49)M | TBD |

*FY2023 revenue includes ~$19M of favorable one-time customer settlements. Organic revenue was ~$600-606M.

**Critical observation:** FY2024 Adj. EBITDA collapsed from ~$140M to ~$87M despite only ~6% revenue decline. This implies massive operating deleverage or significant cost additions (restatement-related advisory, legal, investigation costs eating into earnings). The GAAP net loss of $46-49M in FY2024 vs. net income of $62-65M in FY2023 is a ~$110M swing.

## Balance Sheet — Net Cash but Deteriorating

| Metric | Dec 2024 | Dec 2025 |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Cash & Equivalents | $295M | $120M |
| Debt | $208M | $75M |

The $175M cash decline in 2025 was primarily from repurchasing convertible notes and issuing new senior secured debt ($75M term loan, maturing June 2030). Net cash dropped from $87M to ~$45M. Cash burn from operations + restatement costs is consuming liquidity.

---

## Normalized Earnings Power

Stripping out restatement noise, the business should generate:
- Revenue: ~$585M (flat, with provider attrition offset by data/analytics)
- Adj. EBITDA: $85-100M (14-17% margin) — but this is AFTER excluding restatement costs, SBC, and transaction expenses
- True FCF: Likely $40-60M normalized, depending on capex ($30-40M) and working capital
- **EV/EBITDA on normalized: ~6.4-6.8x** (EV ~$575M with ~$45M net cash) — cheap, pricing in significant permanent impairment
- **FCF yield: 6.5-10%** on normalized free cash flow — attractive if the business stabilizes

---

## What Consensus Gets Wrong

1. **At $4, the market is pricing in permanent impairment or further deterioration.** At 6.4-6.8x EBITDA, the stock trades like a melting ice cube. If attrition stabilizes and restatement costs roll off, there's significant upside. The FY2022 10-K was filed March 2025. Relisting expected 2026.

2. **EBITDA quality is poor — but the restatement costs ARE temporary.** The gap between Adj. EBITDA ($85-90M) and GAAP net loss ($46-49M) in FY2024 is enormous. Transaction/restatement costs are treated as "non-recurring" but have persisted for 3+ years. However, once filings are current, these costs genuinely go away.

3. **Revenue is leaking but the valuation now reflects it.** Customer attrition among large physician groups drove FY2024's revenue miss. FY2025 guided flat. At $4, flat revenue + cost normalization = meaningful upside.

## Red Flags

- $40M revenue overstatement requiring multi-year restatement
- SEC investigation (ongoing)
- Class action lawsuits (Hagens Berman et al.)
- Poison pill / stockholder rights plan extended through Dec 2026
- Strategic alternatives process concluded Jan 2025 with NO deal — 30+ parties, 5 bids, zero final proposals
- Interim CEO and interim CFO (Tom Langan, Lee Westerfield) — no permanent leadership
