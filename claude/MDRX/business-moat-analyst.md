# MDRX (Veradigm) — Business Moat Analysis

**Verdict: Narrow moat, actively eroding.**

---

## The 1-3 Things That Matter

### 1. EHR Switching Costs Are Real but Weakening

Veradigm's ~3.6% ambulatory EHR market share (small/mid practices via Practice Fusion and Veradigm EHR) benefits from traditional EHR stickiness: data migration pain, workflow retraining, IT integration costs. ~80% of provider segment revenue is recurring (maintenance/subscription).

**But:** TEFCA (Trusted Exchange Framework) has exchanged 500M health records as of early 2026 vs. 10M in Jan 2025. Interoperability mandates are structurally reducing EHR switching costs over time. Epic dominates large systems; Oracle Health (Cerner) and athenahealth ($17B PE buyout) are investing heavily. Veradigm is losing large physician groups.

### 2. The Data Network Is the Real Asset (But Vulnerable)

Veradigm's differentiated value is its health data network: EHR-connected data flowing to life sciences companies, payers, and real-world evidence (RWE) researchers. This is a flywheel: more providers → more data → more attractive to pharma/payers → more revenue to reinvest in provider tools.

**Problem:** The provider base is shrinking (customer attrition in 2024), which directly degrades the data asset. IQVIA (33% health analytics market share), Optum (25%), and HealthVerity/Datavant are formidable competitors with larger data sets and deeper pockets.

### 3. Governance Chaos Has Broken the Flywheel

The restatement, delisting, SEC investigation, failed strategic review, interim management, and poison pill have:
- Distracted management from product investment
- Spooked customers (especially large groups evaluating alternatives)
- Made M&A impossible (no audited financials = no deal)
- Frozen the data flywheel (can't invest in growth while fighting fires)

## Pricing Power: Weak

- Provider segment: Commoditizing; small practices are price-sensitive
- Data/analytics segment: Competitive market; Veradigm is a price-taker vs. IQVIA/Optum
- Revenue declining despite growing industry (healthcare IT CAGR ~14%)

---

## Non-Consensus View

**The moat bull case is that Veradigm's EHR-connected data pipeline is unique and undervalued.** Unlike IQVIA (primarily claims data), Veradigm has direct clinical data from EHR workflows. This is higher-quality data for RWE studies.

**The problem is that this asset requires a functioning, trusted company to monetize it.** Right now, the governance crisis is causing customer attrition that directly shrinks the data asset. The moat is real but melting.
