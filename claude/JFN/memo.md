## 2. Key Drivers (The 1-3 Things That Actually Matter)

**1. Chinese Tourism Recovery (The Optionality Nobody Is Pricing)**

Jungfraujoch is at record visitor levels (1.06M in 2024) with Chinese tourism still at 50% of 2019 levels. This is not a "recovered" business - this is a business running at normalized levels with its largest historical growth driver sitting at half capacity. When Chinese outbound tourism normalizes (projected 2026-2027), there is CHF 20-25M of incremental EBITDA waiting to materialize.

**2. Valuation Dislocation vs. True Comparables**

The market prices JFN as a tourism stock (12.7x EV/EBITDA). The asset is a perpetual geographic monopoly on irreplaceable infrastructure - closer to a toll road (20-25x EV/EBITDA) than a theme park (8-12x). The discount reflects tourism volatility, but COVID stress-tested the business and it survived without equity issuance. The risk premium is too high.

**3. Experience Mountains Segment - The Hidden Margin Driver**

This 19% of revenue segment runs at 65% EBITDA margins and grew 22% in 2024. As this segment scales, it structurally lifts group margins above historical levels. Analysts focused on Jungfraujoch visitor counts are missing the mix shift.

## 3. Thesis in 3-5 Bullets

1. **Own a perpetual monopoly at a tourism multiple.** JFN trades at 12.7x EV/EBITDA while comparable infrastructure monopolies trade at 15-25x. The asset cannot be replicated (tunnel through the Eiger took 16 years to build). Fair value is 15-18x.

2. **Chinese tourism recovery is optionality the market forgot.** Record volumes achieved with China at 50% of 2019. Full normalization adds CHF 20-25M EBITDA, or 15-20% upside to current earnings.

## 5. The Setup (Only What Matters)

**What JFN Is:** The only railway to Jungfraujoch ("Top of Europe"), Europe's highest railway station at 3,454 meters. The tunnel through the Eiger took 16 years to build (1896-1912) and cannot be replicated at any price.

**Why This Matters Now:** The business has recovered to record volumes but the market prices it as "normalized" when it is actually running below potential. Chinese tourism is the lagging recovery story. The multiple discount to infrastructure comps persists despite demonstrated COVID resilience.

**Structural Forces:**
- Asian long-haul tourism structural growth (India, Southeast Asia)
- Bucket-list tourism post-COVID demand acceleration
- No capacity expansion from competitors (impossible to build alternative)
- CHF 470M Eiger Express investment (completed 2020) modernized the experience
