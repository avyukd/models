## 1. Recommendation

- **Decision: NEUTRAL (Too Early)**

- **Time Horizon: Re-evaluate in 6-9 months**

This is a quality franchise in a cyclical trough, priced for a recovery that is 12-18 months from validation. The stock has tripled off its lows and now trades at 36x trough earnings. The variant perception required to be long here -- that GaN datacenter power adoption will be larger and faster than consensus expects -- is plausible but unverifiable today. The risk/reward is symmetric at best, skewed negative at worst. Pass for now; revisit when order data confirms the inflection.

## 2. Key Drivers (The 3 Things That Matter)

**1. Order intake trajectory.** Orders lead revenue by 2-4 quarters. Q4/2025 orders of EUR 170M (+37% q/q) are the most important signal in the latest release. This must sustain and accelerate to EUR 600M+ annualized to justify the current price. If orders stall at EUR 500-550M, the stock de-rates.

**2. GaN datacenter power timing.** Nvidia's 800V HVDC architecture (targeted 2027) would create a greenfield MOCVD equipment demand wave. Aixtron has 70-90% GaN MOCVD share. This is the optionality the market is paying for. It is real but 18-24 months away from generating meaningful revenue.

**3. Chinese competitive/geopolitical dynamics.** AMEC destroyed Aixtron's LED MOCVD franchise in 2 years (2016-2018). The same playbook could extend to power GaN/SiC if compound semiconductor equipment becomes a Chinese policy priority. Simultaneously, EU export controls could restrict China sales (35-50% of revenue). These risks are coupled: restrictions that limit Aixtron's China access would accelerate domestic Chinese tool development.

## 3. Thesis Summary

- Aixtron owns the dominant MOCVD franchise (70-90% share in power/optoelectronics) with physics-driven switching costs from recipe qualification lock-in. The moat is real but application-specific.
- The business is mid-downturn: three consecutive years of revenue decline, EBIT margins compressed 700bps from peak, FY2026 guided flat-to-down. This is not yet trough.
- At EUR 27.65 (36x trailing, 42x forward), the stock prices in EUR 700M+ revenue and 25%+ EBIT margins by FY2027/28 -- a recovery that requires GaN datacenter adoption to layer on top of SiC stabilization.
- The balance sheet (EUR 200M+ net cash, no debt) provides downside protection and optionality. No near-term financial distress risk.
- The Q4 order uptick is encouraging but one quarter does not make a trend. The SiC equipment trough is likely 2026-2027 per Yole, meaning near-term fundamentals remain under pressure.

## 5. The Setup

Aixtron is the dominant compound semiconductor MOCVD equipment maker, operating in a tight oligopoly with Veeco. The business earns excess returns from recipe qualification switching costs -- once a fab qualifies an epitaxial process on an Aixtron tool, switching requires months of re-engineering. This lock-in is genuine but application-specific (strongest in power/laser, weakest in commodity LED where AMEC already won).

The industry is in a bifurcated cycle: SiC equipment demand is in a post-capex-boom correction (utilization at 50-70%), GaN power is pre-inflection (first commercial datacenter rollouts ~2027), and optoelectronics is in early recovery (AI-driven optical interconnects). Aixtron sits at the intersection of all three but is currently weighted toward the weakest segment (SiC).

Geopolitical risk is moderate. MOCVD is not currently enumerated in EU dual-use controls, unlike lithography and etch. But the distinction between "strategic" and "non-strategic" semiconductor equipment is political. GaN has direct military applications (radar systems), and the 2016 CFIUS block established that the US views Aixtron's technology as sensitive. Progressive tightening of German export licenses is the base case risk, not a binary ban.

## 7. Forward Path and Catalysts

- **Near-term (0-6 months)**: Q1/Q2 2026 order data. Need to see sustained EUR 150M+ quarterly orders to confirm the Q4 uptick was real. FY2026 guidance of EUR 520M +/-30M provides a low bar but no positive catalyst.
- **Medium-term (6-18 months)**: Nvidia/hyperscaler GaN power architecture announcements. First commercial 800V HVDC datacenter deployments. SiC utilization recovery above 80%.
- **Negative catalysts**: Orders reverting below EUR 130M/quarter. EU adding MOCVD to dual-use controls. AMEC announcing a competitive power GaN MOCVD platform. Further SiC oversupply from Chinese substrate expansion.

## 8. Risks and Kill Switches

- **Thesis invalidation**: Orders declining below EUR 120M/quarter for two consecutive quarters would signal structural demand impairment, not just cyclical weakness.
- **Permanent capital loss risk**: Low given fortress balance sheet. Even in a severe downturn, EUR 200M net cash provides 2+ years of runway at breakeven operations.
- **Chinese competition extending to power MOCVD**: This is the structural risk the market underweights. Monitor AMEC's product roadmap and Chinese government compound semiconductor equipment policy.
- **Export control expansion**: If EU adds MOCVD to restricted categories, 15-30% revenue impairment in 12-24 months.
- **GaN adoption delay**: If datacenter HVDC pushes to 2029+, the bridge from SiC trough to GaN recovery becomes too long and the stock de-rates to mid-cycle multiples on lower mid-cycle earnings.

## 9. Positioning and Portfolio Logic

**No position today.** The stock is priced for a recovery that requires multiple things to go right over 18-24 months: SiC stabilization, GaN datacenter adoption on schedule, no competitive disruption from China, and no export control expansion. At 36-42x earnings with flat near-term fundamentals, the margin of safety is insufficient.

**What would change this to a Buy:**
- Price correction to EUR 18-22 (25-30x trough earnings), providing a margin of safety for cycle timing uncertainty
- Two consecutive quarters of orders above EUR 160M, confirming demand inflection
- A major hyperscaler commitment to GaN-based power architecture with named MOCVD equipment suppliers
- Any combination of the above

**What would make this a Short:**
- Orders deteriorating below EUR 120M/quarter
- Evidence of AMEC/NAURA entering power MOCVD competitively
- EU export control expansion to compound semiconductor equipment

This is a high-quality cyclical franchise that the market has already repriced for recovery. The edge is in timing, and the timing is not yet clear. Revisit in Q3 2026 with two more quarters of order data.
