# AIXTRON SE (AIXA) -- Financial Analysis
**Date: 2026-02-27 | Price: EUR 27.65 | Mkt Cap: EUR 3.09B | Shares: ~112M**

## Income Statement Trajectory

| Metric | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 | FY2026E (Guidance) |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|-----------|
| Revenue (EUR M) | 629.9 | 633.2 | 556.6 | ~530-560 (flat/slightly down) |
| Gross Margin | 44% | ~41% | 40% | ~40% est. |
| EBIT (EUR M) | 156.8 | 131.2 | 100.3 | ~100 est. |
| EBIT Margin | 25% | 21% | 18% | ~18% est. |
| Net Income (EUR M) | 145.2 | 106.2 | 85.3 | ~75 est. |
| EPS (EUR) | 1.29 | 0.94 | 0.76 | 0.66 (consensus) |

*Sources: FY2023/2024 Annual Reports, FY2025 earnings release (Feb 26, 2026), consensus per MarketScreener/TipRanks.*

**Key observation**: Revenue peaked in FY2023 and has declined ~12% cumulatively through FY2025. EBIT margins have compressed 700bps from peak (25% to 18%). This is a cyclical downturn -- power electronics (SiC) investment slowed as EV adoption decelerated, and the LED/optoelectronics cycle was weak until late 2025. The company is now guiding flat-to-down for FY2026, meaning three consecutive years of stagnation at best.

## Cash Flow & Normalized Earnings

| Metric | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Operating CF (EUR M) | ~28 (est.) | ~28 (est.) | 208.4 |
| Capex (EUR M) | ~138 | ~100 | ~40 (est.) |
| Free Cash Flow (EUR M) | -109.7 | -72.4 | ~168 (est.) |

*Sources: FY2024 Annual Report, Q3/2025 quarterly report, FY2025 earnings release.*

FY2023 and FY2024 had deeply negative FCF due to construction of the Innovation Center and acquisition of an Italian production site. FY2025 saw a massive swing as: (1) capex normalized dramatically (facility buildout completed), and (2) working capital unwound ~EUR 100M. The FY2025 OCF of EUR 208M is inflated by the working capital release -- this is not sustainable.

**Normalized FCF Bridge (FY2025 as base):**
- Net Income: EUR 85.3M
- Add back: D&A (est. ~EUR 30M based on capex history)
- Less: SBC (not disclosed separately -- a data gap; likely EUR 10-15M based on German semi-cap peers)
- Less: Maintenance capex (est. EUR 25-30M, normalized after buildout)
- Working capital: normalize to zero
- **Normalized FCF: ~EUR 70-80M**
- **Normalized FCF per share: ~EUR 0.63-0.71**

**Note**: SBC is a meaningful data gap. Aixtron does not prominently disclose SBC in its press releases. The 2024 Annual Report would contain this in notes to the financial statements but was not accessible for this analysis. This is a tractability limitation.

## Valuation Framework

**Current multiples (on FY2025 actuals):**
- P/E: 27.65 / 0.76 = 36x
- EV/EBIT: (3,090 - ~200 net cash) / 100 = ~29x
- P/Normalized FCF: 27.65 / 0.67 = ~41x

**On consensus FY2026E (EUR 0.66 EPS):**
- P/E: 42x

**What the market is pricing**: At EUR 27.65, after tripling from the EUR 8.45 low, the stock is pricing in a strong recovery. A 36x trailing P/E on trough-ish earnings implies the market expects: (a) revenue returning above EUR 700M+ by FY2027/28, (b) EBIT margins expanding back toward 25%+, and (c) the GaN/AI narrative materializing.

**Kerrisdale's thesis** (published Nov 2025) targets EUR 1B revenue and ~EUR 2.00 EPS by 2028, driven by Nvidia's 800V datacenter power architecture requiring GaN power semiconductors. At 20x that EPS, you get EUR 40. At 25x, EUR 50. Their thesis requires revenue to nearly double in 3 years.

**Reasonableness check**: Aixtron's prior peak was EUR 630M (FY2024). Getting to EUR 1B requires a 60% increase, which would require a new end-market cycle (GaN for datacenters) to layer on top of recovering SiC/power electronics. Possible but highly uncertain -- this is a bet on a product cycle that hasn't started yet.

## Red Flags Checklist

- [x] **Persistent negative FCF**: FY2023-2024 were negative, but driven by identifiable facility investment. FY2025 reverted to strong positive. Moderate concern resolved.
- [ ] Rising SBC: Insufficient disclosure to assess definitively.
- [x] **Margin compression**: 700bps EBIT margin decline from peak. Driven by volume deleverage and mix, not accounting. Cyclical, not structural.
- [ ] Weak cash conversion: FY2025 showed strong conversion once capex normalized.
- [ ] Balance sheet fragility: No -- fortress.
- [ ] Aggressive revenue recognition: No evidence.
- [ ] Related party transactions: No evidence.

## Key Financial Drivers

1. **Order intake trajectory** -- This is the load-bearing variable. Orders lead revenue by 2-4 quarters. The Q4/2025 uptick to EUR 170M is the critical inflection signal. If orders accelerate to EUR 600M+ in FY2026, the stock works. If they stall at EUR 500-550M, it doesn't.

2. **GaN adoption timeline for AI/datacenter power** -- This is the optionality the market is paying for. Nvidia's 800V power architecture targeted for 2027 would create a new demand wave for GaN epitaxy equipment. Aixtron has 70-90% MOCVD market share. The timing and magnitude are highly uncertain.

3. **Gross margin recovery** -- 40% gross margins in FY2025 vs. 44% in FY2023. Volume recovery with operating leverage could restore margins, but product mix (more GaN vs. SiC) matters.

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**The bear case is underappreciated at this price.** The stock has tripled off its lows, pricing in an AI/GaN cycle that won't generate meaningful revenue until 2027-2028 at earliest. But the near-term reality is stark: FY2026 guidance is flat-to-down, consensus EPS is EUR 0.66 (below FY2025), and the company is guiding for its third year of revenue stagnation.

**The non-obvious financial insight**: FY2025's EUR 208M operating cash flow is being celebrated, but ~EUR 100M of it was a one-time working capital release as the company shrank. Normalize for that, and underlying cash generation is ~EUR 100M OCF, or ~EUR 70-80M FCF. At EUR 3.09B market cap, that's a 2.3-2.6% FCF yield on a business with flat near-term revenue. You're paying a premium for a story that is 18-24 months from validation.

**The bull case requires**: (1) GaN datacenter adoption on schedule, (2) Aixtron maintaining dominant share in a greenfield market, and (3) no competitive response from Veeco or emerging Chinese players. All three must go right to justify current multiples.

**Verdict**: Financially solid company with a clean balance sheet, currently priced for a growth cycle that hasn't started. At 36x trough earnings with flat guidance, the risk/reward is skewed to the downside unless you have high conviction on the 2027+ GaN catalyst.
