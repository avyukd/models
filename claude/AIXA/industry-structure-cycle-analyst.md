# MOCVD / Compound Semiconductor Equipment: Industry Cycle Analysis

## Current Cycle Position: Early-to-Mid Downturn in SiC, Pre-Cycle in GaN/Optoelectronics

The MOCVD equipment market is not one cycle -- it is three overlapping cycles at different stages, and conflating them is the consensus mistake.

**SiC power electronics (historically ~40-50% of Aixtron-relevant demand):** Deep in a post-capex-boom correction. Per Yole Group, SiC equipment capex peaked at ~$3B in 2023 and is declining at a -7% CAGR through 2030, with the trough expected in 2027-2028. Upstream utilization has collapsed to ~50%, device line utilization to ~70%. This is a classic capex overshoot: the 2019-2024 investment wave built capacity far ahead of demand. Chinese entrants (SICC, TanKeBlue) grew from 10% to 40% of SiC substrate market share in four years, adding uncoordinated supply. Wolfspeed burned through -$1.98B in free cash flow in FY2025 building fabs into this oversupply.

**GaN power electronics (~15-20% of demand):** Pre-inflection. The GaN device market is growing at 42% CAGR from $355M in 2024 toward $3B by 2030 per Yole, but the equipment spend cycle has not yet begun in earnest. Data center HVDC adoption (the key catalyst) has first commercial rollouts around 2027. TrendForce forecasts SiC/GaN adoption in data center power at 17% by 2026 and 30%+ by 2030. This is real secular demand but equipment orders are 12-18 months away from inflection.

**Optoelectronics / micro-LED / laser (~30-40% of demand):** Early cycle. Micro-LED is entering the "make or break" phase with first production lines at AUO ramping. Apple micro-LED products expected in 2026. Equipment demand here is nascent but potentially large. AI-driven data center communications (optical interconnects, VCSELs) drove Aixtron's strongest segment in 2025 with 22% Q2 revenue growth.

## Industry Supply/Demand Dynamics

The MOCVD equipment market is a ~$700M-$1B market (estimates vary) dominated by a tight oligopoly: Veeco (~28% share), Aixtron (~24%), and AMEC (rising Chinese player). This concentration normally supports pricing discipline, but the current cycle is unusual because:

1. **Demand is bifurcated.** SiC customers are pausing or stretching orders while optoelectronics customers are ramping. Total industry orders are declining but the mix is shifting, not collapsing uniformly.

2. **Chinese localization pressure is structural.** AMEC and domestic Chinese MOCVD suppliers are gaining share in China, the largest end-market. This is not cyclical -- it is a permanent loss of addressable market for Western equipment makers in commodity LED and standard power applications.

3. **Equipment capex is a derivative of device demand.** Device makers order MOCVD tools 12-24 months ahead of expected demand. With SiC device utilization at 70% and falling, there is no incentive to order new epitaxy equipment until utilization recovers above 80-85%.

## What Drives Profitability in This Industry

MOCVD equipment economics are driven by: (1) end-market capex cycles (not device revenue -- equipment spending is lumpy and front-loaded), (2) technology transitions that force tool upgrades (e.g., 150mm to 200mm SiC, new GaN-on-Si platforms), and (3) geographic mix (China orders carry lower margins due to competitive pressure from AMEC).

Industry gross margins range from 38-48% across cycles, with EBIT margins from 12-25%. Mid-cycle economics for the leading MOCVD vendors: ~42-44% gross margin, ~20% EBIT margin, on relatively stable revenue of $550-650M (for a market leader). We are currently below mid-cycle on volume and margins.

## Cyclicality Assessment: High

This is an equipment business selling into capex cycles of semiconductor fabs. Revenue can swing 30-40% peak-to-trough. Aixtron's own trajectory illustrates this: revenue fell 12% in 2025 to EUR 556.6M, and 2026 guidance is EUR 520M (+/-30M), another ~7% decline. Order backlog fell 25% YoY to EUR 257.8M. Order intake declined from EUR 596M (2024) to EUR 544M (2025). These are mid-downturn numbers, not trough.

Historical pattern: MOCVD equipment revenues tend to trough 2-3 years after device capex peaks. If SiC capex peaked in 2023, the equipment revenue trough is likely 2026-2027.

## The One Industry Factor That Matters

**The timing and magnitude of GaN power adoption in data centers.** SiC overcapacity is well-understood and priced in. Micro-LED is speculative. The variable that will determine whether this industry returns to growth in 2027-2028 or stagnates is whether GaN displaces silicon MOSFETs in 800V HVDC data center power distribution at scale. If NVIDIA's GaN-based power architecture becomes standard, MOCVD equipment demand inflects sharply. If adoption delays (as it has before), the industry faces a prolonged downturn with only gradual SiC recovery providing support.

Monitor: NVIDIA/hyperscaler power architecture announcements, GaN device company (Navitas, GaN Systems/Infineon) revenue acceleration, and data center rack power density trends above 100kW.

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**Consensus view:** SiC downturn is painful but temporary; secular EV-driven demand rescues the industry by 2028-2030.

**The variant view (bearish for near-term, structurally uncertain):** The SiC equipment cycle may not recover to prior peaks because Chinese MOCVD/CVD equipment suppliers are rapidly localizing the supply chain. Chinese SiC substrate players went from 10% to 40% share in four years. Chinese equipment makers (AMEC, NAURA) are following the same playbook. Even when SiC device demand recovers, the equipment TAM addressable by Western vendors like Aixtron may be permanently smaller. The "recovery" everyone is modeling for 2028+ may arrive at lower volumes and lower margins than the 2022-2023 peak.

This is structurally bearish for the Western MOCVD duopoly's SiC franchise, and it is not reflected in consensus models that simply assume a proportional recovery.
