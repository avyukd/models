# WLTH — Investment Decision Memo

## Verdict: NEUTRAL (lean negative) — No Edge

**Price:** ~$8.49 | **Market Cap:** $1.27B | **Normalized P/E:** ~10x | **EV/EBITDA:** ~8x

---

## The 1-3 Things That Matter

1. **Interest rate trajectory drives ~75% of economics.** Cash management ($47B at 58bps) is the revenue engine. Every 25bp Fed cut hits revenue directly AND weakens the customer acquisition flywheel. This is a rates bet masquerading as a fintech stock.

2. **Mix shift is the real killer.** As rates fall, clients move from cash (58bps) to advisory (22bps) — a 60% revenue-per-dollar haircut. Net deposit outflows ($208M vs. $874M inflows YoY) confirm this is already happening. Revenue growth decelerates while blended yield compresses simultaneously.

3. **CEO scandal poisons the trust moat.** For a fiduciary wealth manager, the home-lending conflict-of-interest disclosure (Jan 2026, -16%) and subsequent securities fraud investigations are not just legal risk — they undermine the core value proposition.

---

## Bull Case ($12-14, +40-65%)

- Fed pauses or reverses course; cash management economics stabilize
- Advisory AUM growth accelerates to offset cash revenue decline
- CEO scandal resolves cleanly; securities investigations dropped
- Strategic acquirer emerges at UBS-precedent valuation (~$1.4B+ = $9.30+/share)
- Cross-sell engine works: cash → advisory → lending creates sticky multi-product relationships

## Bear Case ($5-6, -30-40%)

- Fed cuts another 75-100bp; cash management fee rate compresses to ~40-45bps
- Net outflows accelerate as cash becomes uncompetitive
- Normalized earnings fall to $80-90M ($0.53-0.60/share); stock reprices to 10x = $5.30-$6.00
- CEO scandal escalates; SEC action or class action settlement
- AI competitors (Altruist "Hazel") commoditize advisory layer
- VC lockup expiration creates selling pressure

## Base Case ($7-9, roughly flat)

- Fed cuts modestly (25-50bp more); cash revenue declines ~10-15%
- Advisory AUM grows 20-25% partially offsetting
- Margins compress from 47% to 40-43% EBITDA
- Normalized EPS ~$0.65-0.75; stock trades 11-13x = $7.15-$9.75
- CEO scandal remains unresolved overhang

---

## Why Not Buy

- No clear variant perception to exploit — the bull case requires rates to cooperate, which is a macro bet not a company insight
- Moat is narrow and eroding (rate-dependent cash business, low switching costs, no pricing power)
- Governance red flag (CEO conflict) in a trust-dependent business
- SBC dilution at 2.5%/year with no buyback offset
- Decelerating growth + potential margin compression = multiple contraction risk
- We'd be buying a rate-sensitive fintech at what may be peak margins

## Why Not Sell/Short

- 10x normalized earnings provides some valuation support at current rates
- Takeout optionality (UBS $1.4B precedent) creates a floor
- Asset-light model with 89% gross margins and real profitability — this isn't a money-losing startup
- Secular tailwind from generational wealth transfer is real, even if medium-term headwinds dominate
- Already down 40% from IPO — much of the bad news may be priced

---

## Decision: NEUTRAL

**Conviction: Medium.** This is a "too hairy" situation — the rate sensitivity makes it a macro call, not a stock-picking opportunity. We have no differentiated view on where rates go.

**What would change this:**
- **To Buy:** Stock below $6 (provides margin of safety for rate cuts) OR credible takeout speculation OR CEO scandal resolution + rate stabilization
- **To Sell/Short:** Evidence of accelerating outflows + another 50bp+ in Fed cuts + lockup expiration timing

**Position sizing:** Zero. Watch list only.
