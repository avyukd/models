# WLTH — Rigorous Financial Analysis

**Stock Price:** ~$8.49 (Feb 18, 2026) | **Market Cap:** $1.27B | **Shares:** 150.12M
**IPO:** Dec 12, 2025 at $14/share (down ~40%)

---

## Key Drivers

1. **Interest rate trajectory** — ~75% of revenue comes from cash management (58bps fee rate on $47B). Fed at 3.5-3.75%, consensus expects terminal ~3-3.25%. Each 25bp cut pressures cash management yield and incentivizes client migration to advisory.
2. **Revenue mix shift** — Cash management earns 58bps vs. investment advisory at 22bps. As rates fall, clients migrate from cash → advisory, compressing blended yield. This is the core margin risk.
3. **Operating leverage** — Incremental adjusted EBITDA margin of 66% and gross margin ~89% demonstrate strong unit economics *if* top-line growth holds.

## What Consensus Gets Wrong

- **The "cheap on earnings" narrative ignores mix shift risk.** At 10x normalized earnings, WLTH looks optically cheap. But if rates fall another 75-100bp and cash balances migrate, normalized earnings could be $80-90M, not $120-130M. That's 14-16x — not cheap for a decelerating fintech.
- **Net deposit outflows are a red flag.** Q3 showed $208M net outflows vs. $874M inflows prior year. This reversal coincides with rate cuts making cash less attractive. It's not a blip — it's the mechanism of mix shift.
- **SBC at $32M/year is ~2.5% annual dilution.** On a $1.27B market cap, this is material and rarely discussed.
