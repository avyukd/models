# CSG N.V. (Czechoslovak Group) - Financial Analysis

**Date:** February 1, 2026
**Ticker:** CSG (Euronext Amsterdam)
**IPO:** January 23, 2026 at EUR 25/share
**Current Price:** ~EUR 30.55
**Market Cap:** ~EUR 30.5B (1B shares outstanding)

---

## Key Financial Drivers

**This stock trades on three variables:**

1. **Ukraine revenue sustainability** - 26% of 9M 2025 revenue came from Ukraine (down from 42% in 2024). Any ceasefire, funding disruption, or payment collection issues directly impacts the top line. This is the single most important swing factor.

2. **NATO defense budget execution** - 68% of pro-forma revenue from NATO countries. The backlog (EUR 14B) provides visibility, but conversion depends on continued European rearmament spend. Contract deferrals or budget reallocation would compress growth.

3. **Acquisition integration risk** - Kinetic Group (USD 2.23B, Nov 2024) doubled US exposure. Pro-forma EBITDA margin of ~27% assumes smooth integration. Any operational issues or margin dilution from acquired assets would pressure the multiple.

---

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**The Ukraine receivables question is underexplored.** CSG generated EUR 1.7B in Ukraine revenue in 2024 (42% of total). The company discloses "increased allowances against financial assets" in its Q3 2025 report, consistent with rising receivables. With Ukraine running persistent budget deficits funded by Western aid, CSG's collection risk is effectively sovereign credit risk on a country at war.

Key questions consensus may not be asking:
- What is the DSO (days sales outstanding) on Ukraine contracts vs. NATO contracts?
- What percentage of Ukraine revenue is funded directly by third-party governments (Czech Ammunition Initiative, EU aid) vs. Ukrainian government payments?
- What is the actual cash conversion on Ukraine business vs. reported revenue?

The company's "strong cash flow management" narrative is difficult to verify without detailed working capital and receivables aging disclosures, which are not available in press releases.

## What Must Be True for This Valuation

At EUR 30.5B market cap (~28x 2026E normalized earnings):

1. **Growth must continue at 15-20%+ annually** beyond 2026 to justify premium multiple
2. **Ukraine payments must be collectible** or replaced by NATO demand
3. **EBITDA margins must hold at 25%+** through Kinetic integration
4. **No major M&A missteps** that dilute returns
5. **European defense spending must remain elevated** for 5+ years

**What breaks the thesis:**
- Ukraine ceasefire that reduces urgency of Western military aid
- Collection issues on Ukraine receivables creating working capital strain
- Margin compression from commodity input costs or integration challenges
- Loss of key contracts to larger European primes (Rheinmetall, BAE)
- Regulatory/political issues in US market (Kinetic faced scrutiny during acquisition)

## Summary Assessment

**Financially strong, but priced for continued excellence.**

CSG has exceptional recent growth (revenue +131% in 2024) and high margins (27% EBITDA). The backlog (EUR 14B) provides 2+ years of revenue visibility. Balance sheet is acceptable at 2.1x net debt/EBITDA with well-laddered maturities.

However, the stock is priced at ~28x 2026E earnings, requiring sustained growth and margin stability. The Ukraine concentration (26% of revenue) creates binary risk. The controlled company structure (84.8% Strnad ownership) and secondary-heavy IPO raise governance questions.

**Key uncertainty:** Cannot verify cash conversion or working capital trends without detailed financial statements. The EBITDA-to-net-income bridge appears reasonable, but EBITDA-to-FCF conversion is the critical unknown, especially given working capital intensity of defense manufacturing and Ukraine collection risk.

**Rating framework:**
- If you believe Ukraine risk is mitigated by NATO demand replacing any shortfall: reasonably valued
- If you believe Ukraine receivables are at risk or growth normalizes faster than expected: expensive
- Insufficient data on FCF and working capital to assess definitively
