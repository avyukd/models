# CSG N.V. - Investment Committee Memo

**Date:** February 1, 2026
**Ticker:** CSG (Euronext Amsterdam)
**Price:** EUR 30.55
**Market Cap:** EUR 30.5B

---

## Recommendation

**Decision:** Neutral (Pass)
**Conviction:** High conviction in the pass
**Classification:** Too Hairy

This is a war derivative masquerading as a structural defense play. The stock is priced for continued excellence (28x 2026E earnings), but 42% of 2024 revenue came from a single customer whose ability to pay depends entirely on Western financial support to a country at war. The founder just extracted EUR 2.55B personally while raising only EUR 750M for the company. Current 27% EBITDA margins reflect temporary capacity scarcity that normalizes by 2028-2030. There is no durable moat -- ammunition is a commodity, and competitors are building capacity.

We would buy this stock 30-40% lower, when peace risk is either resolved or priced in and margin normalization is reflected in the multiple.

---

## Key Drivers (The 3 Things That Matter)

1. **Ukraine revenue trajectory** -- 42% of 2024 revenue, 26% of 9M 2025. Any ceasefire, funding disruption, or payment collection issues directly impacts the top line. This is binary risk on a company trading at a growth multiple.

2. **Margin sustainability as capacity normalizes** -- 27% EBITDA margins are cyclically elevated. Rheinmetall's Unterluess plant (350K rounds/year by 2027), BAE expansions, and EU-funded ASAP capacity are all coming online. By 2028-2030, CSG faces pricing pressure regardless of demand levels.

3. **Founder behavior post-IPO** -- Strnad sold EUR 2.55B at IPO while the company raised only EUR 750M. He de-risked personally while keeping 85% control. The Kinetic acquisition saw four price increases in a competitive auction -- this is not disciplined capital allocation.

---

## Thesis Summary

- CSG is a capacity beneficiary of a temporary supply-demand imbalance in European ammunition, not a structural compounder with a durable moat
- Ukraine concentration creates binary risk on the largest revenue contributor; NATO restocking is a 2027-2032 story while Ukraine revenue is monetizing now
- 27% EBITDA margins are unsustainable as European ammunition capacity expands 8x from 2022 baseline; normalized margins are 15-18%
- At 28x 2026E earnings and 17x EBITDA, the stock requires continued excellence with no room for peace-driven demand disruption or margin compression
- The 3:1 ratio of secondary to primary IPO proceeds signals monetization motive by founder who has seen more than we have

## The Setup

CSG rode the Ukraine war from EUR 1.7B revenue (2023) to EUR 6.4B+ (2025E). The backlog (EUR 14B) provides 3 years of visibility. The Kinetic acquisition (USD 2.2B) diversified into US commercial ammunition (Federal, Remington brands).

The bull case is straightforward: European defense spending is structural (NATO 5% GDP target by 2035), CSG is #1 small-caliber globally and #2 medium/large-caliber in Europe, and the EUR 58B Slovak framework agreement provides long-term visibility.

The problem: this is already priced in. At 28x normalized 2026E earnings, the stock requires:
- Growth to continue at 15-20%+ annually beyond 2026
- Ukraine payments to be fully collectible
- EBITDA margins to hold at 25%+ through integration
- No major M&A missteps
- European defense spending to remain elevated for 5+ years

Any deviation from this path compresses the multiple materially.

## Risks and Kill Switches

**What invalidates the thesis:**
- Ukraine ceasefire that reduces urgency of Western military aid before NATO restocking fills the gap
- Collection issues on Ukraine receivables creating working capital strain
- Margin compression as capacity normalizes and competitors price aggressively

**What causes permanent loss:**
- Loss of key contracts to Rheinmetall or BAE as capacity becomes available
- Strnad overpaying for acquisitions that dilute returns on capital
- Serbia subsidiary caught in sanctions controversy (sleeper risk)

**Kill switches:**
- EBITDA margins below 22% for two consecutive quarters
- Ukraine revenue decline not offset by NATO contract growth
- Net debt/EBITDA above 3.0x indicating stress
- Strnad selling additional shares before lock-up expiration

## Why This Is "Too Hairy"

1. **Binary Ukraine risk cannot be sized.** 42% of 2024 revenue from a single wartime customer. We cannot underwrite the probability of peace negotiations, the timing of any ceasefire, or the collection risk on Ukraine receivables.

2. **FCF and working capital are black boxes.** The company discloses EBITDA but not free cash flow, capex, or receivables aging. We cannot verify cash conversion, which is critical for a defense manufacturer with Ukraine collection risk.

3. **No clear variant view.** The specialists identified real risks, but those risks are likely known to the market. We are not positioned to have a differentiated view on Ukraine peace negotiations or NATO procurement timing.

4. **Margin normalization is certain, timing is not.** We know 27% EBITDA margins are unsustainable. We do not know whether normalization happens in 2027, 2028, or 2030.

5. **Founder sold 3x what the company raised.** Strnad has seen the future better than us. His decision to monetize EUR 2.55B while keeping control suggests he views current valuation as attractive to sell.

---

## What Would Change Our View

**What moves this out of "too hard":**
- Stock 30-40% lower (16-18x 2026E earnings) where margin normalization and Ukraine risk are priced
- Ukraine peace resolution with clear visibility on NATO replacement demand
- Two quarters of disclosed FCF showing strong cash conversion
- Strnad demonstrably walking away from an acquisition (evidence of discipline)

**What would make this a buy:**
- All of the above, plus margin visibility from locked-in framework agreements
- Ukraine concentration below 15% of revenue with NATO/commercial offsetting

## Positioning and Portfolio Logic

**Position:** None. Pass.

**Why no position:**
- We cannot size the Ukraine binary risk
- The valuation requires continued excellence with no margin for error
- Founder behavior (IPO structure, Kinetic bid escalation) suggests less discipline than ownership alignment implies
- This is a cyclical capacity play priced as a structural compounder

**What we would do if forced to have a view:**
- Short on any peace negotiation headlines + lock-up expiration approaching
- Or wait for 30-40% pullback to buy long with margin normalization priced

**Alternative exposure:**
- If bullish on European defense, Rheinmetall offers better moat (systems integration, technology, scale) at similar valuation with lower Ukraine concentration
- If bullish on ammunition specifically, wait for CSG de-rating

---

## Summary

CSG is a well-positioned company in the right industry at the wrong price. The 42% Ukraine revenue concentration, temporary nature of the capacity scarcity moat, and founder's EUR 2.55B IPO monetization all signal this is a stock for the too-hard pile. The thesis requires a variant view on Ukraine peace negotiations or NATO procurement timing that we do not possess.

At 28x 2026E normalized earnings, the risk/reward is asymmetric to the downside. We pass and revisit on a 30-40% pullback.

**Decision: Neutral (Too Hairy)**
