## Evaluating the VIC Author's Claims

**Claim: KRW 25-30B+ ex-lease EBITDA from Gwacheon alone.**
- Gwacheon guided revenue: KRW 53-64B. VIC assumes KRW 16B cash costs + networking at 30% margin.
- At 80%+ contracted with named anchor tenants, revenue guidance is credible.
- But "ex-lease EBITDA" is doing heavy lifting. Gwacheon likely carries significant lease/right-of-use obligations (land, equipment). True unlevered FCF contribution will be lower. The VIC author strips lease costs to make the EBITDA look higher and then applies a cap rate that already excludes lease costs -- this is internally consistent but flatters the number vs. standard EBITDA.

**Claim: 6-7x stabilized EBITDA.**
- EV KRW 469B / stabilized EBITDA KRW 57-64B = **7.3x-8.2x**. Slightly higher than claimed. The VIC uses unconsolidated financials which exclude some overhead; consolidated stabilized EBITDA is probably 5-10% lower per MW, pushing the multiple to 7.5-9x. Not egregiously wrong, but the "6x" bottom is aggressive.

**Claim: 20x EBITDA comp multiple.**
- Global data center REITs (Equinix, Digital Realty) trade at 20-25x. But these are diversified, liquid, USD-denominated. A Korean KOSDAQ small-cap controlled by a parent deserves a discount. 12-15x is more defensible as an attainable multiple with governance improvements.

**Claim: KRW 250-350K target (130-230% upside).**
- At 15x stabilized EBITDA of KRW 60B = EV KRW 900B. Less net debt KRW 50B = equity KRW 850B / 4.84M shares = KRW 175K. That is roughly a double, consistent with the low end.
- The high end requires 20x multiple AND the cap-light expansion contributing, which is speculative.

## Red Flags Checklist

- [x] Persistent negative FCF -- Yes in FY23-FY24, but capex-driven and now normalizing
- [ ] Rising SBC -- No, immaterial at <0.5% of revenue
- [x] Margin expansion from accounting -- "Ex-lease EBITDA" is a management-friendly metric; watch for lease classification games under K-IFRS 16
- [ ] Weak cash conversion -- No, pre-Gwacheon cash conversion was strong (OCF/NI >1.0x)
- [ ] Balance sheet fragility -- No, 1.1x leverage is comfortable
- [ ] Aggressive revenue recognition -- No evidence
- [x] Related party risk -- Gabia controls 37%, consolidates Kinx; history of value extraction

## Key Financial Drivers

1. **Gwacheon revenue ramp trajectory.** Every KRW 10B of incremental Gwacheon revenue at 60% flow-through = KRW 6B EBITDA = ~KRW 1,240/share in earnings power. This is the single most important variable.

2. **Multiple re-rating conditional on governance change.** The stock trades at 7-8x stabilized EBITDA because of Gabia's control discount. If activists succeed at the March 2026 vote, the re-rating could be significant. Without governance change, the multiple stays compressed.

3. **Lease obligation transparency.** The gap between "ex-lease EBITDA" and true free cash flow after all obligations determines whether this is actually cheap or just looks cheap.

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**The orphaned data point:** Gwacheon capex was KRW ~116B (visible in FY24 cash flow statement). At KRW 25-30B stabilized ex-lease EBITDA, that is a 4.6-5.2 year payback on invested capital -- a 19-22% unlevered return. This is excellent for a data center and validates the facility economics independently of any multiple argument. Most analysts focus on the EBITDA multiple; the return on invested capital tells you the business quality is genuinely strong. Even if the stock never re-rates, shareholders earn a high return on the capital deployed.

**The risk nobody discusses:** KRW 70.9B of total debt against KRW 20.7B of cash means Kinx needs the Gwacheon ramp to succeed to comfortably service debt. If occupancy stalls at 60-70% instead of reaching 80%+, debt service becomes tight relative to FCF. The contracts mitigate this, but execution risk on ramp timing is real.

**Bottom line:** Kinx is a solid asset at a reasonable (not cheap) price. The VIC thesis oversells the discount (6-7x is really 7.5-9x) and uses aggressive comp multiples (20x). Realistic upside is 50-100% over two years if Gwacheon ramps and governance improves. Without the governance catalyst, this is a 15-18x normalized P/E data center business in Korea -- fairly valued, not a screaming buy.
