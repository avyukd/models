## 2. Key Facts & Data Points

**Source: `vic-posting.md`**

**Company profile:**
- Kinx is the largest independent, publicly traded data center company in South Korea (ticker 093320 KS)
- Parent company: Gabia, which owns 37% of Kinx but consolidates it as a subsidiary under Korean law
- 80%+ of unconsolidated sales from Data Center (IDC) business
- Total IT load: 13.5MW (10MW fully owned, 2.5MW leased/subleased, 1MW networking-only)
- Kinx is the largest "carrier-neutral" data center provider in South Korea
- 70% of pre-Gwacheon data center sales and 33% of Gwacheon estimated revenue from networking services (vs. 15%-20% at peers)

**Gwacheon facility:**
- Opened October 2024; increased company IT capacity by over 2.5x
- As of January 2026: 80%+ contracted, effectively "full" per company definition
- 3MW-4MW taken by one of the largest tech companies in the country; another 3MW by another large customer
- Company guided to KRW 53B-64B/yr revenue when ramped, translating to KRW 25B-30B+/yr ex-lease EBITDA
- Rack rental revenue guidance: KRW 35B-40B/yr; networking revenue target: KRW ~20B/yr at initial ramp, potentially KRW 35B-40B/yr longer term
- Company KRW 28B ex-lease EBITDA in 2024 (entire company)
- Ramp expected over next 12-24 months (from time of writing)
- South Korea data center vacancy rate: ~5%
- KEPCO (utility monopoly) power constraints limit new supply in Seoul metro

**Capital-light expansion:**
- Kinx in negotiations to operate/master-lease 20MW-30MW of a new 40MW-60MW data center
- Would 2x-3x Kinx's current managed/owned IT load
- Capital requirement: KRW 5B-20B
- Pricing (ex-power) at Gabia data centers increased 30%-43% from December 2022 to September 2025

**Activist/governance situation:**
- Miri Capital: ~25% of Gabia, ~13% of Kinx; largest combined holding (>20% of disclosed investments)
- Align Capital Partners: ~14% of Gabia (including via tender offer); filed Korean equivalent of 13D; third largest disclosed position
- Combined activists hold ~40% of Gabia voting shares (inclusive of treasury stock impact)
- Activists believed to have gained majority of Gabia voting shares in December 2025
- Miri track record includes Dream Incubator (described as similar situation)
- Align track record includes JB Financial (tripled since mid-2022 entry)
- Next shareholder vote: March 2026; potential board flip as late as spring 2027

**Valuation claims:**
- Author claims Kinx trades at 6x-7x stabilized EBITDA
- Local Korean data center transactions cited at ~20x EBITDA / 5% cap rate
- Macquarie acquired a single data center in Seoul metro at 20x EBITDA
- Global public data center comps: median ~20x EBITDA / 5% cap rate
- Legacy business: KRW 33B-36B ex-lease EBITDA in FY26, valued at 15x-20x
- Price target: KRW 250k-350k/share by end of 2027 (claimed 130%-230% upside, 50%-80% IRR)

**Risks stated:**
- Lack of governance change at Gabia, value locked or siphoned
- Outside shareholders gaining Gabia control but siphoning from Kinx (mitigated by Miri owning both)
- Data center supply/demand turning negative (assessed as minimal by author)
