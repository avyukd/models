# Rand Worldwide (RWWI) -- Financial Analysis

**Date:** 2026-02-24 | **Price:** ~$16.11 | **Market Cap:** ~$541M | **Shares Out:** 33.6M | **P/E (TTM):** ~32x

## Business Financial Profile

Rand Worldwide is a value-added reseller (VAR) of Autodesk and other design/engineering software, providing implementation, training, and support. Operates through IMAGINiT Technologies, ASCENT, Rand Simulation, and Rand 3D divisions. This is a low-capital-intensity services/reseller business with recurring maintenance/subscription revenue streams. Revenue quality depends on the mix between pass-through software resale (low margin) and professional services/training (higher margin). Zero analyst coverage. Reports on a June fiscal year-end; no longer files with the SEC (last EDGAR filing was FY2014 10-K, CIK 852437). Now files on OTC Markets.

## Critical Data Quality Warning

**The available financial data for RWWI is inconsistent and difficult to reconcile across sources.** FY2023 (ended June 2023) revenue is reported at ~$377M, while FY2025 is cited at ~$179M -- a 53% decline that, if accurate, suggests either a major business change or a reclassification of revenue recognition (net vs. gross treatment of software resale under ASC 606). TTM revenue per Yahoo Finance shows ~$86.7M, which conflicts with a single quarter reportedly at $121.8M. TTM diluted EPS is $0.50, implying ~$16.8M net income. EBITDA of $82M at a 37.66% margin is cited by Simply Wall St but is irreconcilable with $86.7M TTM revenue -- it would imply near-100% EBITDA conversion, which is nonsensical.

## Valuation Framework

At ~$16.11/share and 33.6M shares, market cap is ~$541M. Enterprise value including net debt: ~$556M.

Using provisional normalized earnings of ~$16.4M, the stock trades at ~33x normalized earnings. For a VAR/services business with no analyst coverage, this is a demanding multiple. Comparable publicly traded Autodesk VARs do not exist at this scale, but professional services firms in the CAD/engineering space typically trade at 12-20x earnings.

**What must be true for this valuation to make sense:** RWWI must be growing earnings meaningfully (15%+ annually) with expanding margins, and the business must have durable competitive advantages that justify a premium to typical services firms. Without visible revenue growth (and in fact with apparent revenue decline), the current multiple appears elevated unless the revenue decline is purely an accounting reclassification and underlying profitability is growing.

**What breaks:** If normalized earnings are closer to $10-12M (possible given data inconsistencies), the P/E expands to 45-55x, which is difficult to justify for any VAR business.

## Key Financial Drivers

1. **Revenue recognition method (gross vs. net):** This single accounting choice explains the apparent revenue collapse and is the most important factor for understanding the real business trajectory.
2. **Autodesk relationship and subscription transition:** As Autodesk shifts to subscriptions, RWWI's role and economics are changing. Whether RWWI captures a recurring revenue stream or gets disintermediated is existential.
3. **Profitability on a shrinking revenue base:** If $16-17M net income is real and sustainable, the question is whether this earnings power is growing or at risk.

## What Consensus May Be Missing

There is no consensus -- zero analysts cover this stock. The more relevant question is what the OTC investor base may be missing. **The sharp increase in LOC borrowings ($1.8M to $9.7M in one quarter) combined with a thin current ratio of 1.04 suggests potential cash flow stress that the headline earnings number may not reflect.** If the company is reporting $17M in net income but simultaneously drawing down its credit line, cash conversion may be poor -- possibly due to working capital dynamics around software license renewals or because reported earnings overstate economic cash generation. This divergence between apparent profitability and balance sheet deterioration is the single most important thing to investigate in the actual financial filings.
