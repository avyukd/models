# [Research/Initiation] Rand Worldwide Inc
**Source:** Buyback Capital (Substack) — Forbes Jamieson | **Date:** September 17, 2025 | **Disclosure:** Author holds RWWI.

---

## Key Facts & Arguments

### Peter Kamin / 3K Limited Partnership
- Kamin (ValueAct founder) retired 2011, opened 3K LP in 2012
- 3K did a levered recap and tender offer for RWWI in 2014
- Playbook: buy large stakes in small companies, delist to OTC (limited disclosure), pursue organic + inorganic growth at high hurdle rates, return all excess cash via dividends
- Bolt-on acquisition IRRs typically 20-30%
- RWWI has acquired at least 6 smaller competitors since Kamin took over
- **Calloway's Nursery precedent (take-private risk):** 3K used a reverse split at ~$200K/share to odd-lot minority shareholders and buy them out at ~$7/share pre-split while the business was undervalued. RWWI shareholders exposed to similar risk if shares drift lower. However, RWWI's ~$500M market cap makes uplisting or outright sale (perhaps to ADSK) more likely exit paths.

### IMAGINiT Technologies (Core Business)
- Largest AutoCAD reseller in North America by a very considerable margin
- Responsible for ~1/3 of all North American ADSK seats
- Operates as outsourced business development: free webinars (top of funnel) → consulting → software sale → training/implementation → account management/renewals
- RWWI has grown topline faster than ADSK itself since 2014

### Agency Model Transition (June 10, 2024)
- **Before:** RWWI handled entire sales process — quoting, fulfillment, billing, collection, payment to ADSK. Revenue = gross software value.
- **After:** RWWI maintains customer relationships, presents info for proposals, ADSK fulfills/bills/collects directly, pays RWWI a commission. Revenue = commission only.
- **Impact:** Technical revenue decline but gross profit per sale unchanged. OpEx currently maintained at FY23/FY24 levels during transition — will decline as legacy infrastructure no longer needed. Working capital improves as 3-year deal obligations run off. Full roll-off expected by year-end 2026.
- **Three-year deal distortion:** 2018-2023, ADSK incentivized 3-year subs. Various billing arrangements (upfront or annual) created balance sheet assets/liabilities that are still unwinding. This distorts cash flow statements and explains the LOC draw — management tapped credit facility to maintain dividend during transition.

### Why ADSK Won't In-House Distribution
- AED customers are highly dispersed; industry in constant deconsolidation
- Smaller average customer accounts make outsourcing economical
- ADSK has no expertise in training/consulting/implementation and no historic desire to build it
- Investor pressure (Starboard Value activist) demands margin improvement — entering an OpEx cycle would be "madness/potentially suicidal for management"
- Path of least resistance for ADSK to capture this capability = acquire a top VAR (*wink wink*)

### ADSK CEO Anagnost on Channel Strategy (Q1 FY26 Earnings Call)
- "We want fewer, more solution-focused channel partners... and fewer transactionally focused channel partners"
- Smaller gold/silver VARs being bought up by platinum-tier or shutting down
- ADSK store grew nicely with increased price realization from capturing down-market business
- Shifting incentives from renewals to new business — favors VARs with real sales forces

### Rand Simulation (Secondary Segment)
- Started 2018, acts as outsourced design house and ANSYS VAR
- Per a VIC writeup: possibly ~$40M/year revenue, growing 20-30% annually
- Same strategy as core: specialized outsourcing → royalty-like commission revenues

### Valuation (as of Sept 2025)
- ~10x LTM earnings, ~8x LTM EBIT
- Company pays out all FCF as dividends; forward yield ~8%
- Author argues RWWI is currently underearning due to transition (OpEx elevated, legacy infrastructure maintained)
- Annualized figures place Rand between 2022 and 2023 performance levels

### Risks (Author's Assessment)
1. ADSK continues squeezing VARs — author believes worst case already enacted
2. ADSK falters in marketplace — recent results suggest unlikely
3. VAR model permanently impaired — author expects long-term boon for RWWI
4. **Kamin takes company private at bad price** — most pressing risk
