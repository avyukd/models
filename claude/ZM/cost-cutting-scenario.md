# ZM Cost-Cutting Scenario: 40% Headcount Reduction

**Punchline: A 40% cut would push economic FCF to ~$1.6-1.7B, implying $87-103/share ($62-78 from operations + $25 cash). But Zoom is already lean -- the incremental gain over current buyback-driven compounding is modest, and the execution risk of gutting a 7,400-person company is real. This is not a DOCU/SNAP situation where obvious fat justifies the knife.**

## Current Cost Structure (FY2026, ending Jan 2026)

Revenue: $4,869M. Headcount: ~7,400 (per FY2025 10-K; roughly flat YoY post-2023 layoff of ~1,300).

| Line Item | GAAP ($M) | % Rev | Est. SBC Incl. ($M) | Cash Cost ($M) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cost of Revenue | ~1,015 | 20.8% | ~60 | ~955 |
| Research & Development | ~575 | 11.8% | ~300 | ~275 |
| Sales & Marketing | ~1,140 | 23.4% | ~250 | ~890 |
| General & Administrative | ~258 | 5.3% | ~158 | ~100 |
| **Total Opex (ex-COGS)** | **~1,973** | **40.5%** | **~708** | **~1,265** |
| **Total Costs** | **~2,988** | **61.4%** | **~768** | **~2,220** |

Derivation: GAAP opex lines computed from reported % of revenue (R&D 11.8%, S&M 23.4%, G&A 5.3% per FY2026 earnings release). SBC allocation estimated from total $768M, distributed based on historical 10-K patterns where R&D absorbs ~39%, S&M ~33%, G&A ~21%, COGS ~7%.

**Current P&L summary:**
- GAAP Operating Income: $1,124M (23.1% margin)
- Non-GAAP Operating Income: $1,967M (40.4% margin)
- FCF: $1,924M (39.5% margin)
- SBC: $768M (15.8% of rev, down from 23% in FY2024)
- Economic FCF (FCF - SBC): ~$1,156M

## The 40% Cut Scenario

40% of ~7,400 = ~2,960 heads eliminated, leaving ~4,440 employees.

### Where the Cuts Fall

| Function | Current Est. Heads | Cut % | Heads Cut | Remaining |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R&D | ~2,600 | 45% | 1,170 | 1,430 |
| S&M | ~2,400 | 40% | 960 | 1,440 |
| G&A | ~700 | 50% | 350 | 350 |
| COGS/Ops | ~1,700 | 28% | 480 | 1,220 |
| **Total** | **~7,400** | **40%** | **2,960** | **4,440** |

Logic: R&D gets deepest cuts -- Zoom's core product (video/voice) is mature. G&A is always bloated. S&M cuts proportional. COGS is lightest -- you still need infrastructure and support.

### Post-Cut P&L

Assume personnel costs are ~65% of each GAAP line (remainder is infrastructure, third-party costs, facilities). A 40% headcount cut reduces personnel costs by ~40%, but total line items by less.

| Line Item | Current ($M) | Savings ($M) | Post-Cut ($M) | % Rev |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cost of Revenue | 1,015 | 90 | 925 | 19.0% |
| R&D | 575 | 170 | 405 | 8.3% |
| S&M | 1,140 | 250 | 890 | 18.3% |
| G&A | 258 | 90 | 168 | 3.5% |
| SBC | 768 | 350 | 418 | 8.6% |
| **Total Costs** | **2,988** | **600** | **2,388** | **49.0%** |

Key assumptions: (1) Revenue flat at $4,869M -- aggressive cuts likely cause some churn, offsetting organic growth. (2) Personnel ~65% of opex lines. (3) SBC cut by ~45% as fewer employees receive grants and some unvested RSUs are forfeited. (4) One-time severance of ~$300-400M (not included in run-rate).

### Post-Cut Earnings

| Metric | Current | Post-Cut | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| GAAP Operating Income | $1,124M | $1,724M | +$600M |
| GAAP Operating Margin | 23.1% | 35.4% | +1,230 bps |
| Non-GAAP Op Income | $1,967M | $2,142M | +$175M |
| Non-GAAP Op Margin | 40.4% | 44.0% | +360 bps |
| SBC | $768M | $418M | -$350M |
| Est. FCF | $1,924M | ~$2,200M | +$276M |
| Economic FCF (FCF - SBC) | $1,156M | ~$1,780M | +$624M |

The non-GAAP improvement looks modest (+$175M) because Zoom is already running at 40% non-GAAP margins. The real gain is in economic FCF: SBC drops from $768M to $418M, so the gap between reported FCF and economic FCF shrinks by $350M. Total economic FCF improvement is ~$624M.

## Valuation

Post-cut economic FCF: ~$1.7-1.8B. Apply 12-18x for a low/no-growth, highly profitable software utility.

| Multiple | EV ($B) | + Net Cash ($B) | Equity ($B) | Per Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12x | $20.4-21.6 | $7.8 | $28.2-29.4 | $92-95 |
| 15x | $25.5-27.0 | $7.8 | $33.3-34.8 | $108-113 |
| 18x | $30.6-32.4 | $7.8 | $38.4-40.2 | $125-131 |

At 15x post-cut economic FCF, ZM trades at ~$110/share vs. $75 today -- **47% upside**.

**Comparison to current valuation:** At today's $75 ($15.2B EV), the market pays 13.1x current economic FCF of $1.16B. A cost-cut scenario at 15x yields $110. The question is whether the market would re-rate the multiple upward for higher margins, or whether gutting the company signals decay and compresses the multiple.

## Does Zoom Even Need This?

**No. This is the wrong framework for ZM, unlike DOCU and SNAP.**

| Metric | ZM (Current) | DOCU (Pre-Cut) | SNAP (Pre-Cut) |
|---|---|---|---|
| GAAP Op Margin | 23.1% | ~5% | Negative |
| Non-GAAP Op Margin | 40.4% | ~25% | ~5% |
| FCF Margin | 39.5% | ~25% | Low single digits |
| SBC % of Rev | 15.8% | ~25% | ~30%+ |

Zoom already did the hard work. The Feb 2023 layoff (15%, ~1,300 heads) plus natural attrition took headcount from ~8,400 to ~7,400. Margins expanded 570 bps YoY in FY2026. SBC is declining at ~300-400 bps/year as a % of revenue.

**The buyback math is compelling without cuts.** ZM repurchased $1.6B in FY2026 against a $23B market cap -- a 7% buyback yield. Economic FCF of $1.16B fully funds buybacks after SBC dilution. At 4-5% revenue growth and continued SBC compression, economic FCF grows to $1.4-1.5B within 2-3 years organically. The share count shrinks 5-7% annually.

**Risk of cutting into bone:** At 7,400 heads, Zoom runs ~$658K revenue per employee -- already efficient for enterprise software. Cutting to 4,400 pushes this to $1.1M/employee, which is extreme. The AI-driven product roadmap (Zoom AI Companion, Contact Center) requires R&D investment. Gutting R&D risks turning a slowly growing platform into an accelerating declining one.

**Bottom line:** A 40% cut could theoretically push the stock to $90-110 from operations alone. But unlike DOCU/SNAP, the incremental value ($624M in economic FCF improvement) comes with meaningful product/revenue risk in a company that is already printing cash. The better path: let buybacks compound at 7%/year, let SBC continue declining naturally, and wait for the market to recognize that ZM at 13x economic FCF with $25/share in cash and no debt is already cheap.
