## The Setup

ARDS kills ~40% of patients. There are 190,000+ US cases annually. There is no FDA-approved drug. This is one of the last major critical care indications with zero approved pharmacotherapy — not for lack of trying, but because the biology is brutally heterogeneous.

Paridiprubart is a first-in-class anti-TLR4 monoclonal antibody. TLR4 sits at the top of the innate immune cascade — blocking it should dampen the inflammatory response regardless of the trigger (viral, bacterial, trauma). The Phase 3 showed 27% relative mortality reduction in COVID-ARDS (p<0.001, n=278). That is a real result in a hard endpoint.

The critical question — and the only question — is whether this translates to general ARDS. The BARDA platform trial (~200 patients, multiple ARDS etiologies) will answer it. If yes, this is a $500M+ peak sales opportunity with no competition. If no, the company has cash, some residual COVID-ARDS IP, and limited options.
