# Edesa Biotech (EDSA) -- Financial Analysis

**Date**: 2026-02-24 | **Price**: ~$1.01 | **Basic Market Cap**: ~$8M | **Fully Diluted Market Cap**: ~$15.6M

## Business Financial Profile

Pre-revenue, clinical-stage biotech. Zero recurring revenue. Entire value is option-value on paridiprubart (EB05) for ARDS/respiratory failure and EB06 for vitiligo. Cost structure is almost entirely fixed (clinical trial spend + G&A). Binary outcomes dominate -- this is a call option on regulatory approval and partnership, not a cash-flow story.

Quarterly burn increased to $2.2M in Q1 FY26 (annualized ~$8.8M) vs. $7.2M for full FY25. The increase reflects EB06 manufacturing costs for the planned Phase 2 vitiligo study plus Phase 3 closeout costs on paridiprubart. There is no revenue and no near-term path to revenue absent a licensing deal or partnership.

## Cash Position and Runway

**Cash as of Dec 31, 2025: $12.1M. Working capital: $12.0M.**

The near-parity between cash and working capital suggests minimal non-cash current assets and liabilities -- the balance sheet is clean. No debt mentioned in filings. This is real, unencumbered cash.

**Runway calculation**: At the Q1 FY26 burn rate of ~$2.2M/quarter, cash lasts approximately 5.5 quarters (~Q2-Q3 FY27, or mid-to-late 2027). However, if EB06 Phase 2 ramps, burn could increase to $2.5-3.0M/quarter, compressing runway to 4-5 quarters. Critical point: **the company is trading at a discount to its own cash balance** on a basic share count (~$8M market cap vs $12.1M cash).

## Dilution History and Capitalization -- This Is Where It Gets Complicated

The share structure is heavily layered:

- **Basic common shares outstanding**: ~7.97M (per Q1 FY26 weighted average)
- **Feb 2025 private placement**: 3.47M common shares + 834 Series B-1 preferred shares (stated value $10K each, convertible at $1.92/share = ~4.34M additional common equivalent)
- **ATM program**: $4.0M capacity (filed Sep 2025), ~$3.4M net already raised post-FY25
- **Warrants**: Exercisable at $3.445/share from Oct 2024 strategic investment (exact share count not disclosed in available filings)

**Fully diluted share count estimate**: stockanalysis.com reports ~15.46M shares. At $1.01, that implies a fully diluted market cap of ~$15.6M. This is the relevant figure for valuation -- still a meaningful discount to cash ($12.1M) plus any pipeline option value.

**Key dilution risk**: The Series B-1 preferred shares convert at $1.92, which is nearly 2x the current price. These are NOT currently dilutive at $1.01 -- holders have no economic incentive to convert. Similarly, warrants at $3.445 are deeply out of the money. However, if paridiprubart succeeds and the stock re-rates above $2, expect the full diluted share count to matter.

Shares increased 16.68% YoY per stockanalysis.com. For a micro-cap biotech burning $7-9M/year with no revenue, this level of dilution is structural and expected to continue.

## Normalized Earnings Power

Not applicable in any traditional sense. This is a pre-revenue biotech. There are no earnings to normalize. The relevant framework is probability-weighted asset value.

## Probability-Weighted Pipeline Valuation (Rough Framework)

**Paridiprubart (EB05) -- ARDS/Respiratory Failure**:
- Phase 3 complete with statistically significant results (p<0.001 on primary endpoint, 27% relative mortality reduction across 278 patients)
- FDA Fast Track designation granted
- Subgroup data in sepsis, AKI, pneumonia filed for provisional patents
- Additional government-funded ARDS study (~200 patients) ongoing
- Company exploring expedited regulatory pathways and licensing partnerships

**The core question**: What is a Phase 3-successful anti-inflammatory biologic for ARDS worth? ARDS has no approved pharmacotherapy. The addressable population is large (~200K US cases/year with high mortality). Peak sales estimates for a successful ARDS drug range widely ($500M-$2B+), but EDSA cannot commercialize independently. The realistic outcome is a licensing deal.

**Assumption (clearly labeled)**: If we assume a 40-50% probability of eventual approval (reasonable for a Phase 3-complete asset with strong data but needing additional regulatory steps), and a licensing deal worth $50-150M in total value (upfront + milestones) to EDSA, the probability-weighted value is $20-75M. Even at the low end, this is meaningfully above the current ~$15.6M fully diluted market cap.

**EB06 (vitiligo)**: Phase 2 not yet started. Early stage, low incremental value at this point. Call it optionality.

**The single biggest risk is binary regulatory/partnership outcome.** If paridiprubart fails to gain regulatory traction or no partner materializes, this is a $12.1M cash pile burning at $8-9M/year that eventually goes to zero.

## Key Financial Drivers

1. **Partnership/licensing deal for paridiprubart** -- this is the only thing that matters. The entire equity value above cash is the option on this outcome. A deal announcement would likely be a 3-10x event.
2. **Cash runway preservation** -- every quarter of burn without a deal or revenue reduces the terminal value and increases future dilution risk.
3. **Regulatory pathway clarity** -- FDA feedback on whether additional trials are needed vs. accelerated/expedited approval will determine timeline and capital needs.

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**The stock is trading below cash on a basic share count and roughly at cash on a fully diluted basis, yet carries a Phase 3-complete asset with statistically significant data in an indication with zero approved therapies.** This is unusual. The market appears to be pricing in either (a) significant further dilution to fund operations, or (b) zero probability of a licensing deal. Given that the Phase 3 data is genuinely strong (p<0.001, multiple confirmatory subgroups, favorable safety), the market's implied probability of zero commercial value seems too low.

The non-obvious angle: the preferred shares and warrants are deeply out of the money at $1.01 (conversion at $1.92, warrants at $3.445). If you believe a partnership is probable, the basic share count (~8M) is what matters on the way up, with dilution only kicking in above $1.92. This creates asymmetric upside leverage that the fully diluted market cap obscures.

**Counterargument**: Micro-cap biotechs with positive Phase 3 data frequently fail to monetize. The ARDS market has no commercial precedent for pharmacotherapy. A partner may demand onerous terms or the regulatory path may require an additional confirmatory study that EDSA cannot fund without heavy dilution.

## Conclusion

At ~$1.01, EDSA is priced at or below its cash value, with a Phase 3-complete asset in a high-unmet-need indication priced at effectively zero. The balance sheet is clean, runway extends to mid-2027 at current burn, and the dilutive securities are out of the money. The investment case is straightforward: this is a binary bet on partnership/regulatory progress with a cash floor providing downside protection (minus ongoing burn). The risk is time decay -- every quarter without a deal erodes the margin of safety.
