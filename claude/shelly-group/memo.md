## THE BOTTOM LINE

Shelly Group is a high-quality, founder-led business with exceptional capital allocation discipline, strong margins, and a defensible niche in the smart home market. The problem is not the business -- it is the valuation. At 38x trailing earnings and 26x forward normalized earnings, the stock prices in continued 25-30%+ growth for 3-5 years with margin expansion.

Growth is already decelerating (from 57% to 34%). The Matter protocol represents a structural threat to Shelly's core differentiation (open ecosystem, local control). The SaaS transition that would create recurring revenue moats shows a 0.1% attach rate. China manufacturing concentration is unaddressed.

**There is no variant perception here.** The market sees a high-growth European IoT leader with great margins. That is exactly what it is. Without an edge, there is no trade.

**We would be buyers at a 25-30% lower price (EUR 38-42/share) or upon evidence of SaaS traction and manufacturing diversification.**

---

## KEY DRIVERS (The 1-3 Things That Actually Matter)

### 1. Revenue Growth Trajectory (CRITICAL)

This stock trades on growth. Period. The deceleration from 57% (2023) to 42% (2024) to 34% (TTM) is the single most important variable. At current multiples, if growth drops below 25%, the multiple compresses violently.

- **What the market watches:** Quarterly revenue prints, geographic expansion progress
- **What the market should watch but doesn't:** German macro weakness (47% revenue concentration), installer channel adoption rates, Matter protocol adoption pace

### 2. Matter Protocol Impact on Competitive Position (UNDERAPPRECIATED)

Shelly's entire value proposition -- "buy Shelly for local control and open ecosystem compatibility" -- is being standardized by Matter. Within 3-5 years, every Matter-certified device will have local control and work with any platform. Shelly's hardware differentiation erodes to: build quality (replicable), size/form factor (temporary), and brand reputation (fragile).

The only escape route is a successful software/services transition. Current evidence: 0.1% Premium app attach rate (35,000 subscribers on 28.8 million devices sold).

### 3. FCF Conversion and Working Capital Normalization (SECONDARY)

FCF/Net Income of 25% is inadequate for a "quality growth" company. Working capital absorption from inventory build-up is the cause. Management claims SAP implementation and procurement optimization will improve this. The H1 2025 improvement (EUR 6.2M OCF vs. negative H1 2024) suggests progress, but must continue.

## THESIS (IF WE WERE BUYING)

We are not initiating a position, but if we were, this would be the thesis:

1. **Market leader in defensible niche:** Dominant position in DIY/prosumer smart home segment with genuine product differentiation (local control, open API, Home Assistant integration) that competitors have not replicated
2. **Exceptional management alignment:** 59% founder ownership with demonstrated capital allocation discipline, minimal dilution, no empire-building M&A
3. **Operating leverage inflection:** Q3 2025 EBIT margin of 29.5% suggests scale benefits; normalized margins of 25-27% are sustainable
4. **EU regulatory moat:** Cyber Resilience Act raises barriers for Chinese competitors; Shelly's EU headquarters and compliance capabilities are underappreciated assets
5. **Energy management positioning:** European energy transition creates structural demand for home energy monitoring/automation; Shelly is well-positioned

**Why we are NOT buying this thesis today:**

- Bullet 1 is being commoditized by Matter protocol
- Bullets 3-5 are already priced into 38x earnings
- No variant edge exists at current valuation

## THE SETUP

### Business Context

Shelly Group is a Bulgarian-headquartered IoT company designing WiFi/Bluetooth smart home devices (relays, dimmers, sensors, plugs). Key characteristics:

- **Fabless model:** 100% contract manufactured in China; Shelly designs and develops firmware in-house in Bulgaria
- **Distribution:** B2B channels (Amazon partners, distributors, professional installers) with 47% revenue in DACH region
- **Installed base:** 28.8 million devices across 5.2 million households
- **Positioning:** Mid-market (EUR 20/device) between cheap Chinese alternatives (EUR 10) and premium players (EUR 40-50)

### Structural Forces at Play

**Tailwinds:**
- Secular smart home adoption (15-25% household penetration, early majority phase)
- EU energy efficiency regulations driving monitoring/automation demand
- Professional installer channel expansion (higher margins, stickier relationships)
- European privacy consciousness favors local processing

**Headwinds:**
- Matter protocol standardization eliminates ecosystem lock-in
- Tuya platform enables rapid commodity competition
- Growth rate deceleration as base scales
- Amazon channel concentration creates platform risk

## FINAL ASSESSMENT

Shelly Group is exactly what it appears to be: a well-managed, high-quality European growth company in a decent industry position. The business is real, the margins are real, and the growth is real.

The stock, however, is fully priced. At 38x trailing P/E:
- You need 25-30%+ growth for 3-5 years (growth is already decelerating)
- You need EBIT margins to expand to 27-30% (Q3 was 29.5%, but sustainability is questionable)
- You need Matter protocol not to commoditize the business (it probably will)
- You need SaaS transition to work (0.1% attach rate says it isn't)

**The positive attributes are consensus. The risks are underappreciated. There is no variant edge.**

**We admire the business but do not own the stock.** Add to watchlist. Revisit at EUR 38-42 or upon evidence of SaaS traction.
