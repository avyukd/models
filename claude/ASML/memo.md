**1. China Revenue Replacement Rate**
2024 China revenue (~EUR 10B, 36% of sales) was artificially inflated by stockpiling ahead of export controls. This drops to ~20% in 2025 guidance. Q4 2024 bookings (EUR 7.1B) suggest partial replacement, but sustainability is unproven.

**2. AI Capex Cycle Duration**
ASML's demand is now concentrated in a single use case: AI infrastructure buildout. TSMC alone represents 30-35% of revenue and is growing capex 37% while Samsung/Intel are cutting. If hyperscaler AI capex stalls, there is no second wave of demand behind it. Traditional end markets (PC, smartphone, auto) are flat-to-declining.

**3. Cycle Position**
The semiconductor equipment industry is at late-cycle/peak. ASML gross margins (52-53%) and operating margins (34-35%) are at record highs. Memory share of bookings hit 56% in Q4 2025 - historically, memory surges precede downturns. Backlog (EUR 38.8B) provides visibility but has historically peaked before cycle turns.

## The Setup

ASML occupies a unique position in global industry: a true monopoly protected by 30 years of accumulated knowledge, 33,000+ patents, exclusive supplier relationships (Zeiss optics, Cymer light sources), and switching costs that exceed the value of competition. No competitor has shipped a single EUV system. China's SMEE is a decade behind at best.

The structural story is bulletproof. The cyclical story is concerning.

2024 financials were distorted by China stockpiling ahead of export controls. China surged to 36% of revenue (49% in Q2 2024), entirely DUV systems. This was one-time demand that will not repeat. The "growth" in 2024 was actually negative ex-China pull-forward.

The AI-driven demand replacing China is real but concentrated. TSMC is increasing capex 37% while Samsung and Intel are cutting. Memory customers are aggressively building HBM capacity. This creates a narrow demand funnel: if TSMC disappoints or HBM pricing compresses, there is no offsetting demand.
