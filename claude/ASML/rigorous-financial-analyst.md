# ASML Holding N.V. (NASDAQ: ASML) - Rigorous Financial Analysis

## Key Finding

ASML's 2024 financials obscure a structural earnings cliff: China accounted for ~36% of revenue (entirely DUV) due to pre-restriction stockpiling. Guidance implies China revenue drops to ~20% in 2025, a ~48% YoY decline in that geography. The market is pricing in successful backfill from non-China customers, but the installed base and EUV ramp may not fully offset China headwinds until 2026-2027.

**Source**: ASML Q4 2024 earnings release, MacroTrends, Yahoo Finance

### Key Observations

1. **Revenue stagnation in 2024**: Only +2.5% growth despite record Q4 (EUR 9.3bn). EUV system units fell from 53 (2023) to 42 (2024). DUV units also declined 396 to 374.

2. **Margin stability**: Gross margin pinned at ~51% despite product mix shifts. Operating margin declined slightly due to R&D investment ramp for High-NA EUV.

3. **Net income decline**: Down EUR 200M YoY despite revenue increase, reflecting higher R&D and SG&A.

4. **Geographic mix distortion**: China surged to ~36% of revenue (up from 29% in 2023), entirely DUV. This is non-recurring pre-restriction demand.

### Cash Flow Volatility Explanation

- **2023 FCF decline**: Working capital consumed cash as ASML built inventory for China orders and High-NA ramp
- **2024 FCF surge**: EUR 5.7bn YoY improvement driven by customer down payments (contract liabilities) and favorable timing of supplier payments
- **Caution**: 2024 FCF includes pull-forward of China-related down payments that will not repeat

- Market implies sustained 15-20% earnings growth through 2027
- Requires successful China revenue replacement + continued AI-driven demand
- Implies gross margins hold at 51-53% (management guidance)

### What Must Be True

1. Non-China demand (TSMC, Samsung, Intel, memory) absorbs ~48% China revenue decline
2. High-NA EUV ramp contributes meaningfully to ASP/margin in 2026+
3. No further export control escalation impacting installed base service revenue
4. Semiconductor capex cycle does not turn down through 2027

### Downside Scenario

- If China service revenue is also restricted (currently excluded from controls)
- If memory investment weakens post-HBM buildout
- If Intel continues losing share and cuts capex further
- Normalized P/E of 25-28x on EUR 7bn earnings = EUR 175-200bn market cap = 25-30% downside

## Key Financial Drivers

**1. China Revenue Replacement Rate**

The single most important variable. 2024 China revenue of EUR ~10bn must be replaced by TSMC, Samsung, Intel, and memory makers. Q4 2024 bookings surge (EUR 7.1bn) suggests some replacement underway, but sustainability is unproven.

**2. EUV/High-NA Mix & Pricing**

High-NA systems priced at ~EUR 350M vs EUR 150-200M for standard EUV. Only 2 High-NA systems recognized in 2024. Ramp to meaningful volume (10+ systems/year) could drive margin expansion.

**3. Installed Base Management Growth**

IBM revenue (EUR 6.5bn in 2024, +16% YoY) is recurring and higher margin. Guidance for >20% growth in 2025. This is the earnings stability anchor.

---

## What Consensus May Be Missing

### The IBM Underappreciation

Most analysis focuses on system sales, but IBM (service) is:
- ~23% of revenue
- Higher margin than blended (estimated 55%+ gross margin)
- Growing at 15-20% annually
- Less vulnerable to export controls (servicing existing installed base)

**Insight**: If export controls eventually target service (they currently do not), this would be a material earnings hit. Conversely, if service grows as guided, it provides significant earnings stability as system sales fluctuate.

### China Stockpiling Was Rational But Unrepeatable

China's 49% share in Q2 2024 was peak stockpiling. The 2024 full-year 36% share is still artificially elevated. **The 2023-2024 comparison is misleading**; true underlying growth ex-China pull-forward was likely negative.

### Backlog Quality Deserves Scrutiny

Backlog declined from EUR 40bn (end 2022) to EUR 36bn (end 2024) before recovering to EUR 38.8bn (end 2025). The EUR 1.4bn China cancellation in 2025 suggests backlog includes some optionality for customers. True "locked-in" backlog may be lower than headline suggests.

---

## Conclusion

ASML's FY2024 results were distorted by China pre-restriction demand. Normalized earnings power is EUR 6.5-8.5bn depending on assumptions. At EUR 280bn market cap, the stock trades at 33-43x normalized earnings, a premium justified only if:

1. AI-driven demand sustains through 2027+
2. China replacement occurs smoothly
3. High-NA ramp accelerates margins

The balance sheet is pristine, cash generation is strong, and the monopoly position is secure. However, the current multiple prices in near-perfect execution. The key risk is not competition (there is none) but demand cyclicality and geopolitical overhang.

**Assessment**: Financially strong, but fully priced assuming favorable cycle. Limited margin of safety at current levels.
