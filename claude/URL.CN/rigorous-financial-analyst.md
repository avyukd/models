# NameSilo Technologies Corp. (TSX: URL.CN / OTC: URLOF)
## Rigorous Financial Analysis

**Date:** January 2026
**Currency:** All figures in Canadian dollars unless otherwise noted

---

## Executive Summary

NameSilo Technologies is a small-cap Canadian domain registrar generating approximately CAD $65-67M in annualized revenue (based on recent quarters), with a market cap of approximately CAD $142M. The company has demonstrated consistent revenue growth (12 consecutive quarters), improving gross margins (from ~18% to ~26%), and recently achieved positive GAAP net income after years of losses. The business generates real operating cash flow with minimal capex, making it a legitimate free cash flow generator. However, the company's investment portfolio of convertible loans to early-stage ventures and recent diversifying acquisitions (SewerVUE) introduce complexity and risk that requires careful scrutiny.

**Bottom Line:** A fundamentally sound domain registrar business trading at a reasonable (not cheap) valuation, but obscured by a conglomerate-lite structure with opaque investee companies that complicates normalized earnings analysis.

## 1. Business Financial Profile

| Attribute | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| Capital intensity | Low - domain registrar model has minimal capex requirements |
| Cost structure | Mixed - registry fees (ICANN/wholesale) are largely variable; operating costs are more fixed |
| Operating leverage | Moderate positive - incremental domains/ancillary revenue flow through at higher margins |
| Cyclicality | Low - domains are essential business infrastructure, reasonably recession-resistant |
| Revenue quality | High - deferred revenue model with multi-year registrations provides visibility; ~$33M deferred revenue balance |
| Customer concentration | Low - ~5.8M domains across ~160 countries (retail/SMB focus) |

**Business Model:** NameSilo earns revenue through:
1. Domain registration fees (core business) - recognized ratably over registration period
2. Marketplace/aftermarket domain sales
3. Ancillary services (SSL, hosting, email, DNS)
4. **Investment portfolio** (convertible loans/equity in early-stage companies)

The deferred revenue model is a key strength - CAD $33M+ in deferred revenue represents ~50% of market cap and provides substantial revenue visibility.

The company reports "Adjusted EBITDA" ($2,884,190 for FY2024) which suggests there are add-backs - the fact that Adjusted EBITDA is *lower* than Operating Income ($4.08M) is unusual and may indicate that Adjusted EBITDA excludes certain gains or includes losses from investee companies. **This requires clarification.**

## 5. Capital Allocation Assessment

### Historical Allocation

1. **Debt Repayment (Completed):** Reduced debt from >$1.4M to zero over 2024-2025. Good discipline.

2. **Share Buybacks:**
   - NCIB authorized for up to 4.95% of shares
   - Repurchased 1,308,000 shares in 2024 (~1.5% of float)
   - At current prices (~$1.62), buybacks are mildly accretive if FCF is sustainable

3. **Investment Portfolio:**
   - Alchemy Labs: $1.25M facility, 12% interest, 4% equity stake, related party (common director)
   - Cheelcare: $150K+ facility, 12% interest, convertible to equity
   - Ola Media: $75K+ promissory note, equity investment

   **Concern:** These are early-stage companies with uncertain outcomes. The returns on these investments are not clearly disclosed. Related party status of Alchemy is a governance yellow flag.

4. **Acquisitions:**
   - SewerVUE (Sep 2025): CAD $2.45M for pipeline inspection business - completely unrelated to domain registrar operations
   - CommerceHQ (Sep 2025): Website builder - strategically adjacent to domain business

### Capital Allocation Verdict

**Mixed.** The core domain business generates cash and management has been prudent with debt. However, the investment portfolio in unrelated early-stage ventures and the SewerVUE acquisition suggest a "mini-conglomerate" strategy that introduces complexity, related-party risks, and potential value destruction if investments go poorly.

## Key Financial Drivers

**The 1-3 metrics that actually drive this stock:**

1. **Gross Margin Trajectory:** This is the single most important metric. NameSilo's margin expansion from 18% to 26% has been the primary driver of earnings growth. Each 100 bps of margin expansion on $65M revenue = $650K incremental gross profit. If margins can sustainably reach 28-30% through ancillary services, there is meaningful upside. If they compress back toward 20%, earnings will disappoint.

2. **Domains Under Management Growth:** Revenue is largely a function of DUM x average revenue per domain. Growth from 5.7M to 5.8M domains represents low single-digit unit growth. The market appears to assume mid-teens unit growth can continue - watch for deceleration.

3. **Investment Portfolio Outcomes:** The ~$6M invested in early-stage companies (Alchemy, Cheelcare, Ola Media) is currently a black box. A major write-down or, conversely, a successful exit could swing valuation materially.

---

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**Non-Obvious Insight:**

**The indirect tax liability ($5.2M) and investment portfolio (~$6M) roughly offset each other on the balance sheet, but with asymmetric risk profiles.**

The tax liability is a quasi-certain cash outflow that will eventually crystallize. The investment portfolio has optionality - it could be worth zero or significantly more, but the base case expectation for early-stage venture investments should be skeptical.

**Net effect:** Investors looking at "net cash" may be overstating balance sheet strength by ~$5M. True excess capital is closer to $4-5M, not $9M.

**Second insight:** The SewerVUE acquisition is a red flag for capital allocation discipline. Paying $2.45M for an unrelated infrastructure inspection business suggests management may be pivoting toward a "holding company" model rather than focusing on the core domain registrar. This introduces execution risk and potential conglomerate discount that is not reflected in current valuation.

---

## Summary Assessment

**NameSilo Technologies is a fundamentally sound domain registrar business demonstrating real operational improvement - revenue growth, margin expansion, and free cash flow generation. The company has achieved positive net income, eliminated debt, and trades at a reasonable (not cheap) valuation of ~25x FCF.**

**However, the investment thesis is complicated by:**
- Opacity around stock-based compensation
- Related-party investments in early-stage companies
- A $5.2M tax liability overhang
- Diversifying acquisition (SewerVUE) that questions capital allocation discipline

**Verdict:** Fundamentally solid, but priced for continued execution with limited margin of safety. The company is not broken, but it is not clearly undervalued either. Appropriate for investors who believe in the growth trajectory and are comfortable with the conglomerate-lite structure.
