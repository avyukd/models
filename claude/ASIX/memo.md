## 5. The Setup

AdvanSix is the sole remaining integrated caprolactam-to-nylon-to-ammonium-sulfate producer in the US (alongside BASF Freeport). The business operates from a single world-scale site at Hopewell, VA producing ~400kt of caprolactam annually, with ~4 pounds of ammonium sulfate generated per pound of caprolactam as an unavoidable stoichiometric byproduct.

The structural forces:
- **Global caprolactam is in massive oversupply.** China added ~4.6M tons of capacity since 2020 (13% CAGR), creating global utilization of ~74%. This broke the old industry equilibrium.
- **But the damage is being absorbed by the weakest players.** European producers with high energy costs are permanently exiting. The North American duopoly (AdvanSix + BASF) benefits from trade protection and logistics advantages that insulate against the worst of Chinese pricing pressure.
- **The ammonium sulfate story has quietly become dominant.** At trough, Plant Nutrients likely contributes >50% of economic profit. This is a structurally advantaged position -- near-zero marginal cost, constrained supply (62% of global ammonium sulfate comes from caprolactam co-production), and growing demand from soil sulfur deficiency.

The moat is weak on the nylon side and moderate on the ammonium sulfate side. This is not a quality compounder. It is a cyclical business at trough with improving structural positioning and a potential FCF inflection.

## 8. Risks and Kill Switches

**Thesis invalidators:**
- **Capex creep above $100M in 2026.** If management reverts to elevated spending, the FCF transformation doesn't happen. This is the primary kill switch. Monitor quarterly capex disclosures.
- **Chinese caprolactam exports to North America at scale.** If tariff protection weakens or Chinese producers dump product into the US market, nylon/caprolactam margins compress further. The trough could deepen rather than recover.
- **Ammonium sulfate price collapse.** If Chinese caprolactam expansion generates massive co-product ammonium sulfate that floods global markets, ASIX loses its earnings floor. Monitor ammonium sulfate FOB pricing -- below $200/ton is bearish.

**Permanent capital loss scenarios:**
- Extended trough + elevated capex + revolver draws = balance sheet deterioration. At 1.2x net leverage this is not imminent, but the $48M revolver tranche maturing October 2026 needs refinancing.
- Single-site risk at Hopewell. Extended operational disruption (fire, environmental, equipment failure) would hit the entire product chain with zero redundancy.

**Re-evaluation triggers:**
- CEO selling shares at current levels (would signal lack of confidence in the capex guidance she set).
- Management announcing a new major capital project that extends the elevated capex regime.
- Dividend cut -- would signal balance sheet stress that isn't visible in current numbers.
