# Nylon 6 / Caprolactam / Ammonium Sulfate Industry Analysis

## The Bottom Line Up Front

This industry is in a prolonged trough driven by massive Chinese capacity additions that have fundamentally altered global supply-demand dynamics. Global caprolactam capacity stands at ~9,500 KT against ~7,000 KT of demand -- roughly 74% utilization globally, with nylon 6 utilization at just 65%. Chinese capacity alone grew from 4.3M tons in 2020 to ~8.9M tons in 2025, a 13% CAGR. That is not a cycle -- it is a structural oversupply event that has broken the old industry equilibrium. European rationalization (BASF, Fibrant, DOMO, Spolana) is removing capacity, but it is a rounding error against Chinese additions.

The non-consensus view: **this trough is shallower than it should be for North American integrated producers, and consensus may be underestimating the asymmetry of the recovery path.**

---

## What Drives Profitability in This Industry

Three interlocking product markets determine economics:

1. **Caprolactam spread over benzene/cyclohexane feedstock** -- the core conversion margin.
2. **Nylon 6 resin conversion margin over caprolactam** -- driven by downstream demand (auto, textiles, packaging).
3. **Ammonium sulfate co-product credit** -- 2.0-2.2 tons of ammonium sulfate are produced per ton of caprolactam. This is not optional; it is a stoichiometric byproduct.

**Fact:** The caprolactam byproduct route accounts for 62% of global ammonium sulfate supply.

**Judgment:** For a North American integrated producer making its own ammonia and sulfuric acid, the margin structure is fundamentally different from a standalone caprolactam converter. The ammonium sulfate leg can carry the business through nylon troughs.

## Supply Elasticity and Capacity Dynamics

### Capacity Timeline

| Year | China Caprolactam Capacity | Global Capacity (est.) |
|------|---------------------------|----------------------|
| 2020 | 4.3M tons | ~7.5M tons |
| 2022 | ~5.5M tons | ~8.5M tons |
| 2024 | 6.95M tons | ~9.5M tons |
| 2025 | ~8.9M tons | ~11M+ tons |

**Fact:** China added more caprolactam capacity in the last 5 years (~4.6M tons) than the entire non-China world operates.

### Supply Response (finally happening):
- **BASF:** Cut European caprolactam by 100 KT/yr
- **Spolana (Czech Republic):** Exiting caprolactam and PVC by mid-2025
- **Fibrant (Netherlands):** Ceasing caprolactam production at Chemelot (announced Oct 2025)
- **DOMO (Germany):** Filed insolvency Dec 2025
- **China:** Producers implemented 20% phased production cuts in Q4 2025 as losses exceeded 600 RMB/ton

**Judgment:** European rationalization is structurally permanent -- those plants will not restart given energy cost disadvantage. This removes perhaps 300-500 KT from the Western market permanently. But Chinese capacity additions dwarf this.

## Current Cycle Position: Late Trough / Early Recovery. Confidence: Moderate.

Evidence for trough:
- European producers literally going bankrupt (DOMO) or shutting down (Fibrant, Spolana)
- Chinese producers cutting production 20% in Q4 2025 after losses exceeding 600 RMB/ton
- Global nylon 6 utilization at 65%

Evidence this is late-trough:
- European capacity destruction is accelerating and permanent
- Chinese producers have begun self-correcting (operating rate down to 71.8% in Dec 2025)
- AdvanSix EBITDA stabilized and slightly improved in 2025 vs. 2024

---

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**Consensus view:** "This is a normal commodity chemical trough and the cycle will turn as it always does."

**What they may be missing (bullish for North American integrated producers):**

1. **European supply destruction is permanent, not cyclical.** These plants will not restart.
2. **Tariff protection is higher than ever.** The sole domestic producer has structural protection that didn't exist in prior troughs.
3. **The ammonium sulfate co-product has become the margin anchor.** In 2025, plant nutrients revenue ($564M) exceeded nylon solutions ($310M) + caprolactam ($271M).
4. **The trough floor is higher than prior cycles.** $142-157M EBITDA through 2024-2025 vs. likely $100-120M in 2019-2020.

**Net assessment:** Consensus may be wrong about the magnitude of upside when the cycle turns, because they are applying old-cycle mental models to a market where the North American competitive position has structurally improved.

---

## Primary Industry Risk

**China never rationalizes.** Provincial governments continue subsidizing capacity additions. Chinese demand disappoints. Chinese producers export at marginal cost, compressing global pricing indefinitely.
