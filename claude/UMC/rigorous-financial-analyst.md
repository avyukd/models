# UMC (United Microelectronics Corporation) - Financial Analysis

**Date:** February 2026
**Ticker:** UMC (NYSE ADR) / 2303 (TWSE)
**Market Cap:** ~$26.5B
**Sector:** Semiconductor Foundry (Mature/Specialty Nodes)

---

## Executive Summary: What Actually Matters Here

**Verdict:** UMC is a cheap, slow-growth, capital-intensive foundry trading at 14-15x earnings with a 4-6% dividend yield. The stock is a margin-and-utilization story, not a growth story. AI tailwinds are real but indirect - UMC does not make AI training chips; it makes the surrounding silicon (edge AI, IoT, auto, connectivity). Depreciation headwinds from Singapore fab ramp are the key near-term drag. Valuation is undemanding vs. peers (TSMC at 33x, GFS unprofitable) but justified by structurally lower growth and margin profile.

**Key Insight:** The market is pricing UMC as an ex-growth, mature-node commodity foundry. This is mostly correct, but the 22nm ramp (now >13% of revenue, growing 31% QoQ in Q4 2025) and Intel partnership optionality are underappreciated. If utilization recovers to 80%+ and depreciation peaks in 2026-27, margins could return to low-30s, supporting meaningful EPS recovery.

| Adjustment | Impact | Rationale |
|------------|--------|-----------|
| Excess depreciation (above mid-cycle) | +NT$0.40-0.60 | Depreciation running ~20% above normal; peaks 2026-27 |

## Key Financial Drivers (What Actually Moves This Stock)

### 1. Utilization Rate (Primary Driver)
- Current: ~76-78%
- Sensitivity: Every 5 percentage points = ~1.5pts gross margin = ~$0.05/ADS EPS
- Utilization went from 70% (Q4 2024) to 78% (Q3 2025) - this is the main 2025 story
- Target for margin recovery: 80%+

### 2. Depreciation Cycle
- 2024 depreciation: ~NT$45B
- 2025 depreciation: ~NT$54B (+20%)
- 2026-27: Growth slows to single-digits, then potentially flat/declines
- This is the key margin headwind that should reverse in 2027+

### 3. 22nm Revenue Mix
- Currently 13%+ of revenue, growing 30%+ QoQ
- Higher margin than legacy nodes
- Management targeting 22/28nm at 45% of revenue by 2026
- This is the differentiation story vs. pure commodity foundry

## What Consensus May Be Missing

### The Non-Consensus Insight

**Depreciation timing creates an earnings trough that masks underlying improvement.**

The Street is focused on headline EPS declining from NT$3.80 (2024) to NT$3.34 (2025). This looks like deterioration. But the underlying business is actually improving:
- Utilization: 70% -> 78%
- 22nm revenue: Growing 30%+
- Wafer shipments: Up 12% YoY in 2025

The earnings decline is almost entirely depreciation-driven (Singapore Fab P3, Taiwan P6 ramp). Depreciation guidance shows high-20% growth in 2025, then decelerating to single-digits in 2026-27.

**Implication:** 2026-27 should show meaningful EPS recovery even with modest revenue growth as:
1. Depreciation growth slows dramatically
2. Utilization stays at improved levels
3. 22nm mix shift continues

A path to NT$4.50+ EPS (vs NT$3.34 in 2025) by 2027 is plausible, which at current prices would be <11x P/E.

### Secondary Non-Consensus Element: Intel Partnership Optionality

The 12nm collaboration with Intel (production starting 2027) is not priced in. If it succeeds:
- UMC gains access to US manufacturing capacity without capex
- Provides geopolitical diversification
- Could add $500M-1B revenue annually at mature margins

This is not in consensus estimates but represents free optionality.

## AI Context: How Does UMC Fit the AI Trade?

**Direct AI exposure:** Minimal. UMC does not make GPUs, HBM, or leading-edge AI accelerators. That is TSMC's domain.

**Indirect AI exposure:** Meaningful.
- **Edge AI/IoT:** UMC's 22nm platform is ideal for edge inference chips, smart sensors, AIoT devices
- **Connectivity:** 5G/WiFi chips for AI data center networking
- **Automotive:** ADAS, sensor fusion chips use mature nodes
- **Power management:** Every AI server needs power ICs (mature node products)

**Bull case for AI tailwind:** AI infrastructure buildout increases demand across the semiconductor ecosystem. Even if UMC doesn't make the "AI chip," it makes the surrounding silicon. IDC forecasts semiconductor market +17.6% in 2025; UMC can participate in rising tide.

**Bear case:** AI capex flows disproportionately to leading-edge (TSMC, memory). Mature node foundries get table scraps.

**Assessment:** AI provides modest tailwind via edge/automotive/IoT, but UMC should not be bought as an "AI play." It is a cyclical recovery + margin normalization story with AI as supporting backdrop.

## Conclusion

**Investment Thesis:** UMC is a reasonably cheap, well-managed foundry at trough margins due to depreciation headwinds. At 14x normalized earnings with a 4-6% dividend yield, the downside is limited by balance sheet strength and valuation support. Upside comes from utilization recovery, depreciation peak, and 22nm mix shift - a plausible path to 20-30% EPS growth by 2027.

**Not a high-conviction buy** - growth profile is structurally limited and there's no scarcity value like TSMC. But for yield-oriented investors willing to wait for the depreciation cycle to turn, the risk/reward is reasonable.

**What I would watch:**
1. Q1-Q2 2026 utilization rates (needs to stay >75%)
2. 22nm revenue trajectory (should continue >20% growth)
3. Intel partnership milestones (2027 production start)
4. Any guidance on depreciation peak timing
