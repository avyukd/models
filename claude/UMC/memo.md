## Key Drivers (What Actually Matters)

1. **Depreciation Peak Timing (2026-27):** Depreciation is running 20%+ above normalized levels due to Singapore Fab P3 ramp. This is the primary margin headwind masking underlying operational improvement. Depreciation peaks 2026-27, then flattens or declines. Earnings power re-emerges without revenue growth.

2. **Utilization Recovery:** Already in motion. Utilization went from 70% (Q4 2024) to 78% (Q3 2025). Every 5pts = ~1.5pts gross margin = ~$0.05/ADS EPS. Staying above 75% is sufficient; 80%+ unlocks meaningful margin expansion.

3. **22nm Revenue Mix Shift:** Growing 30%+ QoQ, now 13%+ of revenue, targeting 45% by 2026. Higher margin than legacy nodes. This is the differentiation story against commodity foundry narrative.

---

## Thesis

- UMC trades at 14x on trough earnings depressed by a depreciation cycle, not operational deterioration. Underlying metrics (utilization, wafer shipments, 22nm mix) are improving.
- The market prices UMC as a permanent ex-growth commodity foundry. We see a 2027 path to NT$4.50+ EPS (vs. NT$3.34 in 2025) as depreciation peaks and margins normalize to low-30s. That is <11x on current price.
- Intel 12nm partnership (production 2027) provides unpriced optionality for US capacity without capital outlay. If it works, adds $500M-1B revenue at mature margins.
- 4-6% dividend yield provides downside support while waiting for cycle to turn.
- Management has demonstrated capital discipline since 2017-18 exit from leading-edge race. They will not chase growth at the expense of returns.

## The Setup

UMC is the #4 pure-play foundry (4-5% global share) focused on mature nodes (22nm-90nm). The 2017-18 decision to exit leading-edge was the pivotal event - transformed from a capital-destruction machine chasing TSMC into a disciplined, dividend-paying operator earning 20%+ operating margins.

Current positioning:
- 22/28nm portfolio = 34-36% of revenue, growing
- Specialty processes (BCD, RFSOI, embedded memory) = >50% of revenue
- Singapore expansion provides geographic diversification
- Intel partnership provides US capacity optionality without heavy capex

The stock trades at a persistent discount to peers because (1) no leading-edge AI exposure, (2) Taiwan concentration, and (3) Chinese capacity threat. These are valid concerns but may be over-discounted at 14x trough earnings.

## Risks & Kill Switches

**What invalidates thesis:**
1. Utilization falls back below 70% due to demand weakness or Chinese pricing war
2. Depreciation does not peak as expected (new capacity announcements)
3. Intel partnership fails to reach production scale
4. Taiwan Strait conflict (existential - Singapore/Japan provide residual value only)

**What forces re-evaluation:**
- Gross margin falls below 25% for two consecutive quarters
- 22nm revenue growth decelerates below 10%
- Management reverses discipline (re-enters leading-edge with heavy capex)
- Chinese foundry pricing becomes structurally predatory (sustained 40%+ discounts)

**Tail risk acknowledgment:**
Taiwan conflict is binary and unhedgeable. We assign <10% probability over investment horizon. If your probability is materially higher, this goes in the too-hard pile. We are not betting on Taiwan invasion not happening - we are betting the current discount is excessive given diversification progress and cycle position.

## Positioning & Portfolio Logic

**Why now:**
- Earnings at trough due to depreciation timing, not operational deterioration
- Utilization has already recovered from 70% to 78%
- Optionality on Intel partnership and policy catalysts not priced
- Downside supported by 4-6% yield and net cash balance sheet

**Size:** 1-2% position. This is tactical, not core. The structural challenges in mature-node foundry are real. We are playing the depreciation cycle and optionality, not buying a 10-year compounder.

**What to pair/hedge:** Long UMC as part of a semi basket could pair with short exposure to weaker mature-node names (if available) or simply size small given idiosyncratic risks.

**AI context per user request:** UMC is not an AI play in the direct sense (no GPUs, no HBM). It benefits modestly from AI infrastructure buildout (power management ICs, connectivity chips for data centers). The "AI tailwind" narrative provides a favorable backdrop for semiconductor multiples broadly, but UMC's value case rests on its own depreciation cycle and margin normalization, not on AI demand specifically. We are giving credit for the AI narrative in accepting a 14x multiple on trough earnings, which would historically be full value for this asset class.

**Post-entry watchlist:**
- Quarterly utilization rates
- 22nm revenue contribution
- Depreciation guidance
- Intel partnership milestones
- Chinese foundry pricing behavior
- USTR tariff developments

## Classification

This is a **Medium-conviction tactical buy** driven by:
1. Earnings trough from depreciation cycle (temporary headwind)
2. Operational improvement already underway (utilization, mix)
3. Unpriced optionality (Intel, tariffs)
4. Downside protected by yield and balance sheet

It is not a high-conviction core position because:
1. Structural Chinese capacity threat is real
2. Taiwan geopolitical risk cannot be hedged
3. This is a 14x earnings foundry with low-single-digit growth, not a franchise
4. Variant perception rests on depreciation timing, which is knowable but not exciting

**Bottom line:** UMC is a bet that the market is conflating cyclical margin compression with structural impairment. The business is improving while earnings are declining due to depreciation timing. At 14x trough earnings with a 4-6% yield and optionality on Intel/tariffs, the risk/reward is attractive for a tactical position. Pass if Taiwan tail risk dominates your framework.
