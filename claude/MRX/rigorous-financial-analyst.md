| Normalized EPS (75M diluted shares) | ~$3.20 |

At $43/share, the stock trades at ~13.4x normalized 2024 earnings. Consensus 2025E EPS is $3.57, putting the forward multiple at ~12x. For a commodities broker/dealer growing revenue 25%+ and with 20% adjusted pretax margins, this is optically cheap.

**But 2024 was not a normal year.** Market Making revenue surged 79% to $145M driven by exceptional metals volatility (Russian metals LME guidance disruption). Net interest income nearly doubled to $227M from $122M as rates and client balances expanded simultaneously. Both factors are cyclically elevated.

**Mid-cycle normalization (judgment):** Market Making should be treated at roughly 2023 levels (~$81M). Net interest income at mid-cycle rates and normalized balances is probably $150-170M vs $227M reported. This implies mid-cycle net revenue closer to $1.3-1.4B vs $1.59B reported, and normalized pretax profit in the $230-260M range rather than $321M. After-tax, SBC-adjusted: ~$150-175M, or $2.00-2.30/share. At $43, that is 19-22x mid-cycle earnings -- more expensive than it appears.

## Business Financial Profile

Capital-light intermediary (capex $12M on $1.6B revenue). Four segments: Clearing, Agency & Execution, Market Making, Hedging & Investment Solutions. Revenue mix is diversified but Market Making and net interest income swing results materially. Fixed cost base creates operating leverage in both directions. Client money balances ($15.5B average in Q4 2024) generate float income that is rate-sensitive.

## Cash Flow

Operating cash flow of $1.16B in 2024 looks enormous but is misleading for a broker/dealer. Most of this reflects changes in client balances and trading positions, not recurring cash generation. Capex is negligible ($12M). The business genuinely requires minimal reinvestment. True owner earnings are closer to net income adjusted for SBC, roughly $190-240M depending on normalization.

## Key Financial Drivers

1. **Net interest income on client balances (~$227M, 14% of net revenue):** This is rate-sensitive float income. Every 100bps in rate cuts compresses this materially. With $15B+ in client money, interest rate trajectory is the single most important variable for near-term earnings.

2. **Market Making volatility dependence:** 79% revenue growth in 2024 was driven by exceptional metals dislocations. This segment will mean-revert. The question is whether structural market share gains in energy and metals provide a higher floor.

3. **Organic growth in Clearing volumes:** Contracts cleared up 30% to 1.1B. This is the most durable revenue stream and the core franchise value. Growth here compounds.

---

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**The interest rate sensitivity is underappreciated.** NII of $227M in 2024 (up from $122M in 2023) was driven by both higher balances (Cowen acquisition) and peak rates. Street estimates of $3.57 EPS for 2025 likely assume NII stays elevated. If rates normalize 150-200bps lower and client balances stabilize, NII could compress by $50-80M pretax, wiping $0.50-0.75 off EPS. Combined with Market Making normalization, mid-cycle earnings power is meaningfully below where the Street is modeling.

The NINGI Research short report alleging fictitious intercompany transactions is a separate and serious risk. For a 25x levered balance sheet, accounting credibility is existential. The lawsuit remains unresolved as of February 2026. Until the 20-F is filed and audited without qualification, this overhang persists.

**Bottom line:** Optically cheap at 12x forward earnings, but mid-cycle earnings power is likely $2.00-2.30/share (19-22x), and there is an unresolved accounting integrity question on a highly levered balance sheet. The stock is priced for continued peak-cycle performance with no discount for litigation risk.
