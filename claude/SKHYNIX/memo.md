## KEY DRIVERS (The 1-3 Things That Matter)

1. **HBM Margin Sustainability** - Current 49% operating margins are 2x mid-cycle. HBM commands 50-60% margins vs. 20-30% commodity DRAM. When Samsung qualifies HBM4 or Nvidia diversifies suppliers, these margins compress violently.

2. **Cycle Position** - We are definitively at peak. 95%+ utilization, 55-60% QoQ price increases, 26-39 week lead times. Every historical memory cycle with these characteristics has reversed within 12-18 months.

3. **NVIDIA Concentration** - 27% of revenue, 70% of HBM4 orders. This is the moat AND the fragility. Single-customer dependency at this scale creates binary risk if Nvidia diversifies.

## THESIS IN 5 BULLETS

- SK Hynix is the clear HBM leader (62% share) with real technology advantage (MR-MUF process, 20% yield premium) and customer lock-in (Nvidia sold out through 2026).

- The business is printing money - KRW 47T operating profit in 2025, balance sheet transformed from net debt 20T to net cash position.

- **However**: Current valuation (KRW 830,000, 13x trailing peak earnings) requires margins to stay elevated indefinitely. Normalized mid-cycle EPS is KRW 15,000-22,000, implying 60-80% downside if margins mean-revert.

- We are late cycle. Every indicator is flashing peak: margins 2x historical, utilization maxed, pricing parabolic, "supercycle" narrative. This has ended badly every time in memory history.

- The setup is "great company, terrible entry point." There is no margin of safety buying a commodity cyclical at 49% operating margins.

## THE SETUP

**Why This Matters Now**: Memory is at textbook peak conditions. Every cycle metric is extreme:
- Operating margins 44-59% vs. mid-cycle 20-25%
- Utilization 95%+ vs. normal 80-85%
- Price growth +55-60% QoQ vs. normal flat-to-5%
- Lead times 26-39 weeks vs. normal 8-12 weeks

The 2022-2023 trough (negative margins, inventory crisis) was 18 months ago. Typical memory cycles run 2-3 years trough-to-trough. We are in the back half.

**HBM Capacity Arbitrage**: The current margin structure is not sustainable oligopoly economics - it is a temporary arbitrage. HBM wafers consume 3-4x capacity vs. conventional DRAM. This starves conventional supply regardless of end-demand, creating artificial shortage. When HBM demand growth slows (inevitable), capacity floods back. The flip from shortage to glut will be faster than 2018-2019.

### Valuation Sensitivity

At current KRW 830,000:
- If mid-cycle earnings (KRW 21,700) valued at 12x: Fair value = **KRW 260,000** (-69%)
- If margins stay at 40% through 2027: Fair value at 15x = **KRW 850,000** (flat)
- If trough cycle (2027-28): Stock could trade to **KRW 150,000-200,000**

**The math**: To justify current price, you need to believe either (a) margins stay at 40%+ permanently, or (b) revenue grows enough to offset margin compression. History suggests neither.

## RISKS & KILL SWITCHES

### What Invalidates the Neutral Thesis (Could Become a Buy)
- Stock falls 40%+ to KRW 500,000 or below, creating margin of safety at mid-cycle valuation
- HBM demand exceeds supply through 2028 with pricing power sustained
- Samsung HBM4 fails qualification, extending SK Hynix dominance

### What Invalidates a Bull Thesis (Why We Pass)
- **Margin normalization** - 49% operating margins are not sustainable. History shows 20-25% mid-cycle.
- **Capacity response** - All three producers adding HBM capacity aggressively. Supply discipline breaks.
- **NVIDIA diversification** - Single customer controls HBM pricing power. Any shift destroys premium.
- **Technology catch-up** - Samsung hybrid bonding progress. MR-MUF advantage is process, not physics.

### Tail Risks
- **China fab forced divestiture** - 40% DRAM capacity stranded. $15-20B write-off. Probability ~10%. Material but not existential (HBM is 100% Korea).
- **Taiwan conflict** - Unhedgeable. Affects all semis.
- **Korean peninsula** - Existential but priced as zero probability for 70 years.

## FINAL ASSESSMENT

SK Hynix is the best-positioned player in the most attractive segment (HBM) of a structurally improved industry (memory oligopoly). The business quality is high. The balance sheet is fortress. The technology lead is real.

**None of this justifies buying at peak.**

Current price embeds permanent HBM margin premium and continued "supercycle" conditions. History shows memory margins always mean-revert. The 49% operating margin is 2x sustainable. Normalized valuation is 60-80% below current price.

This is not a short - the business is too good and the timing is uncertain. But it is definitively not a buy. The correct answer is to wait for the cycle to turn, buy at trough when margins are compressed and narrative is bearish.

**Decision: NEUTRAL (Too Expensive at Cycle Peak)**

Pass. Revisit at KRW 450,000-500,000 or when margins compress to 25%.
