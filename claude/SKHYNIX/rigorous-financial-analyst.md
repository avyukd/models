# SK Hynix (000660.KS) - Rigorous Financial Analysis

## Key Financial Drivers (What Actually Moves This Stock)

**1. HBM Revenue Mix & Pricing Power** - HBM now represents 40%+ of DRAM revenue and commands 2-3x gross margins vs commodity DRAM. This single product line is the primary driver of margin expansion from ~24% operating margin (2022 cycle peak) to 49% (2025).

**2. Memory Cycle Position** - SK Hynix's earnings are hyper-cyclical. The company swung from -KRW 7.7T operating loss (2023) to +KRW 47.2T operating profit (2025) in 24 months. Where we are in the cycle determines 80%+ of stock performance.

**3. CapEx Discipline vs Demand** - Memory is a capacity-constrained business. HBM cannibalization of wafer capacity (HBM uses 2-3x wafer area vs standard DRAM) creates structural tightness. Company guides mid-30% capex/revenue ratio through 2026.

---

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**The HBM margin cliff risk is underappreciated.** Current 49% operating margins (2025) are not sustainable through-cycle. Historical mid-cycle margins for SK Hynix are 20-25%. The market is pricing in HBM dominance continuing indefinitely, but:
- Samsung is aggressively ramping HBM4 (2026 launch)
- Micron has gained share (from 7% to 21% of HBM market)
- HBM pricing will normalize as supply catches up

Second, **the Solidigm (ex-Intel NAND) asset is a hidden geopolitical risk.** The Dalian fab (China) creates concentration risk under US export control escalation scenarios. SK Hynix management has discussed potential divestiture but faces the paradox: strategic synergy value vs geopolitical exposure.

### What the Market Implies

At 13x trailing P/E on peak-cycle earnings (49% op margin), the market implies:
- Either: Margins stay elevated (HBM dominance permanent)
- Or: Revenue grows enough to offset margin normalization

**Bull Case Validation:**
- 2026E Revenue: KRW 120-130T (consensus ~27% growth)
- If margins hold at 40%: EPS ~70,000+ KRW
- 13x multiple = ~910,000 KRW (9% upside)

## Summary: Financial Quality Assessment

**Strengths:**
- Balance sheet: Net debt ratio 12%, fortress condition
- Cash generation: KRW 37T operating cash flow (2024)
- Margin expansion: Real, driven by product mix (HBM)
- Minimal dilution: Shares outstanding flat, treasury cancellation ongoing
- Capital allocation: Heavy reinvestment in HBM4 capacity is correct priority

**Weaknesses:**
- Extreme cyclicality: 49% op margin is peak, not normal
- Capex intensity: 30%+ of revenue is non-discretionary
- Geopolitical exposure: Solidigm/Dalian fab creates China risk
- Customer concentration: Heavy NVIDIA dependency for HBM

**Financial Verdict:** Financially strong but priced for perfection at cycle peak. Current valuation assumes HBM margins persist. Normalized mid-cycle earnings power is 70-80% below current run rate. Not a financial red flag situation - this is a quality cyclical at an expensive point in the cycle.
