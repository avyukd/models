# EarthLabs Inc. (TSXV: SPOT.V) - Financial Analysis

## Executive Summary

EarthLabs is a holding company masquerading as a mining media business. The core operating business (advertising and subscriptions) loses money. All reported profitability comes from unrealized/realized gains on an investment portfolio of junior mining stocks. The stock is essentially a closed-end fund with a small media business attached, but priced without the typical NAV discount that closed-end funds carry.

---

## The Non-Obvious Insight (What Consensus May Be Missing)

**EarthLabs is not a media company - it is a leveraged bet on junior mining stocks with a negative carry.**

The operating business loses approximately $8M/year on ~$7.5M in revenue (operating margin of roughly -87%). The $25.7M in "net income" for 9M 2025 is almost entirely mark-to-market gains on a portfolio of volatile junior mining stocks ($31.7M in investment gains offset by operating losses).

Think of it this way: If you invested $70M in junior mining stocks directly, your only cost would be minimal commissions. By investing through EarthLabs, you effectively pay ~$8M/year in operating losses - an **implicit management fee of ~11% of assets annually**.

---

## Key Financial Drivers (The 1-3 Things That Matter)

1. **Investment Portfolio Performance (90% of what matters)** - The stock trades on NAV, which is ~$0.46/share based on $64M equity and 139M shares. Current price of ~$0.28-0.30 reflects a 35-40% discount, appropriate given operating losses and illiquid holdings.

2. **Operating Loss Trajectory** - At ~$8M/year in losses, the company destroys ~$0.06/share annually. No path to profitability is evident.

3. **Junior Mining Sentiment** - Rising advertising revenue correlates with mining sector bullishness, which drives investment gains.

---

## Income Statement Analysis

**Revenue:** FY2024 revenue of $7.54M (+126% YoY) was almost entirely driven by the December 2023 acquisition of The Northern Miner Group for ~$4M. Organic growth was modest.

**Operating Loss:** ~$(6.5M) on ~$7.5M revenue = operating margin of approximately -87%

**Net Income Composition (9M 2025):**
- Operating loss: ~$(6.0M)
- Investment gains: $31.7M
- Net income: $25.7M

**Conclusion:** No normalized earnings power exists. Profitability is entirely investment-driven.

---

## Red Flags

- Persistent negative free cash flow from operations
- "Profits" are mark-to-market investment gains (weak cash conversion)
- No path to operating profitability demonstrated
- Eric Sprott owns 17%+ - alignment but also potential conflicts with advertiser holdings

---

## Valuation Scenario Analysis

At current ~$8M/year operating losses:
- Year 3 NAV: $64M - $24M = $40M ($0.29/share)
- Year 5 NAV: $64M - $40M = $24M ($0.17/share)

**Unless operating losses stop or investment gains exceed them, the stock is a wasting asset.**

---

## Conclusion

**Financial Assessment: Structurally Unprofitable Operating Business with Speculative Investment Overlay**

The core business has no normalized earnings power. The 35-40% discount to NAV appropriately reflects ongoing value destruction. Investors who want junior mining exposure are better served buying the underlying stocks directly or through low-cost ETFs.
