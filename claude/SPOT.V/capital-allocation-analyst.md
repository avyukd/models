# EarthLabs Inc. (TSXV: SPOT) - Management & Capital Allocation Analysis

## Management Overview

**Key Executives:**
- **Denis Laviolette** - Founder, Executive Chairman & CEO (since March 2023). Co-founded New Found Gold Corp. Direct ownership: 2.23% (~3.1M shares)
- **Vincent Dube-Bourgeois** - Co-Founder & President
- **Binh Quach** - CFO

**Key Shareholders:**
- **Eric Sprott**: 23.8M shares, ~17.3% ownership
- **Founder Denis Laviolette**: Only 2.23% direct ownership - notably low

**Company Background:** Founded 2016, IPO Feb 2019 at C$0.38/share. Renamed from GoldSpot Discoveries to EarthLabs Sept 2022. Current shares: ~139M.

---

## Incentive Structure & Alignment

**CEO Compensation:** C$225,000 (100% salary, 0% bonus). Heavy reliance on options.

**Option Grants Pattern:**
- September 2023: 13.4M options granted total (8.55M to directors/officers)
- Share count grew from ~50M at IPO to 139M (~180% dilution)

**What This Encourages:**
- Management has limited downside exposure
- CEO owns only 2.23% - insufficient skin in the game
- No ROIC/ROE performance hurdles disclosed
- Options reward any price appreciation regardless of value creation

**Alignment Assessment: MIXED**

---

## Stock-Based Compensation Discipline

**Dilution Since IPO:** ~180% share count growth (2.8x)

**Sources of Dilution:**
- 2021 bought deal: C$11.5M at C$0.80/share
- 2021 Sprott placement: C$9.15M at C$0.975/share
- CEO.CA acquisition: C$17M (partially in shares)
- Ongoing option grants

**Buybacks:** NCIB exists but no meaningful activity. Company trades below cash value but won't repurchase shares.

**SBC Discipline Assessment: LOW**

---

## The One Thing That Determines Value Creation

**The investment portfolio (~C$39M), not the operating business, will determine shareholder outcomes.**

EarthLabs is effectively a mining-focused holding company with media assets attached. A 20% portfolio swing (C$8M) matters more than any realistic media EBITDA improvement.

---

## What Consensus May Be Missing

**Variant Perception:** This is a C$39M investment portfolio with a media business as optionality, not a "mining media company."

The market assigns negative value to media assets (acquired for C$24M total). Stock is cheap on assets, but management won't return capital.

Shareholders depend on:
- Management returning capital (unlikely)
- Portfolio appreciation (unpredictable)
- Takeout (no evidence)

**This makes me LESS trusting.** Cash-rich balance sheet + below-cash trading + continued option grants = misalignment.
